# Side delt recommendations

<https://www.youtube.com/shorts/o_BiJtjmyoE>

If you're trying to grow massive side delts, what should you use? Dumbbells or cables? This recent study comparing dumbbell and cable lateral raises helped to shine some light on that. 24 trained lifters comprised of 16 men and eight women performed lateral raises as shown. So, which variation ended up reigning supreme as the side delt king? Well, they actually found that both exercises equally grew the side delts with no significant difference in growth between them. Now, here are my recommendations when it comes to your side delt training. What this study tells us is that both options are good variations for your side delts. Part of the limitations in the study, I would be interested in seeing this done over a longer time frame with untrained subjects so we could see a greater difference in terms of the growth between the two variations. My recommendation to cover your bases if you're training your side delts twice a week would be to just include one variation on each of those days. Or just pick the variation you're able to stick to most consistently and enjoy the most. However, if you're someone that moves between different gyms a lot, then I would recommend sticking with a dumbbell lateral raise variation as it's much easier to track your progressive overload compared to using cables or different machines.
