# DON'T Use a Foam Roller...

<https://www.youtube.com/shorts/6x9VVjpr7-I>

using a foam roller to do dumbbell presses is not going to be a good idea using a foam roller and putting it here is just going to decrease stability in the motion when you're on a bench the Bas of support is this wide when you're using a foam roller the Bas of support shrinks to nearly half the size and in addition the foam roll is going to be rotating all around the bench even if you are able to pull your arms slightly farther back behind your body decreasing the amount of support that you have behind you will likely not make the extra range of motion worth the trade-off in addition having a much narrower base of support may be uncomfortable for some of you to actually move your shoulder blades back into so whenever you're pressing just use the bench
