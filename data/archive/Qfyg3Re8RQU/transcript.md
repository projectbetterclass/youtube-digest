# Deadlift Posture Tip

<https://www.youtube.com/shorts/Qfyg3Re8RQU>

Most people avoid deadlifts because they're scared of getting injured, but it's by far probably one of the best ways to get stronger faster. Here's how to deadlift safely from someone who lives relatively heavy weights. So, people think you shouldn't round your back, and that's only half true. Watch any professional deadlifter, and they actually round their upper back, but they keep their lower back incredibly rigid like this. The danger isn't actually in your upper back rounding. It's in your lower back rounding because it places insane pressure on your spine. So, when setting up for a deadlift, make sure your lower back is rigid, but you can allow your upper back to round ever so slightly.
