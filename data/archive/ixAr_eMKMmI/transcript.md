# Squats don't train hamstrings!

[Music] here's why your hamstrings won't grow from squats or leg presses three of the four hamstrings do cross the back of the hip but they also cross the back of the knee which means they bend the knee and anytime you're doing a lower body press like a squat or leg press all of your quads need to activate to straighten your knee and when your hamstrings contract they bend the knee they don't straighten the knee so if you're doing a motion that trains the quads any significant contraction of the hamstrings at the back of the knee will directly oppose the motion so to train the hamstrings do leg curls and straight knee hinge motions don't do squats
