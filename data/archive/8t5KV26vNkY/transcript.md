# Solid glute workout

<https://www.youtube.com/shorts/8t5KV26vNkY>

One of my clients sent me this video asking if I thought this would be a good glute workout for his wife. So, let's look over it together. The first exercise is a barbell reverse lunge, which I think is an excellent glute exercise. If you want a more stable variation, I would recommend using the Smith machine. Her technique is great, but I do think the volume is a bit high, especially if you're doing four sets for each leg. If this were me, I would cut that in half. Same thing for the RDLs, I think two to three sets is going to be enough for most people, especially if you're training glutes twice a week. And please always use straps on your working sets of RDLs and stiff-leg deadlifts. You're never going to have a stronger grip than your glutes and hamstrings. This is a fine intensity technique if you just wanted to do one set of glute bridges, but I would put the calf bridges before the regular hip thrust. With the calf bridge, you're standing in the hardest range of motion for the bridge compared to a regular hip thrust, you can actually shoot out of the bottom a bit to help you get into that top position. And mini band kickbacks to finish off. Overall, a solid workout.
