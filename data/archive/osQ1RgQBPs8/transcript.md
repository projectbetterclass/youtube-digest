# Stop Loss Mistakes

<https://www.youtube.com/shorts/osQ1RgQBPs8>

I don't use stop losses so stop losses are actually really detrimental when you're trading options because oftentimes you'll get stopped out it'll sell your position for a really bad price and then later the stock or option will swing back to a different direction so you have a stop loss that ends up hurting you because it ends up fulfilling your order but it doesn't really do exactly what you wanted you want it to protect yourself but instead it ends up giving you more harm than good
