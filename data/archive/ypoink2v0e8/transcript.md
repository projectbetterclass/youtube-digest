# HOW TO Dumbell Lunge

<https://www.youtube.com/shorts/ypoink2v0e8>

The dumbbell [music] lunge is the best way to train the single leg under a stable position. Most people do this wrong. This is how you do it right. Most people do these two [music] things wrong. They either step too far forward, too close, or they don't manage their center of mass correctly. So, their shoulders are either too far behind, too far [music] in front. You want to find that balance. Here are the four steps to do it right. Step number one, take a step. Step two, find your center of mass. A good way to tell your center of mass is there. You should be able to feel [music] equal pressure between your front and back foot. Step number three, push the knee forward. [music] Lunge all the way down till your knee touches the ground. Step number four, simply push through the balls of the [music] foot and stand all the way back
