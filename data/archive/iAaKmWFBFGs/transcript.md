# If the weight doesn't make you nervous...

<https://www.youtube.com/shorts/iAaKmWFBFGs>

[music] So unless you're a straightup masochist who enjoys physical pain, >> or you're just lifting more casually and not trying to maximize growth 100%, then one likely sign that you aren't training hard enough is that you never feel a bit nervous in the gym, at least for certain lifts here and there, especially lower body. If you're just casually strolling up to a squat like you're picking out cereal at the grocery store. If you don't need a moment to pause and mentally prepare first and you don't feel relieved once it's over and glad you don't have to do that again for a while. Yeah, you need to reexamine things and be honest with yourself. I'm not saying hypertrophy training is some kind of hardcore life or death struggle. We're not storming the beaches of Normandy here, but if you never feel anxious at all during your workouts, you probably are holding back. It doesn't necessarily mean the weight is too light, but at the very least, you're not pushing close enough to actual failure. Like a lot of people, your idea of 0 to2 reps short of failure is probably more like 3 to four plus. And that's really all it takes to separate those who make outstanding gains from those who only ever see mediocre progress.
