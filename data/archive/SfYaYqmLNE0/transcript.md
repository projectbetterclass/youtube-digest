# Why do we butt wink?

<https://www.youtube.com/shorts/SfYaYqmLNE0>

what is buttwink and why does it happen when we squat buttwink is a combination of rounding of the lower spine and tucking of the hips underneath of the Torso butwink happens in a squat for two main reasons reason number one is we simply run out of range of motion at the hip and need to compensate through motion at the spine reason number two is we've lost control of our Center of mass and need to tuck our hips underneath of us in order to prevent ourselves from falling forward you visualize how butwink affects a squat by looking at a book if you keep the base of the book sturdy and you press down on it in compression nothing really happens but the second that you allow the base of the book to bend a little bit now all of a sudden the book wants to get smooshed when the base is supported there's a clear transfer of force from bottom to top but when the base isn't supported there's a lot of force dispersion that occurs keep in mind that buttwink is a completely normal thing technically speaking but if our goals are to train the lower segments what we should do is fix the segments of the upper body so that we can just create motion through the knee the ankle and the hip whatever your goal happens to be move the segment that you are trying to train and don't move the segment that you're not trying to train
