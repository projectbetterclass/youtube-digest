# Palantir - Robotics & Real World AI

<https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=CydG9qoDVOI>

Sarcos and Palantir, pushing the future of AI and robotics to the edge. Palantir's collaboration with Sarcos robotics and technology is pushing the boundaries of modern real-world AI operations. By combining the strength of Sarcos robotic systems with the edge capabilities of Palantir software. Palantir is focused on helping Sarcos stream high-volume sensor data from their robots in real time, run complex AI algorithms at the edge, and continuously deploy software updates to their fleet at scale. As Sarcos deploys its two flagship robots to an increasing number of customer environments in industries worldwide, computing at the edge reliably and efficiently is key. The Guardian XO robot is a full-body powered industrial exoskeleton offered via robot as a service that multiplies worker productivity while mitigating occupational injury and fatigue. The Guardian XT robot is a teleoperated dextrous robotic system that extends human capability in complex industrial environments like shipbuilding, powerline maintenance, or pipe inspection, reducing the need for humans to work in risky or dangerous environments. Palantir's edge offerings can enhance the operations of Sarcos robots as follows. With Palantir's edge streaming, Sarcos knows exactly what is happening with its robots at all times. Sarcos can stream data from approximately 125 sensors on each robot to the Palantir Foundry platform in real time, enabling high-volume data integration and analysis, driving rapid iteration and product development cycles. With Palantir Edge AI, Sarcos can integrate AI models fleet-wide across edge devices. Metadata from edge-deployed models flow back into the organization's core AI/ML infrastructure, accelerating Sarcos' work towards autonomy through model retraining, offering long-term reliable maintenance of edge-deployed AI models. Palantir Apollo for the edge. Sarcos can scale its fleet of robots by enabling continuous deployment of software updates from the single Palantir Apollo control layer. Apollo for the edge provides homogeneous control of heterogeneous environments where edge device requirements may differ based on location or customer need. Sarcos is focused on creating the most productive, cost-effective workforce in the world and its unique approach to AI technology helps support that vision. Palantir Foundry can also help organizations transform internal business operations by optimizing shipment and coordination of hardware parts, avoiding production stalls, and streamlining spend. Palantir aims to serve as the operating system for a growing number of companies seeking to automate their processes and workflows. Successful automation will continue to be a critical competitive advantage where interconnectivity and smart automation are paramount. Together, Palantir and Sarcos are augmenting human capability and charting the future of robotics and AI at the edge.
