# Too tall for the cable machine?

<https://www.youtube.com/shorts/bt3B1J0Bl6g>

Instead of sitting on the floor, a much more stable option is going to be if you kneel underneath of the pad so that you can increase the distance that your hand is away from the handle while still remaining more stable. If you sit on the floor, not only is it going to be difficult for you to actually take the weight back to where you need to be in the first place, but as you begin to pull and as load increases, you're going to get pulled more and more up off the floor. When you're kneeling underneath the pad, you can do a calf raise up into the pad to secure you down to the floor. And if your shins are too short, what you can do is simply put a plate underneath of your foot to make sure that you can stabilize yourself with the thigh pad.
