# HOW TO Lying Leg Raise

<https://www.youtube.com/shorts/rm7kBBLr6Fs>

The lying leg raise is by far the best way to train the lower abs. Most people do this wrong. This is how we do this right. Most people will use their hip flexors when they're doing [music] the lying leg raises opposed to their abs. The way you want to perform this movement is you want to take your sternum and your groin and you want to shorten this distance. Start with the deepest stretch you can possibly make, heels into the ground, and from there pull up into your torso and then extend. Again, seat position matters as well. Most people sit their butt too far on the bench. What you want to do is you actually want to have basically just your upper butt on the edge of the bench, [music] not your whole butt on the bench.
