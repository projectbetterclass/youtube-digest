<https://www.youtube.com/shorts/XsvB3KqIFxo>

I got out of the car just to record this video because I feel very strongly about this. This is why so many people are not making the progress they should be in the gym. When you have a workout program, you have rep ranges, right? The issue is that I see a lot of people just aiming to hit the top of that rep range, but the weight that they're using is not difficult whatsoever. So, maybe the rep range was eight to 10 reps, and they did 10 reps, so they're happy with that. But in reality, they probably could have done 15 or 20 reps, and those last five reps leading up to failure is where you're going to get the most progress. Otherwise, all you're doing is a bunch of junk volume, adding more fatigue, but not necessarily doing anything that is going to stimulate more growth. So, you might feel like you did a lot over that session or multiple sessions, but in reality, you're not working hard enough to be able to stimulate hypertrophy. Next time you step into the gym, I want you to challenge yourself, and instead of just stopping at the top of the rep range, keep going. See how many more reps you can do, and you might surprise yourself.
