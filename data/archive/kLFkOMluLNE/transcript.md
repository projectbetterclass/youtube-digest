# Why I LOVE Volatility when Trading Options

<https://www.youtube.com/shorts/kLFkOMluLNE>

The premiums for SoFi are really nice. So, I'm going to expand the $26 put option here. You can see the implied volatility 76. This is what's known as high implied volatility. High implied volatility happens when the market is uncertain. Investors saying that, "Hey, we don't know if the stock market is going to go up or down. Therefore, we are going to price options more expensive because more can happen. We need to be compensated for the risk that we are taking, which is actually a good thing if you're an option seller." Higher volatility makes higher premium. Higher premium makes you make more money. You make biceps over triceps money.
