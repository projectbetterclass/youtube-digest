# Why you might be coming back from vacation “stronger”

<https://www.youtube.com/shorts/8LcGt3H5WDc>

Have you ever taken some extra rest days off lifting or taken a week off of lifting due to vacation and found that you were actually stronger when you got back? What does that actually mean? Did you gain muscle during the time off of training? Did you maintain all your muscle? No, not likely. What likely happened is that you were not recovering as well as you thought you were from your program and then when you got back you were fully recovered. Therefore, you were able to express your full strength. The fact of the matter is that atrophy muscle loss happens relatively quickly after the initial stimulus period, so you are going to be losing some muscle after a week off of lifting. If you find that you consistently get stronger whenever you take more time off of the gym, that could be a sign that you need to look at your program and decide whether you need to adjust the intensity, the volume, or the frequency. That way you're better optimizing your recovery session to session and therefore going to make better progress. And you may not have to make major changes to your program. It could be as simple as not taking so many sets to failure. You could knock a set or two off of some of your days, or you could put an extra rest day in between your training days. If you never tried out using a more moderate amount of volume compared to high volume training, you may be surprised at how good the gains are.
