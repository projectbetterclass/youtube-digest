# AVOID this shoulder press?

<https://www.youtube.com/shorts/iQjqWwWCk44>

Lots of times I see people doing their shoulder presses like this, and this is so wrong. Let me show you how to do it. There is absolutely nothing wrong with doing a shoulder press like this. But, if you don't find that comfortable, you can simply just move your hands around to a position that does feel comfortable. A more vertical press like this will simply just target the front portion of the delts and the traps. And the farther down you move, the more you'll end up starting to target your chest as opposed to the front delt. Whatever the case, just pick the angle that suits your goal best and that feels comfortable for you. This is not a one-size-fits-all thing.
