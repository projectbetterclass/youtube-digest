# Complete Biceps Training Guide

<https://www.youtube.com/shorts/YiNJdNFbPIM>

the biceps are a muscle group that cross both the front of the shoulder and the front of the elbow which means that to fully stretch them we need to straighten the elbow and take the arm behind the body and to fully shorten them we need to bend the elbow and take the arm up and somewhere overhead both cable and dumbbell variations are going to be awesome choices to train the biceps but to train the fully lengthened position of the biceps I prefer face away cable curls where you step a little bit out in front of the cable stack and where your arms are slightly behind your body and your elbows are fully straightened at the bottom of the motion to train the fully shortened position we can either use something like a dumbbell preacher curl or a machine preacher curl where the arm is up and elevated in front or at least up into the sides of the body now also consider that there are other flexors of the elbow and the brachioradialis and the brachialis both of which are trained to predominantly with any sort of neutral grip variation like a traditional dumbbell Hammer curl or a cable curl with a neutral grip if you like this information and found it useful please like and share for more
