# Do THIS to fix your cable row.

<https://www.youtube.com/shorts/dD0jZeU1uKY>

have you ever felt really unstable trying to do these kinds of single arm cable rows if you have here are two simple steps to make yourself much more stable step number one is to make sure that the same side leg and foot are firmly planted as close to the cable origin as possible and when you do this you want to make sure that your foot is on the inside of that cable rather than the opposite side on the outside step number two is to take the opposite hand and put it on the outside of that same side working leg and use it to pull your leg inward to prevent that rotation from happening when you do those two things you'll be super able to row
