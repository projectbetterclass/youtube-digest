## 1. [00:58] Comparison chart of two investor types: Stock Picker vs Market Participant

STOCK PICKER / 'Active investing' / Doing your due diligence and selecting promising businesses to invest in. MARKET PARTICIPANT / 'Passive investing' / Staying widely diversified across the entire market to achieve the 'general market return'.

## 2. [01:27] Comparison slide with guiding questions for Stock Picker vs Market Participant investor types

STOCK PICKER / Am I going to put in the effort? Do I want to work hard to compound my money faster? MARKET PARTICIPANT / Do I want this simpler approach? Am I satisfied with a ~8% average annual return, and happy to build my wealth over decades?

## 3. [02:14] Definition of stock brokers and their role

STOCK BROKERS. Brokerage sites actually facilitate the trading of shares based on the buy/sell orders their customers submit.

## 4. [02:58] Example calculation of brokerage fees impact on investment

How much are you paying as a brokerage fee every time you buy/sell shares? 'I have $500 to invest' / Investment size: $500 / Brokerage costs: $60 / Brokerage = $30 / Your investment needs to make 12% just to break even.

## 6. [06:09] Investment advice about emotional decision-making

Do not act on short-term emotions. In investing, acting on your emotions is a one-way ticket to losing money.

## 7. [09:19] Stock picking criteria and requirements framework

STOCK PICKING / You must understand the business. / The business must have a competitive advantage. / The management must operate with skill and integrity.

## 8. [09:29] Extended stock picking criteria including valuation requirement

STOCK PICKING / You must understand the business. / The business must have a competitive advantage. / The management must operate with skill and integrity. / The shares must be at a discount to intrinsic value.
