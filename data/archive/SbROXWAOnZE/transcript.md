# Cardio is killing your gains?

<https://www.youtube.com/shorts/SbROXWAOnZE>

You can balance cardio with weightlifting as long as you do these three things. First, you do your cardio after weight training or at a separate time. It's totally fine to do a quick 5 to 10 minute cardio warm-up before lifting. But if you do your full-blown cardio session before weights, you might take a strength hit. >> So, everyone tells you that doing cardio before lifting is a bad idea. >> Conditioning work with your strength work is going to kill your gains. >> But is that actually true? Well, a new 2025 study helps answer the question. Researchers had 45 men train with both weights and cardio, but in different orders. One group did cardio first, followed by weights. The other lifted first, then did cardio after. At the end of 12 weeks, the results were pretty interesting. The group that did cardio first saw significant gains in strength, lean body mass, and V2 max. But the group that did weights first, they saw the same increase in V2 max, but they gained more lean body mass, 45% more maximum strength, 64% more explosive power, and 57% more muscle endurance compared to the cardio first group. Why? Because performing cardio creates central fatigue, which then negatively impacts weight training performance. However, doing weights first doesn't affect cardio in the same way. And this isn't just a one-off result. Multiple studies show the same pattern. So, if your goal is to maximize muscle growth and strength gains, save the cardio until after your workout or just do it at a different time altogether.
