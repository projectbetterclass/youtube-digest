# Why you should do curls on the lat pull-down machine

<https://www.youtube.com/shorts/m3RVw91YERM>

What if I told you that you could use a lat pulldown machine to train biceps and do biceps curls? What you can do is grab a dumbbell, lean back against the pad, and then use the pad as an anchor off of which to curl. Doing regular curls while you're standing is fine, but you don't have a support, so your shoulder often times gets really involved, and the weight starts to swing. And this is why leaning up against a pad and using a pad and shoving the arm into the pad is a really, really good idea for training biceps.
