## 6. [04:17] Text explanation of SEC Rule 10b5-1 regulation for insider trading plans.

What Is Rule 10b5-1?; Rule 10b5-1 is a Securities and Exchange Commission (SEC) regulation that allows insiders of public companies to set up a trading plan for selling stocks they own. Rule 10b5-1 permits major holders to sell a predetermined number of shares at a predetermined time. Many corporate executives use 10b5-1 plans to avoid accusations of insider trading. It is used to clarify Rule 10b-5, which was created under the Securities and Exchange Act of 1934, which is the primary vehicle for the investigation of securities fraud.
