# Upper/Lower is so stupid

<https://www.youtube.com/shorts/3BmNDRtd9tA>

Upper lower is so stupid. You have a million muscle groups on the upper and maybe three on the lower. I mean, two to three sets for chest, four to five sets for back, two sets for shoulders, biceps, and triceps. If you're truly locking in and pushing your sets close to failure, that's enough to get the job done and shouldn't take much more than an hour. Even if you prefer a bit more volume, it still shouldn't take more than 90 minutes. If it does, you're probably trying to cram too much into one session. >> [music] >> Six total movements is a solid amount, eight maximum, and to get in more exercise variety, you can rotate between an upper A and an upper B. Yes, there are more muscles to cover on an upper day, but upper body lifts are also less taxing in general compared to lower body lifts, which helps to balance things out. you have the structure and execution dialed in, a three to four-day upper lower split is a great option that hits every muscle efficiently across the week. It's my personal favorite that I always seem to come back to, and it's the number one program people use inside Train With Us. It's not a flashy split, but it covers everything you need without having to live in the gym.
