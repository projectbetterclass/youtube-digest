The barbell squat is by far the best way to train the quads. Most people do this wrong. This is how we do this right. Step number [music] one, you want to put your ring fingers on these rings. I always aim for the outer ring and simply just place your ring finger on that ring. [music] From there, you're going to duck underneath the weight until your lower traps sit [music] on the bar. From here, you're simply just going to stand up and take two steps back. Pull the armpits into back pockets. Keep the chest tall. Bend the knees. Squat all the way down until your calves [music] are in your hamstrings. And then from here, simply just stand all the way back up. A hot tip, you want the bar to be armpit height when you start this movement.
