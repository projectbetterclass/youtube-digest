# Creatine = Hair Loss? (New Study)

<https://www.youtube.com/shorts/HQ_TVun7fdM>

If you're a guy who takes creatine and you actually care about your hairline, you should probably know this. After 30 years, we finally have the first study that directly tested whether creatine causes hair loss. And they didn't just measure hormones, they did actual hair follicle imaging, too. Researchers tracked 45 resistance-trained men over the course of 12 weeks. One group took 5 g of creatine per day, while the other got a placebo. What were the results? No significant increase in DHT, which is the main hormone that drives male pattern baldness, no change in the ratio of DHT to testosterone, and no change in hair density, thickness, or growth phases. Now, does 12 weeks completely rule out a possible long-term effect? No. But the whole idea that creatine makes you go bald stems from one questionable paper on rugby players in 2009 that didn't even measure hair loss, and that study has never been replicated. Based on the current evidence we have, you're probably not avoiding hair loss by skipping creatine, you're likely just leaving results on the table.
