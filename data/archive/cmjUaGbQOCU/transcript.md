# Tutorial: DB Incline Press (upper chest)

<https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=cmjUaGbQOCU>

Here's a step-by-step guide on how to target the upper chest with a dumbbell incline press. Step number one, to find the appropriate angle for you, imagine that you're shoving your elbow directly into the inner collar bone. You should aim your biceps toward your chin when you're doing this. From there, lie down and observe the angle between your arm and the floor. When you have the perfect angle, you'll notice that there's a 90° angle between your upper arm and the floor. If the bench is too low, your arm will not be 90°. And the same is true if your bench is too high. If you like to arch your back a little bit more, you'll need to move the bench slightly higher to account for that angle change. And if you like a flatter back position, you'll need to lower the bench to account for the same. From there, assume an arm path that's maybe about 45° away from the torso. Once you've got the weights in your hands, aim to press in the same exact way that we set up the arm path initially. Drive your elbows upward and inward toward your nose or inner collar bone. This is so that the arm path can align with the upper portions of the chest appropriately. If that starting position doesn't feel great to you, then you can try out using a little bit of a wider arm path, which may feel better on your upper chest given your individual differences. You can also experiment around with a narrower arm path. There's no perfectly right or wrong answer because everyone is unique. Also, use a comfortable amount of range of motion for you. You don't need to try to touch the dumbbells all the way to the ground, and you don't need to fully lock out if you don't want to. I personally prefer a technique that looks something like this where I keep the dumbbells stacked over my elbows the whole time, but I'm not actively trying to get an excessive stretch on my chest. Just lowering down to where I'm comfortable and then pressing my elbow toward my chin.
