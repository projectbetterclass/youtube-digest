# The #1 Leg Curl Mistake

<https://www.youtube.com/shorts/_vklduPx46c>

one of the most common mistakes to make on the seated leg curl is to have the pad way too far away from the knee when you apply the pad close to the bottom side of the femur because the pad is really far away from the hip joint it has a really easy time stabilizing the hip so your legs don't move all over the place but if you apply the pad high up toward the femur and the pad itself has a tougher time stabilizing the hip because of how close to the hip it is so then your leg ends up shifting all over the place as you're trying to go through the curl so instead what I recommend is anchoring the PAD as close to the knee as you can make it just make sure that the pad doesn't actually sit on top of the knee capap itself
