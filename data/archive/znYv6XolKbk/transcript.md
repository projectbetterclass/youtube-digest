# If you want triceps that pop you need to understand how the three heads of the triceps work

<https://www.youtube.com/shorts/znYv6XolKbk>

If you want triceps that pop when you're not even doing tricep exercises, then you need to understand how the three heads of the triceps work. All three heads of the triceps do elbow extension as shown. However, the long head of the tricep does not get worked well in things like presses unlike the medial and lateral head because it crosses the shoulder and aids in shoulder extension. Therefore, to maximally develop your triceps, I would recommend including a push down like this where you have the arms at your sides as the long head has really good leverage to do elbow extension in this position. And if triceps are a big priority, then I would also include some kind of overhead extension as a secondary movement or on another day. Something to keep in mind is that the order of your exercises does matter due to fatigue. So, if you're putting triceps at the very end of your push days and you're not seeing much growth, it's worth putting them at the start of another day. That way you're not as fatigued going into your tricep push downs or overhead extensions. I often put triceps at the start of back or leg days for this reason.
