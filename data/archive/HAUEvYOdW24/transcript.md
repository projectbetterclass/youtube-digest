# The truth about electrolytes

<https://www.youtube.com/shorts/HAUEvYOdW24>

This is one of the biggest money grabs in the supplement industry right now. Guys, you don't need a $50 electrolyte powder to lift weights for an hour in an air-conditioned gym. These companies are acting like your lat pulldowns followed by an incline treadmill walk is the [ __ ] Tour de France. Most people aren't sweating enough for electrolyte loss to matter, and the small amounts you do lose are easily replaced through your regular diet and a good multivitamin. The majority of the population is already above the recommended intake for sodium, so paying premium prices to dump more on top doesn't make sense. And you don't need some fancy electrolyte matrix in your pre-workout either. It looks impressive on the label, but it's not something most lifters will actually benefit from. That's why I left it out of my PureForm pre-workout, which only uses ingredients that directly improve performance during your sessions. Now, if you are part of the small subset of people who train intensely for multiple hours in high heat, or you're an unusually heavy sweater, then electrolyte loss becomes more important, but even then all it usually takes is some regular salt added to your shaker for a few extra cents.
