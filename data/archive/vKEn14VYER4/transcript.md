# Put your feet HERE on leg press

<https://www.youtube.com/shorts/vKEn14VYER4>

If you want to buy us more glutes in a leg press, put your feet high, keep them narrow. If you want to buy us more quads in a leg press, put them low and either narrow or wide, whichever you like better. And if you want to buy us a little bit more adductor magnus, that big hip extensor guy, put your feet a little bit higher and a little bit pointed out.
