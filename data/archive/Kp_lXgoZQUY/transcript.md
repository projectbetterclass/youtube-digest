# Fix your lateral raise pain using this one trick!

<https://www.youtube.com/shorts/Kp_lXgoZQUY>

have you ever used a lateral raise machine and just haven't felt comfortable doing it if you have here's a quick variation that you can do on it instead that may feel better for you what you're going to do instead is do it single arm turn slightly in toward the working arm use the opposite hand to serve as your anchor for the exercise to make sure that you don't get turned in the opposite direction turning slightly more toward the pad may feel better for you and not grabbing the handle in front of you and just pretending like you're doing a regular lateral Ray may also actually improve the mechanics of your shoulder as a consequence of being less restricted through the elbow and the forearm
