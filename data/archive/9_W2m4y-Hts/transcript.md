# Big juicy triceps

<https://www.youtube.com/shorts/9_W2m4y-Hts>

This is one of the simplest changes you can make for big juicy triceps and this may even help if you've been having some elbow discomfort during your push-downs. Most gyms only have this short rope, which is okay. It'll still work your triceps. My issue with it is if you are larger than a small child then it forces you into this range of motion and doesn't allow you to adjust your upper arm position nearly as well as a longer attachment would. For example, if I wasn't limited by the shorter rope, this is where my elbow would want to extend to. It's a bit easier to see from the back so I want you guys to take a look at the difference between using a short rope and a longer rope attachment. This is the short rope. Now compare that to a longer rope attachment, which allows me to naturally flare out my upper arms, which is going to better align the long head of the triceps, the biggest head, with its origin on the scapula, which is hopefully going to result in a better stimulus for the long head. A lot of my clients also find this to be easier on their elbows. Now, what if your gym only has short ropes? Don't worry, I got you. You're going to take two short ropes, pull them through, and then you're going to use both of them as your new long rope attachment. From there, all you have to do is slightly pull the upper arm back, allow it to flare naturally, and then keep the upper arm fixed in place, only moving about the elbow. Then fully extend the elbow until your hands are at your sides. This also works for other exercises like pull-overs if you were not able to close off that end range with a shorter rope.
