# Action leads to motivation

<https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=wx8ydyUtrXg>

How do you self-motivate? It's one of those things where I see a bit of a loop, right? And this came from Mark Manson. He's got a great book called Subtle Art of Not Giving an F. And in that book, Mark highlights that people tend to think we need to sit around to wait to be motivated. And And it's true. Sometimes we search for motivation on Instagram. We search for motivation in environment. Sometimes we search for motivation in books. Sometimes we fall into the mindset of thinking, "I'm going to sit around and wait to be motivated." It's true. You can sit and wait for motivation, or you can go out there, take action. And take action. Motivation doesn't just come out of thin air or from an Instagram video. Motivation also can be generated through action. So, for me, the way I stay motivated is when I'm feeling like, "Oh, I'm not really motivated today." Then I'll just start to take some action. Hand on my heart. This morning when I woke up and I started working again, I kind of went, "Ah, I don't know if I want to do it." A lot of emails came through during the break. So, I sat then and I went, "You know what? I'm just going to take action. I'm going to do the hardest thing first." And the hardest thing for me today was recording a few coaching videos. I just did those things first. And once I took action, I did those things, guess what followed? Started to feel motivated. And then once I was motivated, I used that fuel source to do the things that I needed to do. I don't wait for motivation to find me. I take action, and in the process of taking action, I generate motivation. That's how I view it.
