# Barbell squats in the snow

<https://www.youtube.com/shorts/NHyGb7MXP24>

Yeah, I was 15 years old and training from home, but the problem was that I didn't have a squat [music] rack. So, on leg day what I'd do is take all my sand-filled Walmart weight plates from the basement, load them all into a wheelbarrow, and wheel them over to the other side of the lawn where there were these two big, jagged, uneven rocks that I can just barely rest the bar [music] across. Un-rack it, do my set of squats to failure, drop the bar off my back onto the lawn, take the plates off, put the bar back up, load the plates back on. I even remember doing this out in the snow a few times during winter. I mean, obviously injuries and illnesses are exceptions, [music] but if you're genuinely serious about your fitness goals and you're physically capable, there's almost always a way to make it happen if you truly wanted to.
