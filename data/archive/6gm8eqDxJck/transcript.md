# Why does arching make your bench stronger?

<https://www.youtube.com/shorts/6gm8eqDxJck>

So, why is it that you're so much stronger with a big arch in a bench press as compared to a really flat back posture? So, when you're in this flat back posture and you're trying to bench press, but you'll notice that all the pecs are actually pulling in very different directions. What this does is it actually makes you much weaker because all these different muscles are contributing to very different directions. So, what happens when you're able to arch your back much more like this is all of the pecs actually get stretched more and as a consequence because the arm is farther behind the body, all of the pecs actually align and want to pull more in the same direction. So, all of the pecs can actually contribute to the same exact motion rather than pulling in very distinct directions. With this flat back posture because the arm is more in line with the body, lower pec wants to pull down, the middle pec wants to pull this way, and the upper pec actually wants to pull the arm in the opposite direction as the lower pec. And then when we assume this more arched position, what happens again is the pecs stretch more and because the pecs stretch more, they're not only stronger, but again all of them are pulling in a much more similar direction where they all want to pull the arm forward from this spot and not forward and across or forward and upward.
