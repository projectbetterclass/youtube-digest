# Feeling triceps on pullovers - the solution

<https://www.youtube.com/shorts/bLzNZxJbzMI>

If you're struggling to feel too much triceps during this movement, here's a quick change that you can make that will likely help that a lot. Number one, before you set up and go to start pulling, bend your elbow and keep that elbow angle soft. Number two, as you initiate the pull, think about pulling from your armpits and your elbows rather than your hands. If you drive from the armpits and the elbows, you'll be much more likely to use your lats and your back muscles as compared to your triceps, which would be more likely if you just think about pushing with your hands Down.
