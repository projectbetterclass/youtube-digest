# Optimal upper/lower workout

If you want the upper lower workout that's going to make all the aunties tell you that it's enough muscle and that girls don't like that sort of thing, then I've got the perfect workout for you. We're starting out with a JM press doing two sets of eight to 12 reps. If you don't have a flat machine press you can do this variation with it at your gym then you could always use the Smith machine instead. Next up we have leg press in the six to eight rep range using a low foot placement with the seat all the way back and this is going to do a good job of training the hip extensors as well as the quads. Guys need glutes too so we're doing two sets of six to eight reps of glute bridges. This workout was taken from my three-day Titan build training team program that I just dropped this week along with four and five-day options. I also dropped three to five-day options for my lower body focus training team glute maxed. Bouncing back upper body we've got two sets of six to 10 reps of pec deck flies. Pay attention to how I'm allowing my elbows to bend as I'm coming back through the eccentric and then I'm punching my arms forward driving the upper arm into the side of my pec to finish the rep. For the back we're doing two sets of eight to 12 reps of these neutral grip pull-downs. Use a handle that closely aligns with the sides of your torso that way you can use the proper arm path and allow your shoulders to elevate at the top before pulling again. To finish off the quads we've got one set of six to 10 reps of leg extensions. Make sure the knee is aligned with the axis of rotation and pull yourself into the seat. I'm opting to do this unilaterally because the stack is not heavy enough to do it with both legs but you definitely can do this exercise bilaterally.
