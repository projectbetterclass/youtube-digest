# Slight arch during an incline press is not a big deal

<https://www.youtube.com/shorts/YvAciXJaYkI>

It's funny because people try to call me out on things that I made videos on in the past, but they take it to these extremes and speak in absolutes because they don't understand the nuances of the topic. Arching on the incline press for stability is not an issue of anything that's going to make the press a bit better. And even if you are arching a bit, I mean it's it's kind of hard to see here, but I am slightly arching. If you're arching a bit and you're using say a 45° incline bench, well, now the degree of incline is maybe 40°. That that's maybe how much it's changing, 35. As long as you're using the correct arm path you're not flaring out too much with your press, then it's still very much so going to be an upper chest biased exercise. While a slight arch is not an issue, it is an issue if somebody is using a more extreme arch like you would see somebody using on a flat bench press during a powerlifting competition. If you were doing that on your incline or overhead press, then you very well could be turning it into more of a flat press.
