# Stop Making THIS Pressing Mistake!

<https://www.youtube.com/shorts/Au868L5EeYU>

The number one pressing mistake that you're making is using too wide of an arm path. Using too wide of an arm path is going to do a couple of things. The first thing that it's going to do is it's actually going to decrease the amount of range of motion that you can experience toward the bottom end of the range where the muscle should be stretched the most and should be experiencing the most amount of force. In addition, too wide of an arm path is going to lead to a less structurally stabilized position meaning that not only is the joint less structurally supported from a bony and a connective tissue standpoint, but also from a muscular standpoint where the muscles have less leverage able to actually pull the arm up and across the body. Using a slightly narrower arm path between 40 and 60° is not only going to give you more range of motion toward the bottom end of the movement, but it's also likely going to be much more structurally stable and much more comfortable on most of your shoulders and elbows. Keep in mind that if you're using machines instead of free weights or cables, you have to sort of abide by the laws of the machines which may force you to use an arm path that's too narrow or too wide for your structure.
