# You Haven't Tried This Triceps Exercise

<https://www.youtube.com/shorts/8oSpSbRFV38>

Doing overhead extensions with a brace behind the upper arm is a great way to target the more stretched position of the triceps with a lot of stability. But, if you want to really focus on the most stretched position you can get the triceps in, you want to make sure that the cable is set relatively higher. So, to maximize for the stretched position, you want to just make sure that the cable is aligned 90° from the forearm when your elbow is in the most bent position. I'll usually end up putting the origin of the cable pretty close to the actual shoulder itself. There's nothing wrong with putting it a little bit lower, but that just means it'll be heavier toward a more shortened position of the triceps. Both variations are going to be great for training the triceps. Having the cable a little bit higher will actually maximize for the stretch stimulus a little bit more.
