# Low carb is NOT optimal

<https://www.youtube.com/shorts/68n_z3vmgQg>

I'm honestly so tired of all these social media goofballs proudly showing off their cutting boards full of steak and eggs and acting like they've unlocked some sort of superhuman diet. Can you build muscle and get lean and perform while eating low carb? Sure, but you're not going to do it optimally and you're just making things harder for no real benefit. The plain fact is that carbs are your body's preferred fuel for hard training and when glycogen gets low, not only will your physique will look flatter and less filled out, but your brain senses this and actually creates fatigue to prevent further depletion. There's even research showing that just swishing carbs around in your mouth can improve performance because your brain has receptors to detect it and [music] will reduce those fatigue signals. Now, if you're cutting carbs for a specific health reason or you just prefer eating low carb and feel better that way, that's totally fine, but just understand that you're the exception and not the rule. Most people will do much better both physically and mentally with carbs included. It won't hinder fat loss as long as total calories are matched and if you've made eating off a cutting board your entire personality, it might be time to broaden your horizons a bit.
