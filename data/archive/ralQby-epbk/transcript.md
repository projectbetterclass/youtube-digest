# The Best AB Exercise You've Never Done!

<https://www.youtube.com/shorts/ralQby-epbk>

This is an athome ab exercise for the sixpack muscles. All you're going to do is lean over top of your hands so that you feel stable and keep your feet against whatever surface you're using. I'm personally using my kitchen counter and it's the perfect height and size for me. You can play around with different options. Do whatever feels really, really great. In terms of queuing for this exercise, make sure that you're keeping your shoulders kind of out of your ears and that you're really just focusing on curling your butt up to the ceiling. And yes, the cat is optional but preferred.
