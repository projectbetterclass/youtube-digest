The majority of the exercises that I program are two sets because you really don't need as much volume as you think to see good progress. And it's cool seeing people who are more advanced lifters taking this approach, a more moderate approach to their volume, doing only one or two sets so they can hit that muscle with a higher frequency and recover better in between their sessions. The thing is, and I think the reason that a lot of people don't like this approach is because you have to understand how to train hard if you're only doing one or two sets. There are a lot of people who make up for their lack of intensity in the gym by just adding more volume, but when you only have one or two working sets you can do, you have to understand how to train hard from that very first set. Anyways, solid workout, solid physique.
