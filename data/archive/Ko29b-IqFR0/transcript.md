# Avoid This 1 Key Back Training Mistake ❌

<https://www.youtube.com/shorts/Ko29b-IqFR0>

If you're trying to build a bigger back, but you mostly feel it in your arms, you're probably making this common mistake. For all pulling exercises, make sure the line of force from the weight is running directly in line with your forearms. If your forearms are angled backward or forward excessively, you'll be shifting emphasis onto elbow flexion or extension, and those smaller arm muscles will become the weak link in the movement. Fix this and you'll see an instant increase in strength and overall back gains.
