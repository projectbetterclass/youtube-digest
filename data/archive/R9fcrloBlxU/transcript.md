# How to: DB Overhead Triceps Extension

<https://www.youtube.com/shorts/R9fcrloBlxU>

the dumbbell overhead triceps extension could be a great exercise to Target the triceps but here's one simple trick to make it even more effective instead of doing it standing up lie sideways on a bench then place the back of your arm on top of the bench and use your opposite hand to pull yourself into the bench when you do it with the back of your arm supported you'll be able to actually just move through the range of motion at your elbow so that shoulder stability isn't an issue
