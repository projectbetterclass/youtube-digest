# Stop doing leg extensions!

<https://www.youtube.com/shorts/jwWN5P6Qb5I>

Yep, stay off this machine unless you're looking forward to knee replacement surgery. It isolates the knee in the worst possible way causing the quad to yank on the patellar tendon with zero support. Look, we don't do anything in life by kicking our leg out in front of us with a pad over our shins. Actually, we do. Leg extensions, cuz they're literally one of the best quad builders that exist. They directly train the quads through their function of knee extension. They're highly stable so you can just sit [music] down and push close to failure with full focus, and they also target the rectus femoris, which is one head of the quads that squat patterns won't develop maximally. I've personally done leg extensions for over 20 years now, including [music] low rep sets to failure, and my knees feel perfectly great. It's almost like loading a joint gradually and progressively over time makes it stronger, not weaker. Just make sure you're adequately warmed up first before moving into your heavier working [music] sets, and that you're using an amount of weight you can lift under strict control using a deliberate eccentric, [music] and starting out with slightly higher reps more toward the 8 to 10 range if it's a newer movement for you. But as long as your execution is dialed in, a leg extension paired with a squat pattern is as good as it gets for overall quad development.
