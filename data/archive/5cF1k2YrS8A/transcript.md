# Programming with Muay Thai

<https://www.youtube.com/shorts/5cF1k2YrS8A>

Since I started doing Muay Thai, I've had so many people ask me what exercises that I do translate well to Muay Thai, what's going to be beneficial for that. And the thing is, it's all beneficial. I train 3 days a week in the gym, and I'm still training the exact same way that I was when I was bodybuilding. The only difference is that I've added in Muay Thai on top of that. Like sure, I can make a video saying, "Oh, do glute bridges because glute bridges are going to help with your teeth [music] and chest presses going to help with your punches." But the thing is, all the exercises that I do that make my muscles stronger, make my body stronger as a whole are going to be beneficial. I've been lifting for the last 16 years, so I already [music] started with a really good strength and mobility base because I train hard, I lift with a good range of motion. So, the technique improvements that I've made are from practicing the technique, working in the Muay Thai gym. The average person who's training a combat sport doesn't need a highly specialized program for that combat sport. They just need to make sure that they can recover from the program that they're doing. The biggest change that I made to my lifting program is that I went from lifting 5 days a week when I was bodybuilding to 3 days a week now to accommodate more Muay Thai training. I also don't do that much static stretching at all, but I can still throw head kicks, and that came from one of my strength training program, which is going to help with my mobility, and then also just practicing the technique over and over. Slowly but surely, I was able to kick higher and higher. >> [music]
