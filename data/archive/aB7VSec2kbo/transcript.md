# How i got rid of my shoulder blade pain

<https://www.youtube.com/shorts/aB7VSec2kbo>

I used to have tightness and pain right here for the better part of several years. Tried so many different things. Stretches, mobilizations, banded drills, rotator cuff work, and none of it actually got me any better. The only thing that took me from feeling like garbage every day here to not having felt that pain in literally like 4 years now is simply just training all of the different scapular motions, and more specifically allowing my scapula to move where it needed to in all of my different exercises. See, many of us get told that the proper position to have your shoulder blades in is constantly back and down toward the spine. You want to actually allow that thing to move. It's literally designed for that. This is probably the biggest problem for me with presses, where I was told that, "Okay, I need to pin my shoulder blades back, and when I press, I need to just not allow them to move." Well, if you're constantly pinning your shoulder blades back and you're keeping all of these muscles shortened as much as possible, what do you think is going to happen to a muscle that is constantly shortened all the time? You need to allow this thing to wrap around your ribs as you press. You need to allow this thing to elevate when your arm goes overhead. You need to drive this thing down when you're pulling something down. When you're pulling something back, that's certainly a time to pull your shoulder blade back, as well. Just get the shoulder blade moving, and in all your exercises, wherever your upper arm is moving, allow your shoulder blade to follow. This is what worked for me.
