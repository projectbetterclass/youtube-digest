# Shrugs - the fix!

<https://www.youtube.com/shorts/hyXq3b7AvVk>

Why do your dumbbell and barbell shrugs bother your shoulders and what can you do to fix it? Both dumbbells and barbells simply want to pull your arm to the floor. But the motion of a shrug isn't really a downward motion, it's actually an upward and inward motion. When the clavicle pivots upward, it moves the scapula up toward the skull, not just straight up and down to the sky. What this means is that when you do a shrug, your arm is going to naturally want to pivot outward along with that. As a test, what you can do is fix your elbow to your side and do a maximal shrug and just simply watch how your arm actually moves outward instead of just staying straight up and down. This sort of jammed up position has a potential to create an unstable joint position. So rather than using a free weight implement, what I recommend is using a cable. Using a cable allows you to load your arm outward so that you can move inward and outward with the arm as opposed to being jammed up to your side. What this allows for is a more stable joint position. Now, if you are forced to use a free weight implement, what I would recommend is sort of side bending over to one side and leaning so that your arm is actually loaded slightly outward relative to your body instead of being jammed close to your side.
