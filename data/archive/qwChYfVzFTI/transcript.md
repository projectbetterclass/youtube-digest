# You can’t “fix” posture

<https://www.youtube.com/shorts/qwChYfVzFTI>

The reason why I don't try to change my posture is because I don't think that there's anything to fix. And if you're someone who wants to change their posture to achieve some kind of permanent perfect state, just think about all of the different activities that you do and that you would like to do. And ask yourself if any one of those activities, even in and of themselves, only assumes one posture. The answer is always no. So then the question becomes what exactly are you trying to get to with changing your posture? Why are you trying to get there? And do you really ever think there will be a point at which you can maintain a perfect posture? This is kind of like the idea of being a perfect person. What does it mean? Well, maybe it means something like doing the thing that you know that you want to do in every scenario. But every scenario is different. And every scenario requires different things of you.
