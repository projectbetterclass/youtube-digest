# What does this exercise target?

was this press that Jay Cutler used to do for the PC or was it for the triceps we look at the direction of resistance of this motion from the point of application where he's grabbing the bar line of resistance is actually going upward directly through his shoulder and if the resistance is angled like this directly through the shoulder that also means that it's far from the elbow and that as he does the push down the elbow is doing pretty much all of the work and the muscles of the shoulder are doing very little so although this may look like some kind of decline cable pressing exercise can actually be a really effective choice for training the triceps because of how he's grabbing the bar and how he's allowing the elbows to flare outward during the motion
