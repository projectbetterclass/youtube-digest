this	  may	  have	  happened?

b. The	  proportion	  of	  stock	  held	  by	  institutional	  investors	  has	  increased.	  
c. Transactions	  costs	  for	  trading	  equity	  have	  decreased	  
d. Companies	  feel	  much	  less	  secure	  or	  certain	  about	  future	  earnings	  
e. Investors	  are	  more	  short	  term	  than	  they	  used	  to	  be.

before	  earnings	  increases	  
b. Dividend	  increase	  contemporaneously	  with	  earnings	  
c. Dividends	  increases	  lag	  earnings	  increases
