# JPGCOACHING- 4 EXERCISE LEG DAY

Here's my leg day from yesterday that took around an hour to an hour and a half. It's only four exercises, but it's extremely effective when done with intensity. Eight to 10 reps of a seated hamstring curl with two to three second negatives on the way up. My wife also had pendulum squats programmed in today. She has an excellent coach. It's me. We both did six eight reps and I wanted to go up to a top set of four reps, but the whole machine was starting to sway after five plates, so I thought it best to stop there. After that, I did eight to 10 reps for each side on walking lunges with this tempo. Notice how I'm not propelling myself forward with that back leg. Finished off the session with eight to 12 reps of seated leg extensions.
