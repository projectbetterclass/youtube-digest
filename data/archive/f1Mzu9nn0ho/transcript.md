stop doing push-ups like this this can lead to shoulder injury instead do push-ups like this doing push-ups with your arms wide is not more dangerous than doing push-ups with your arms tucked doing wider push-ups will simply recruit more of the upper portions of the PEC in the front delt and doing a narrower push-up will simply recruit more of the middle and lower pecs so if your goals are more specific to the upper PEC choose a little bit of a wider arm path but if your goals are more specific to the middle and lower packs you can do more of a narrow push-up in both cases the triceps will be involved and neither the wide nor the narrow push-up is better they just simply do different things
