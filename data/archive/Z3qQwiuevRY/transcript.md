# Can’t grow rear delt? Do this!

<https://www.youtube.com/shorts/Z3qQwiuevRY>

Many people struggle to feel, target, and grow their rear delts until they discover this exercise. And no, it is not the face pull. What you're going to do is set up next to a cable stack. Take your arm and basically bring your elbow directly to your nose with your palm facing behind you. After you bring your arm there, you want to set a cable wherever your hand ends up. From there, you can either use a cup or loop the dandle around the back side of your hand. Basically, just perform a reverse fly, not side to side directly, but with a resistance that is pulling slightly behind you. This will be a lengthened emphasis and will limit the amount of trap and other accessory muscle involvement in the motion. You can also feel free to do this with a little bit more of a bent elbow where the elbow is static or even with an elbow position that is changing throughout the range. You just want to make sure that your hand is in some way getting pulled across your body and that you're pulling to about when your arm gets to like the side of your body. I actually even like to limit the range a little bit more just to the range where my arm is hanging in front of my body. If you've never done this exercise before, I recommend starting with anywhere between 10 and 15 reps on average because going a bit too heavy right out of the gate can feel a little clunky just given the position and the fact that it might be novel to Yeah.
