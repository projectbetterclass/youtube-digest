# What my family made me realise

<https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=wFD3XrmsOWY>

How has your role as a husband and a father impacted and influenced your career and your other decisions and your overall brand and just your enjoyment of life? Something my wife said to me when I was in the US. When I lived in Southern California, I lived there 2017, 18, 19, 20. And I wasn't a good father. I wasn't a good family man for many of those years. Only got better towards the end of those years. Cuz I was away doing like 90 events a year, 200 days on the road. And I remember one time she said to me, "You know, you work so hard to provide for us this beautiful home, this beautiful garden, metaphorically, but you're not in it." And that was a lesson that taught me to reflect on well, why am I working? And first of all to me it was to create abundance. And then second of all was all for the family. And also they don't have to worry about money. But I I I didn't do enough digging into myself as to why I was working so hard. And my family made me realize that well, the reason I was working was to actually build this beautiful environment to actually live in with my family. So I think my big words now that I kind of have in front of me in my office is I want to be more intentional. I want to be more deliberate. And I want to be more strategic about what I do. So when I take on a job, well, I want to take my wife. I want to take my son. It's a condition. I got to take them. It's got to be more intentional, more deliberate, more strategic, more aligned. So so things have to serve a greater purpose. It can't just be doing things blindly. It's not, "Oh, I'll take that gig, but it's 5 weeks away from my family." It's more well, how how can I be more intentional about it? How can I live while I work as opposed to just work? So I think that's a big lesson for me is how how to live on the way, not just suffer and sacrifice and then live.
