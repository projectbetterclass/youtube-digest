# How to avoid VOCAL FRY (with @The Futur )

I've never liked my voice. I had to get used to it. I tolerate it, and eventually I'm okay with it. What are the lessons that you've learned that can be applied to me in terms of my voice and what I can do to be a better effective speaker? Thank you for the opportunity to better share some thoughts here. There's something really interesting in the world of communication where it's about energizing your sentences completely. Energizing your sentences completely means that you start the sentence with the same energy as you complete the sentence. And when I listen to you talk, one of the things I notice is you start your sentences extremely energized, but at the end of your sentences you fall into vocal fry. And vocal fry is that part of our voice where when we no longer energize it, what people tend to do is they talk to they run out of breath, and at the end of their sentences they just run out of energy completely. What I noticed happening a few times, and I think it's because your thought processes they happen very fast, and your voice is trying to keep up with the thought process, and we don't think about taking another breath soon enough. So to me, the simple tweak there is to take regular breaths and energizing the sentences from beginning to the end. Perfect. I love that. Thank you for that analysis.
