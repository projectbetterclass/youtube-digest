# What bench press is best?

<https://www.youtube.com/shorts/XcgzpWc4Rr4>

saying that an arcing motion is better than a straight motion is not correct these kinds of presses simply just do different things if you are lowering the bar tighter to your rib cage what that means is that the resistance has to come down closer to your hips and then when you press it'll come closer to your face but when you press with a wider arm path resistance can just follow your arm out in a straight line and then move back up to your face in that same plane and if you're on a flat angle and you use a wider path which ends up being a straighter bar path that will simply recruit more of the upper half of the pecs in the front delt but when you use a more narrow arm path in other words that J path you'll end up recruiting a greater proportion of the middle and the lower paxis compared to the wide press so instead of assuming that the bar path is what makes the difference look at where the arms are directed and then ask yourself is that an alignment with what I'm actually trying to train again wider presses generally mean more upper pecs and narrower presses end up including more of the middle and the lower pecs just choose the technique that more closely aligns with your goals
