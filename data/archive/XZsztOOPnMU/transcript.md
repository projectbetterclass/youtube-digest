# Wide vs narrow rows: the differences

<https://www.youtube.com/shorts/XZsztOOPnMU>

What's the difference between a wide grip row and a narrow grip row? Here's an easy way to think about it. Imagine that there's a line parallel with this bone. Wherever you move the bone in space, the fibers that are parallel to the bone will do the most amount of work. So, when the arm is wider to the body and we're doing wide grip rows, muscles like the rear delts, the traps, and the rhomboids will do more total work. And in addition, the upper lats, which also sit parallel to the arm in this position, will do work, especially in the lengthened position. Now, when we perform narrow rows and our arms are tighter to our body, the fibers that become parallel are actually the lats from this perspective. Although there will still be a good amount of rear delt involvement, lats have a fiber direction that allows them to act very directly when the arm is tighter to the body. So, overall, both the wide and the narrow grip row will train the upper lats, as well as the rear delts and the traps. But, the wider grip row will train a greater proportion of the upper back muscles, and the narrow grip row will train a greater proportion of the lats.
