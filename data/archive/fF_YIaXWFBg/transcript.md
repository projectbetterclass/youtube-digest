# Do planks really train abs?

<https://www.youtube.com/shorts/fF_YIaXWFBg>

Why are planks not the most effective exercise for training abs? If you picture that this is the floor over here and this is someone doing a plank, the force of the floor is really trying hard to shove the shoulder blade backward. This essentially makes the shoulder a weight-bearing joint and is often very difficult for people to manage. The other contact points of a plank are the feet and the force of the floor is essentially shoving the foot directly backward. So, in order to resist this motion of our legs moving backward, our hip flexors have to hold our legs there. And although we do get some loading of the abs from this force and from the force of the shoulders, it's very indirect. What I would recommend for training abs instead are motions where we're moving both the trunk and the pelvis closer together. This could be a bending front to back, a bending side to side, or some kind of rotation-based motion. I personally prefer to do cable crunches, cable rotations, and cable side bends. Where again, we can load a ton of trunk motion in the abs very specifically as compared to loading ourselves through the shoulders and the hips indirectly.
