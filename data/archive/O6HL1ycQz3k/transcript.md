So doing a chest fly with your chest up isn't necessarily better, just different. When you keep your chest more down and you move your arm more like this, you'll be targeting more of the upper portions of the chest because those fibers are more aligned with where your arm is moving. When you puff your chest up, all you're doing is basically lowering the angle that you're moving the arm. It will recruit more of the lower half of the chest. So more arch for more lower part of the chest, less arch for more of the upper chest. Both are great and neither are right or wrong, it just depends on what you're trying to do.
