# Why do your elbows flare when you press?

<https://www.youtube.com/shorts/2U3ssxjGr6M>

why does elbow flare occur in a bench press and what can we do to fix it if it's something that needs fixing short answer is that if we're trying to get this locked elbow position what we need to do is we need to internally rotate the shoulder to some degree when the shoulder internally rotates the arm naturally and the elbow naturally wants to come more out to the side to be stacked under the bar as opposed to being in front of the body and then having to move behind the body in addition when we do a press that's more tight to our rib cage where our arm is tighter to the body we end up in a position where even if we are trying to maintain that tucked elbow we end up flaring because again it's a little bit easier of a mechanical position to be in if you do want to perform a press with your arm tighter to your body with this sort of front to back motion as opposed to the side to side motion that's totally fine and it may actually be better for some of you but just keep in mind that if you are doing that tighter arm path thing that your brain is going to want to flare your elbow as long as your intent is to get to this locked position if you do want to use a tighter on path press what I would highly recommend is just to aim to press over your sternum and not to lock your elbow as opposed to the intent to move to this position if your brain knows that you're trying to get to this locked position it's going to take the path of least resistance which is going to be the flare of the arm
