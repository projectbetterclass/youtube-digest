# The cable curl mistake everyone still makes (easy fix)

<https://www.youtube.com/shorts/0ZSCT9SBP5Y>

Can you spot the mistake in this curl? The mistake is putting the cable too high and more specifically trying to load the bottom position of the movement as much as possible around that 90° angle. And many people think that this is just universally a good thing to load more of the stretch position without considering other more practical things about an exercise. So take the example of this cable curl. What do you notice about the direction of force in relation to the elbow and then also the shoulder? What you'll note is that the cable's line of force is twice the distance to the shoulder as it is the elbow. And basically what this means is that the cable is imposing twice the amount of resistance on the shoulder as compared to the elbow in that bottom position. Just lower the cables. When you lower the cables and you load your arms at a little bit more of a diagonal angle, now you're still loading the bottom proportionately more than the top, but you're not overloading the shoulder to nearly the same degree as you were in the first variation where the cable was angled close to 90° from the arm and the bottom.
