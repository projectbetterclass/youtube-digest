# Why cutting will make you look worse

<https://www.youtube.com/shorts/UTfhWDDP0tY>

Before you start cutting, you need to keep this in mind or there's a way higher chance you'll just get discouraged and quit. When you drop into a deficit, you're probably going to look worse before you start to look better. That's because as your calories decrease, you'll lose some muscle fullness, and at the same time, even though you're getting leaner, your body fat is still too high for the definition to really show through. So, for a while, you'll kind of just look like a smaller, softer version of your previous self. A lot of people panic when they see that because they think it means they're losing a bunch of muscle. But, as long as you're still pushing sets close to failure, you're eating enough protein, and your strength is mostly maintaining, you're probably not losing anything at all. And that drop in size is mostly just from less glycogen, less water, and a bit less fat. You have to stay the course because eventually you'll cross over to the other side where the cuts really start to show. A lot of times it happens suddenly where you just wake up one morning and it looks like you got way leaner overnight. And then from there, you'll just look better and better every week.
