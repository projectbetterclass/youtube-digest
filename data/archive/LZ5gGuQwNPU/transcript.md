# Should you script your presentation?

<https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=LZ5gGuQwNPU>

a lot of people who are new to presenting sometimes they feel really scared and anxious so they print out their entire presentation on maybe 20 pieces of paper and then they have it on the lectern with them because they believe that if i have my entire speech with me there's no way i'm going to be able to forget what i'm saying now the problem with this is if you've typed out your entire talk and you've got that with you if you go on a roll for maybe five minutes and you don't look down at your notes you'll forget where you are and then when you go back down to look where you're up to you won't be able to find your place in the sea of paper that's in front of you with this new technology that elon musk has developed we're going to be able to eject ourselves out of any awkward situation [Music] [Music] instead of doing this what you should do is just have one page on stage with you at the lectern if you have to have this and instead of having the whole script just have bullet points on your key speaking points if you've rehearsed your talk properly all you need are the bullet points you don't need the entire script
