# Improve your leg extensions (ONE TIP!)

<https://www.youtube.com/shorts/RI4iZDMumi4>

Why should you point your toes upward during leg extensions? When you point the toes upward, you lengthen the part of the calf that crosses the backside of the knee. Because all the quads pull on the front side of the knee, the calf is an important stabilizer in all quad-based motions. As a consequence of this, a slight increase in length of the calves can actually make it so that your knees feel better and so that your leg extension is stronger. If you feel a stretch that's a little bit too strong in the backside of the knee when you do this, you can simply just not pull your toes as far upward.
