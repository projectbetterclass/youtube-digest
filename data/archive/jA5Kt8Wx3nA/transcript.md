# Old school chest machine :o

<https://www.youtube.com/shorts/jA5Kt8Wx3nA>

what makes this old school chess fly machine so awesome and different from a lot of common pec deck machines chess fly machines that have a pad that falls on the upper arm take away the complication that you might experience through the elbow the forearm and the wrist and this can be really beneficial when you're just trying to isolate your chest but many gyms don't have this kind of machine so here's a quick way to mimic the concepts instead of grabbing the handles on a peck fly machine you can place your forearms or your elbows on the handles instead so that you can perform the chest flies without having to deal with any elbow forearm or wrist complications
