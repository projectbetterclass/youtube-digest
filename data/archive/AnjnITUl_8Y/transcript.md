# Why does this grip work?

<https://www.youtube.com/shorts/AnjnITUl_8Y>

here's why using a thumbless grip often feels better for training back than wrapping the thumb around when you wrap the thumb around fully all the muscles in the forearm are forced to contract when all these forearm muscles tighten up you're actually Limited in how much you can rotate your forearm but when you wrap the thumb to the other side you can actually allow the handle to move farther up in your hands this allows a lot of the forearm muscles to relax a little bit so that you can more freely rotate it and when the forearm muscles relax you can actually move your arm in a little bit more of a variet of different positions comfortably because you're not restricted by the elbow and the forearm Motion in addition unwrapping the thumb allows you to move the handle upward which actually moves the line of force closer to all these joints meaning that there's less complication at the hand and less overall force at the wrist so in essence using a thumbless grip will simply just free up a lot of motion around the forearm and it'll put less Force Through the forearm and the wrist so that you can free up motion to pull your arm wherever you want [Music]
