# Phil heath's worst exercise choice

<https://www.youtube.com/shorts/Kfqwv_VAiMY>

This right here, my friends, is a classic case of trying to do everything and unfortunately doing nothing all at the same time. Is it a curl? Is it a shoulder press? Is it for the biceps? Is it for the delts? I don't know. Is it for something that is supposed to be for the core? Like, is it for the abs? Is it for the erectors? Is it for the ever mysterious oblique sling? Whatever it is, doesn't seem like it's accomplishing very much. If you wanted to train your abs, then do an ab exercise. If you want to train your shoulders, do a shoulder exercise. And if you want to train your biceps, do a biceps exercise.
