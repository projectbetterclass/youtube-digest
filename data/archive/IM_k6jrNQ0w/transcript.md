# Why do bodybuilders do half reps?

Why is it that so many of the greatest bodybuilders like Ronnie Coleman and Dexter Jackson and Phil Heath and Jay Cutler all use half reps when they train their chest? Well, it's actually for a very specific reason related to the biomechanics of the chest. All the different parts of the chest attached to this bone called the humorris. In other words, the chest uses the upper arm to push the load upward. And whenever you're doing a freeway press and even most machine presses, the line of force goes like this downward through the arm. Now the question is why are they only really using the bottom half of this range of motion? Well, in the bottom portion of the range of motion, notice that this upper armbbone is basically pushing this force direction up directly. But when the individual gets closer to the top, do you see how this upper armbbone is actually moving inward? Because this upper arm is moving inward toward the top, it can't act as directly against this force. In other words, the pecs start to lose leverage to move the weight upward. And in any press, whether it's barbell or machine, what happens is the elbow and the triceps start to become loaded more toward the lockout. And this is why all the best powerliffters in the world really emphasize training their triceps to get better at this lockout. So basically, the chest is most active in this bottom half of the range. And the triceps start to become more active toward the top. But keeping the range of motion just to this bottom half of the range really emphasizes the pecs and keeps tension on them the entire range of motion. Now, of course, there's nothing wrong with locking out because in certain instances, that can actually be better for the chest. For example, if you had a cable and the cable was angled outward, this is still creating resistance on the chest because it's pulling the arm outward. Whereas with a free weight, the weight would be stacked over top of the joint and much of the top half of the range of motion with a free weight ends up being more triceps dominant.
