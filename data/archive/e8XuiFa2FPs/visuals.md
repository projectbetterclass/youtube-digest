## 12. [06:44] Key takeaway slide about Robinhood's business model and high-frequency trading relationships

Robinhood is marketed as a commission-free stock trading product but makes a surprising percentage of their revenue directly from high-frequency trading firms. High-frequency trading firms are paying Robinhood over 10 times as much as they pay to other discount brokerages for the same volume. Robinhood needs to be more transparent about their business model.

## 13. [07:49] Educational explanation of NBBO (National Best Bid and Offer) and SEC Regulation NMS

The National Best Bid and Offer (NBBO) is a quote that reports the highest bid price and lowest ask (offered) price in a security. The Securities Exchange Commission's (SEC) Regulation NMS requires brokers to trade at the best available ask and bid price.
