# Training Triceps On The Pulldown?

<https://www.youtube.com/shorts/C6lwFr_oOXQ>

a lot of people use this machine to do pull downs but you can also use it for an excellent triceps press all you need to do is attach individual D handles to either side grab both of them lean forward so that the cables are scrubbing right along the sides of your neck and press against the cables like you're doing a diamond push-up this is a really great exercise to load the triceps in a relatively more stretched position as compared to a traditional cable push down and I would definitely recommend adding it to your arsenal of triceps exercises if you've never done it before
