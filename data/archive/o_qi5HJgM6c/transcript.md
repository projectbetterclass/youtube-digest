# DOMS Explained

<https://www.youtube.com/shorts/o_qi5HJgM6c>

I've never heard of a great explanation of doms, but this is what I think. When I was younger, I was in chemistry class. I remember like I spilled some like lowgrade acid on my hand and I just washed it under water and later on that day it started to burn more and more and more and then I was like Jesus, what's going on? I found out I had a chemical burn and the way you actually treat it is you put something alkaline on it so you neutralize the acid. When I think of delayed onset of muscle soreness, what it really is is this lactic acid building up between the muscle and the fascia and essentially it's not an instant attack. It's a delayed response. DOMS is a chemical burn fundamentally and that's what that soreness kind of builds up in your muscle. And so the solution is that over time your body gets better at handling that acid tolerance [music] and then you don't get sore anymore. You just train.
