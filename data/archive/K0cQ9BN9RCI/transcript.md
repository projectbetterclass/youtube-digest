# BIG OLE BICEPS

<https://www.youtube.com/shorts/K0cQ9BN9RCI>

here's the brutal bicep pairing that I used to work my arms last training session start out with a low cable curl facing the cables with the shoulder slightly flexed keep the upper arm in position then bring that forearm to your bicep do 8 to 10 reps with 1 to 2 second isometric stopping one to two reps before failure once you hit that point immediately flip around and do face away cable curls we just worked the biceps in the mid short position and now we're finishing them off in the lengthened go to failure on these and you really don't need more than one to two rounds of this to fully finish off the biceps I only did one working set of these and that was plenty for me as always make sure you like and follow for more efficient training tips
