# Your program might be backwards

<https://www.youtube.com/shorts/7LJGCqyaf44>

Having worked with hundreds of people over the years, what I noticed is that most people are getting their programming backwards. Now, here's what I mean. Most people think about their program and their split first. They think, "What program is optimal and what should I do?" And then they think to themselves, "Oh, how can I organize my life and my exercises around the program or split?" In reality, this is totally backwards. What you need to do is ask yourself these questions over here first. What does my life allow me to do in the gym? How many days a week can I go? These types of these types of questions. What things do I enjoy? What exercises actually work for me? What exercises feel good on my body? How often can I do those exercises? How much effort can I put into those exercises? And then form the split this way. Not in this direction, but in this direction. What happens over time is this actually becomes a loop [snorts] where the more that you operate around your life and the exercises and what actually is happening in the exercises and whether or not they feel good or bad and whether or not you're progressing in them is you adjust the split of the program to meet these things over here. This emerges from this. This does not emerge from this. If you try to basically fit your splitter or your program and squish it into your life to make it work in your life and to do exercises that other people tell you to do rather than the ones that actually work for you, you're never actually going to be able to create a program that is individual customized to you and that actually works for you over the long term. So start asking yourself these questions first and let the split come out of these things naturally, not the other way around. >> [music]
