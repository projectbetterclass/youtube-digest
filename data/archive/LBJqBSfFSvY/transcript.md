# Should you round your back like this?

<https://www.youtube.com/shorts/LBJqBSfFSvY>

Did you know that if you started doing three to five sets of 10 to 12 reps of the seated good morning just once a week, beginning with body weight and gradually progressing to dumbbells, a barbell, and finally a weighted barbell over the course of weeks or months, you will dramatically strengthen your lower back? This is a perfectly fine way to load the erector muscles, the muscles that sit on the back side of the spine. A lot of people commented on this video and basically said that your back is going to explode if you do this. You're going to get hurt, injured, be in pain doing it. The reality is that we adapt to stresses placed on our bodies. So, if you want to make your back more resilient to different kinds of stresses, you need to put your back into different positions under different kinds of loading. So, if you want to load your erectors, grow your erectors, and make them stronger, this is a fine option. I personally prefer other options like back extensions off of the hyperextension machine or something kind of like a Jefferson curl where you're allowing your back to round over in an RDL. In any case, you're not going to make your back more resilient to being in the bent position if you never enter and train the bent position.
