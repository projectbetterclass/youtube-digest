# Tips for rear delts

<https://www.youtube.com/shorts/5NQULmPco1g>

Want big rear delts, but only feel your traps working? Then try out this tip. When initiating the movement, I like to think about keeping my arms long and having my upper back rounded. I found that this helps me lessen trap engagement a bit and better isolates the rear delts. And I would also recommend including a variation or making this your staple variation that loads the rear delts in a more lengthened position. To do so, set your cable so that it forms around a 90° angle with the forearm when the arm is across the body like so.
