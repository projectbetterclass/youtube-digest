So, the really scary thing about schizophrenia is that people struggle with a lot of a-motivation over time. The illness like really messes with the basic drive to do things. So, one of the scary things is that schizophrenia has a very high morbidity and mortality impact, reduces life expectancy by quite a bit. What a lot of people think is like, "Okay, like why do people with schizophrenia have a lower life expectancy?" It's not because they hallucinate and will jump out of windows or things like that.
