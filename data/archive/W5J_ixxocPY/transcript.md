# Why would you toss weights around like this?

<https://www.youtube.com/shorts/W5J_ixxocPY>

why is it that a lot of bodybuilders do front raises like this you hold it at your side it's really really light and then when you raise it up it's really really heavy cuz the weight is far from your shoulder so if you do it slow and controlled it's going to be really difficult where you're very weak because you're much stronger at the bottom of the range and as these muscles shorten they lose their ability to produce as much tension so by slightly swinging the weight and trying to move it quickly out of the bottom you're magnifying the forces in the bottom and you're lightening the load toward the top in other words the motion becomes more matched to your strengths in a subtle and control acceleration out of the bottom is a great way to match the strength of your muscles against the resistance of the load so that there's more of a full range challenge in the motion
