# Palantir's Worlds Food Programme Partnership

<https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=3ot4iPcT21I>

palantir technologies and the united nations world food program announced in 2019 a five-year partnership the mission was aimed at helping the world food program use its data to streamline the delivery of food and cash-based assistance in life-saving emergency relief operations worldwide the world food program currently uses palantir's foundry software platform to bring together data sources from across the organization enabling staff at headquarters and in field locations to access and analyze operational data in a secure unified environment by connecting proven methods of delivering food assistance with dynamic data the world food program has been able to reduce cost of operations while continuing to serve its beneficiaries the sheer scale of world food programs operations assisting some 90 million people in about 80 countries means that even small efficiencies in operational and supply chain management can lead to dramatic savings the world food program generates vast amounts of data through the purchasing of 3 million metric tons of food every year and the delivery of 15 billion rations across dozens of development projects worldwide making this data accessible across the organization helps the world food program become even more efficient in multiple program areas including cash-based transfers supply chain optimization and nutritional requirements the partnership with palletier underlines world food program strong commitment to digital transformation as it strives to meet the goal of ending world hunger by twenty thirty building on palantir's world-class data integration technology the world food program develops new analytical tools to seize digital opportunities improve real-time decision-making and enhance global operations alex carp pallentier technologies co-founder and ceo said we strongly believe in the world food program's mission and its leadership and are so pleased that our software will be deployed across the organization together we are working to help deliver more food to more people who need it without increasing costs the partnership builds on an initial pilot project between palantir and wfp which supported the world food program supply chain optimization tool called optimus this award-winning application pulls together different data sets and visualizes the nutritional values sourcing locations delivery times and costs of various foods allowing users to compare all options simultaneously for better decision making optimus has saved the world food program huge amounts in operations it is predicted to have saved the wfp 100 million dollars per year since rollout in iraq for example it helped reduce food basket costs by more than 10 percent by making small changes such as swapping out one commodity for something similar or changing procurement sources without reducing nutritional values by making operations more efficient and effective the world food program can make the most of every dollar spent
