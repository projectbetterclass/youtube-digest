# Reentering the Workforce Does NOT Mean Starting Over

<https://www.youtube.com/shorts/WHtSlLF2vhQ>

Johanna asks, "How do I reenter the workforce after being a stay-at-home mom?" I would really just think about it like how do I reenter the workforce after not having worked for a period of time. I think something really cool is being able to say how much you learned that can help inside of work when you were not working. You can talk about all the skills that you accumulated, but then talk about what lessons you learned in the time that you were not working that you wouldn't have gotten during work that you can now bring back into the workplace that make you excited to be there again. Tell me all the things you learned when you weren't working that will now help going back into work.
