# How I manage elbow pain in my training

<https://www.youtube.com/shorts/b4SK-LlQ6LQ>

Between lifting and Muay Thai, my elbows can get pretty beat up, even more so when I'm prioritizing my tricep growth. Here's how I manage elbow pain while trying to balance all of my training. The first thing I'll do is see if I can swap exercises for something that I can do with minimal or no discomfort. This means finding an exercise that aligns better with my elbow. For example, if easy bar push-downs are painful, then I may swap [music] for a long rope. If that's still very uncomfortable, then I'll swap for a unilateral extension. Next, I'll adjust loading and tempo. Perhaps I'll go slightly higher reps from [music] six to 10 to eight to 12. If needed, I'll slow the eccentric and add a pause in the shortened position. From there, I'll reduce volume while maintaining frequency. If I was doing three sets a session, I'll go down to [music] one to two sets to help maintain while I recover. At the start, when the pain is worse, I may lower frequency from two sessions a week to one session, then build back up as pain lessens. Once I'm back to 100%, I'll reassess my programming to reduce the risk of it happening again. When I first started lifting, this is something that would happen to me very often. Now that I have a better understanding of programming and what people can actually recover from week to week, it doesn't happen too often.
