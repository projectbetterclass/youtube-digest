I honestly just feel sorry for anyone who's still following these Athlean-X workouts in 2026. I promise you don't need an entire dedicated shoulder day with 17 sets to failure plus whatever this finisher is. If you're truly training hard and progressively, that's probably more like 2 weeks worth of volume at most. For me personally, it's closer to a month. Now, you don't have to go as low volume as me, but your shoulders don't require a ton of direct sets since the front and rear heads already get a lot of stimulus during compound chest and back work, and one exercise per head is plenty. That's also why you don't need an entire workout for them. You can easily plug your shoulder work into a full body day, upper day, or push day and be way more efficient with your training. And lastly, no, this is not the best overhead press variation because of safe biomechanical stacking. That's really just a made-up term. Your joints don't need to be perfectly aligned at the top, and all you're doing is making a lift as unstable as possible. And combined with the alternating reps, which drag the set out for twice as long, it'll be harder to get maximum output from your delts and push them close to actual failure.
