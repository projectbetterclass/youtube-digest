# How does bench angle affect muscle activation?

<https://www.youtube.com/shorts/ltF3di76d2I>

what is changing the bench angle of a press impact muscle recruitment a simple way to think about this is that the lower the angle the lower the recruitment on the front of the body and the higher the angle the higher the recruitment on the front side of the body here's how this works changing the bench angle is just changing the way that the dumbbell or the barbell interacts with our body so if you're pressing on a totally flat bench angle and the arm moves fairly horizontally the muscles that act more horizontally like the middle and lower packs as well as the front delt will do the majority of the work but as the bench angle starts to Incline effectively the arm path just changes from being slight more low to slightly more high so as we move from this more horizontal path to this slightly more vertical path we'll get less recruitment in the lower and the middle pecs and more in the upper half of the middle pecs and the upper PEC if you continue to increase the bench angle what that means is that you continue to make the Press more vertical and as a consequence of this if you're doing a totally Vertical Press you're effectively not going to be recruiting any of the PEC significantly and rather mostly just the front delt and the traps to be able to press the arm up toward the ceiling
