# How to target your chest with dips

<https://www.youtube.com/shorts/k6lOCfyeG_E>

here's a really simple way that you can further bias the chest specifically the lower portions of the chest as opposed to the triceps in something like a dip all you need is a bench or something to put your feet on behind you so that you can use the bench to angle your torso forward when your torso is angled more forward and you're less upright you'll be able to better bias the chest as opposed to the triceps and any dip variation you do
