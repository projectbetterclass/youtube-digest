# Exercise Tutorial: Incline Smith Machine Press

<https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=tLB1XtM21Fk>

here's a four-step guide on how to set up a Smith incline press step number one put the bar at around waist height and drag a bench over and underneath the bar around 30° of incline step number two lie down on the bench and use your back to pull your arms down as far as you go before you grab the bar step number three adjust around the bar so that your wrist and forearm are stacked directly underneath of the bar step number four unrack the bar and begin to press if your goals are purely PEC and shoulder related i' suggest using only the bottom 60 to 70% of the motion where the shoulders will be much more involved and where the elbows will be proportionately less involved there are three common mistakes that people make when executing this motion mistake number one starting the Press from the top position as opposed to the bottom one of the biggest benefits of using the smith machine is that it's adjustable in terms of its start and stop position when people set up at the top of the motion they often neglect to realize that they're misaligned toward the bottom since the bottom is arguably the most important part of the motion you want to make sure that that's the portion that you're appropriately aligning and optimizing for mistake number two overemphasizing lockout when people overemphasize lockout they often miss out on a ton of stimulus toward the bottom of the motion this can also lead to an excessive amount of triceps fatigue if that's not your goal and the occasional overr rounding of the shoulders toward the top mistake number three letting the bar rest on your chest and or bouncing it off without control because the bottom is the mechanically most challenging motion we want to be sure that we're spending an extra amount of time there and that we're controlling motion of the bar all the way down to the bottom
