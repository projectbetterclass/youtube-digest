Dips really suck because they put your shoulder kind of in this forward tip position and that's just bad for your shoulder. Well, that's what a lot of people say and frankly they're just wrong because dips and any other motion aren't inherently wrong, but whether or not they feel good or bad for you is really just dependent on your sensitivity and whether or not you're prepared for the exercise. So rather than blaming the dip exercise, try to find a way to do dips for you that right now feels good. The favorite way to start people was by putting a bench behind the dip station so they have a foot supported so that they can tip their torso forward. This makes it a bit easier to press more toward the lower chest which I think tends to feel better on average for a lot of my clients. And then as the arm moves upward and backward, the shoulder blade is kind of able to move back a little bit more freely whereas here it tends to be the case that the shoulder blade wants to tip forward more which again isn't bad but a lot of people just aren't used to that tipping.
