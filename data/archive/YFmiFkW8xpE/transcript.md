# The 3 Ways to Start a Business (And Which Makes the Most Money)

<https://www.youtube.com/shorts/YFmiFkW8xpE>

There's three options for starting a business. Solve a problem better than other people. That's execution risk. Second, solve a problem that no one knew existed. Oh, everyone wants to be connected online, aka Facebook. That's product market risk. Third, solve a problem everyone knows about, but is hard to solve. If I said, "Hey, I can have a machine that can take sales calls for you." I don't need to like get you to believe that's valuable. They're just like, "I don't believe you can do it." That's technical risk. The surest money is in the first one. The biggest money is in the second and third.
