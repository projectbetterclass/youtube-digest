# THIS MADE MY CHEST TOO BIG ☹️

<https://www.youtube.com/shorts/7EDrtw9q-Fs>

If you want a chest bigger than the interest on your car loan, then you came to the right place. Had to make do in my apartment gym for this session, so I was stacking out this machine with the help of the gym pin as well, adding a little bit more weight, and working anywhere in the 8 to 12 rep range just so that I could reach failure. For those of you in fully equipped gym, you could stick to my typical rep range of 5 to 8 reps. Pay attention to the control that I'm using throughout the eccentric. This ensures that I'm going to find my end range and get that full stretch on the pecs. Control on the way down and explode on the way up. For my second movement, I did a mid-pec cable press to work more in that mid to short range. Again, working around that 8 to 12 rep range, but I would normally do these anywhere from 6 to 10 reps. For triceps, I did one of my favorite supersets here, starting with a pushdown and then leading into an overhead extension to focus on the medial and lateral head. These I was able to load heavy enough to do 6 to 8 reps with the pushdown, and for my overhead extensions, I got around 3 to 5 reps to failure. To finish off the pecs and the triceps, I did a mechanical drop set with dips, starting more bent over, which is going to involve more of the pecs, and then going upright for more triceps.
