What do you think about before lifting heavy weights? >> I used to create scenarios in my head that would just get me to experience these insane emotions, but now I've gotten a lot better at just identifying with that emotion, whatever that is, and almost kind of removing the emotion piece out of it and just going with a feeling. I guess if I was going to explain it, it'd be like I just get these butterflies. I feel like I'm going to throw up. And at that point, I know I'm ready to go.
