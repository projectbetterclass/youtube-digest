# A Lateral Raise Hack You've Never Used!

<https://www.youtube.com/shorts/B18eY64_a08>

I don't know about you, but whenever I use a lateral raise machine, I absolutely hate grabbing the handle specifically because the thumb is the thing that is getting bent. And if you're using a lot of load, it could be annoying. So, here's two solutions. One, and this is my preferred method. Just use a cuff and loop the little rings of the cuff around the handle. And boom, all of a sudden, now you're just doing a lateral raise with the cuff on a machine. Option number two, just grab a D- handle, loop the D- handle around the back of your hand, and then loop the other side of the D- handle around the machine. Will this make your gains crazy better? Will you all of a sudden just explode in muscular growth? No. But these little tweaks can make a big difference and go a long way the longer term.
