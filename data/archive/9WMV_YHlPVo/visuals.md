## 1. [02:12] Slide explaining Bull vs. Bear Market definitions

Bull vs. Bear Market
• Bull Market: a period of upward trending stock prices of 20% off the most recent market bottom
• Bear Market: a drop of more than 20% from previous high
• A bull market is a metaphor for how a bull attacks: it throws its horns upward
• A bear market is named after a bear that swipes downward

## 5. [11:58] Slide with reasons why waiting for a market correction is not a good investment strategy

Why Not Wait for a Correction?
• Trying to time the market is a bad idea
• All time highs are quite common
• Since 1950 the U.S. market has set 1,250 all time highs
• Average = 16 times per year!
• Not investing during a market high = missing out on a lot of opportunity
