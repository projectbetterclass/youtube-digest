# How to isolate rear delts

<https://www.youtube.com/shorts/nMhDIKC0cgU>

how do we Target and train the rear delts as opposed to the other parts of the back the rear delts attach from the back of the upper arm to different parts of the shoulder blade because it attaches directly to the shoulder blade it doesn't move the whole shoulder girdle back and forth or up and down what this means is that Motions like reverse flies that mostly just involve motion of the arm are going to be much more rear delt dominant so all of your normal rows and pull Downs will still Target some rear delts so to isolate more the rear delt reverse fly type motions at an angle of high to low and slightly low to high will be best for targeting it the rear delts are of course involved in any kind of rowing motion or any kind of pull down motion but the lats are heavily involved in those as well but if you really want to isolate them do reverse flies
