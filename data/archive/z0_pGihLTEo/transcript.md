# How to ACTUALLY make small talk! 👀

<https://www.youtube.com/shorts/z0_pGihLTEo>

Here's where most people run dry. They ask, "So, what do you do? And how was your weekend?" And then they run out of ammo. So, I keep these five areas in life in my back pocket. Five places that almost always reveals who somebody actually is. And they all start with P's. With P number one, talk about the past. Their childhood memories, the school they went to, what they wanted to be when they were little. P number two, the patterns that exist in their life. The habits, the good ones and the not so good ones, and maybe even their daily routine. P number three, their preferences. What do they love? What can't they stand? What's the unpopular opinions that they have? P number four, this is their pet peeves. The tiny little things that piss them off and irritate them way more than they actually should. P number five, possibilities. What do they want to try, learn, or experience one day? And adding a sixth because I can, ask them about their preams. Some people do call them dreams, but that's for ploosers. What do they pream about? Ask them. And if you want to share, maybe you could even share one of your preams. Don't march through all six of these questions in six of these areas like a checklist. You reach for whichever one fits into the conversation you're having. Now, these six areas, they do one job. They move the conversation from small talk into a conversation that has way more substance.
