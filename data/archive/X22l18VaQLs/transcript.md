# Stop doing skull crushers! (Here’s why)

<https://www.youtube.com/shorts/X22l18VaQLs>

why is it that your elbows hurt when doing barbell or dumbbell Skull Crushers when we take a look at the anatomy of the elbow in relationship to a traditional skull crusher position most people are actually much more comfortable with an elbow position that directs their forearm across their body as opposed to the super externally rotated position which directs their hands behind their head whenever you're using a barbell your hands are fixed to a bar so you're kind of forced to twist yourself this way but oftentimes people's shoulders end up guiding them a little bit out of alignment with the load as a consequence of this people end up getting a little bit of a twisting force in their elbow and potentially their forearm which creates some measure of discomfort so rather than trying to jam yourself and force yourself into this really unnatural position use a single dumbbell and lay your arm come across your body or simply just use a cable where you can actually direct the load wherever you want to in addition I would advise you to stay away from any exercise that really forces you to fix your hands to any Implement because that's not going to allow for the freedom of motion at the elbow that the elbow needs to be able to actually be loaded in the direction that it moves
