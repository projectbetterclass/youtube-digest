# 10 Muscle Building Hot Takes

10 muscle building hot takes that also happen to be true. Number one, lower [music] reps generally are superior to higher reps overall. You get the same growth stimulus, but they're less fatiguing overall, easier to push close to failure on, and more time efficient. Number two, feeling the muscle is hugely overrated. A set can still be highly effective even when the target muscle doesn't produce a strong local sensation. Number three, you don't always have to lift through a full range of motion. It depends on the goal of the exercise and your individual mobility. This might be appropriate in one case while this might be totally fine in another. Number four, squats are not a must to build big legs. They're a great exercise. Do them if you can, but you can also build very impressive quads with leg presses and leg extensions only. Number five, you can get great results with a bros split. Hitting each muscle once a week may not be optimal, but the long-term difference versus higher frequencies isn't as dramatic as people make it seem. Number six, the pump feels satisfying, but means almost nothing on its own. Any repetitive movement will push blood into the muscle, and you can get a huge pump while leaving 10 reps or more in the tank and stimulating little to no growth. Number seven, free weights are not necessary to maximize your gains. They're obviously great tools, but muscles respond to tension and don't know or care whether it's coming from a dumbbell, cable, or machine. Number eight, doing as much volume as you can recover from doesn't make sense. What you can simply recover from versus what will actually drive the fastest overall gains are not the same thing. Number nine, if you need regular D losses, your program probably sucks. A well-designed hypertrophy routine should manage training stress well enough that you're not routinely forced to back off. And number 10, building muscle isn't actually that hard. Once you're past the initial newbie stage, training and nutrition are pretty routine, and it's mostly just a game of patience.
