# DO THESE PULL-DOWNS!

<https://www.youtube.com/shorts/hrle0VY-taE>

is a simple guide on how to Target different portions of your back with different variations of a pull down if you want to Target your lats the most what I would do is take a chin up grip so your palms are facing your face lean back just a little bit and keep your arms narrow to your body make sure that your arms are basically brushing against your sides as you come up and down and that you pull down toward your back pockets if you want to Target more of your rear delts what you'll do is you'll take a little bit of a wider grip put your palms facing away from you on the bar don't pull as far down as you possibly can but rather just move as much as you can without your shoulder blade moving too much if you want to Target your traps and rhomboids a little bit more what you can do is keep the same grip orientation as the rear delt variation but now instead try to maximize the amount that your shoulder blades are moving with your chest slightly pulled up all these variations will obviously have lots of overlap in many ways but the way that you execute them specifically will determine which specific muscle group is targeted most if you like this video please follow for more
