# STOP doing hanging leg raises (do these instead)

<https://www.youtube.com/shorts/1GR47ryAVxE>

traditional hanging leg raises are an overrated exercise because of how difficult they are to coordinate how little stability we have and as a consequence we usually end up turning it into a hip flexor exercise and a shoulder exercise more than anything else to improve it put a barbell into a rack put padding on top of the barbell to surface cushioning and then use it as an anchor to articulate your entire spine around what this gives you is it gives you an ability to actually shove into something so that you can just focus on training the ABS and in addition when you lean back this way you make the resistance profile a little bit more favorable toward the strength of the ABS give this variation a try and it'll feel much more abdominal
