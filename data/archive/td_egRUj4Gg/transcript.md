# Never Buy a Stock Unless...

<https://www.youtube.com/shorts/td_egRUj4Gg>

Like I was saying, you should never buy a stock unless you have on your desk written out your criteria for buying stocks. And I would very much write down next to it why that particular stock matches those criteria, and keep that record. The second thing you want to do is write out before you buy it where you're going to sell it, either for a small loss or for a gain. You want to cover all those make all those decisions while you're calm, while you're not invested, while your money isn't going up or down. And that process in itself will save you from yourself. Cuz ultimately, the biggest enemy of your success as an investor is you.
