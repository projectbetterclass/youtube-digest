# Are these pull-downs bad?

<https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=uxd3Zj7l7Ro>

should you do behind the neck pull Downs like Arnold did or should you just do regular pull Downs instead I don't think the behind the neck pull down is necessarily the best option but let's look at why if we need to force our arms into this backward position as we're attempting to do the pull down we're kind of creating two opposite joint actions and the reason is because when you move your arm backward the traps and the rhomboids need to kind of hold the scapula backward but if your arms are going up and overhead you need to actually allow this scapula to rotate upward you can't keep them backward as you do that if you're shoving your arm backward but you're also allowing a resistance to pull you upward you're creating two opposite actions one of which is pulling the scapula backward and another of which is allowing it to go upward in addition keeping your arms backward will actually take away from your ability to stretch the lats because the lats stretch when the arm is slightly forward and overhead if your goals are lat Focus I would recommend doing a pull down with your arms kind of in front of your body and if your goals are more upper back focused would recommend doing a pull down with your arms slightly out to your sides but not behind your neck in addition if you really wanted to train your traps well I would recommend rows in a little bit of a low to high direction as opposed to pull Downs altogether
