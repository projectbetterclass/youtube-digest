The Story
Like other brick and mortar companies, GameStop struggled with the COVID shutdown, with a surge in online sales not offsetting the lost sales from closed stores. However, the company's troubles predate 2020, with revenues dropping and stores being closed for much of the prior five years, and operating margins dropping down to close to nothing. In thia valuation, I look at a an upbeat and perhaps close to best-case valuation of the company, with sales recovering quickly in 2021 to close to 2019 levels, and subsequent growth primarily in online sales more than making up for fewer stores.
