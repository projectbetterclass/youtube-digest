# Why do people lean during lateral raises?

<https://www.youtube.com/shorts/r2zNOSR5oYE>

why do people like to lean over like this whenever they're doing lateral raises if you're holding a weight in your hand and you're going to shove it out to the side that weight is going to try to push your body in the opposite direction if you're in this regular standing position and you don't have a brace on the opposite side of your body it can feel kind of awkward to get the load moving but if you grab something near you when you lean into that side often times it feels more stable to initiate the motion because your body weight is counterbalancing the force that would otherwise push you that way now leaning over is a perfectly viable option but you can also simply just use your opposite arm to brace up against something and raise the cable up Ward closer to the height of your hand so that you can load the bottom position in a highly stable environment
