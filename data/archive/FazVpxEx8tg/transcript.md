This is a fundamental shift that most people completely miss. For decades, we organize information the way humans think about it. It doesn't care about your folder structure or categories. It looks for patterns and meanings across everything at once. A traditional database is like a filing cabinet organized alphabetically. AI needs something more like a brain that understands connections between ideas. This matters because agents aren't just looking up information occasionally like we do. They're constantly processing data to make decisions. An AI helping with drug research needs to scan through millions of scientific papers, chemical structures, and test results simultaneously. An AI managing a factory needs to process sensor readings, quality reports, and historical data in real time. You can't do that with old database technology. It's like trying to watch a movie using a filing cabinet. The tool just wasn't built for that purpose. The infrastructure has to be rebuilt from the ground up for how AI actually works.
