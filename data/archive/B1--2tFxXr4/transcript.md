# HOW TO Cable Hammer Curl

<https://www.youtube.com/shorts/B1--2tFxXr4>

The cable hammer curl is the best way to develop your forearms. Most people do it wrong. I'll show you how to do it right. Step number one is grip. When you grab the weight, take your thumbs and place it on top of the hammer curl. Step number two is lean forward so that you place tension on your biceps for the whole duration. Last step, take it to sets failure. Make sure that you can no longer bend your elbows. Flexes make your arms look way bigger. Save this if you want giant arms.
