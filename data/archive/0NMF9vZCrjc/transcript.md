All right, one exercise for the brachialis, which one would you choose? So, I would say that you would probably want to choose something where the shoulder and the elbow are moving at the same time because it is a single joint muscle, the biceps crossing the shoulder will be less involved if the shoulder is extending at the same time that the elbow is bending. So, you're probably going to want to pick something like a rope curl where the shoulder and the arms are moving or just a single arm cable curl. Uh again, basically where the shoulder is moving back as the elbow is moving at the same time.
