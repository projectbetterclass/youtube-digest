Okay, everyone. I'm sorry to inform you, but if you are stacking plates like this on top of your knees in a wall sit, you are not actually adding load really to the exercise. You're not really making it more difficult. And this is because of the specific physics of the wall sit. So, a lot of people think the force of a wall sit is basically just coming through the ground like this as if you're doing some kind of traditional squat. But in reality, what's happening is you actually have to push into the ground kind of downward and forward, almost like you're sliding a carpet forward underneath of your feet. And the reason you need to kind of slide your feet this way is because you need to actually push yourself backward into the wall. So, if I were to describe a primary force of this exercise, it would actually be the floor kind of shoving your feet in this direction, against which you actually kind of have to perform a leg extension. So, the quad muscles are actually really the muscles that are going to be targeted with this movement. So, funny enough, what actually happens when you add weights a lot of the time is it actually increases the pressure that you're able to add through your feet. And so, your feet actually don't want to slide very much forward. So, in some cases, adding weight can actually make the exercise easier if it's over top of your knees.
