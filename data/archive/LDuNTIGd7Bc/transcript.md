# the coolest dumbbell ever

This is the most unique kind of dumbbell that you've probably never even seen or used before. So, what actually makes it different from a biomechanics perspective? So, as you can see, you just grab it like a normal dumbbell, but you got to flip it around so that the pad on the dumbbell actually falls on top of your wrist. And this makes it so that the force is actually applied here above the wrist and fingers as opposed to here like a normal dumbbell. So, unlike a normal dumbbell raise, which pulls your wrist down and which requires you to hold on to the load, force of this dumbbell is actually uniquely applied above the wrist. And in my personal experience, this feels way better when it comes to lateral raises. Why? Well, just because less of this stuff needs to be involved. So, you can just focus on the middle delt. Now, because very few gyms actually have these dumbbells, a simple way you can mimic this is just by using a cuff on a cable lateral raise or looping the webbing of a D handle over top of your hand or wrist on a lateral raise, too. All these strategies are similar because they just require less of your forearms and your fingers to do work, which can in some instances feel better on your shoulder and more local to the actual stimulus you're trying to get.
