# It’s funny that leg drive was mentioned when you use your legs much more standing than seated…

<https://www.youtube.com/shorts/iBlUJCSLf40>

It always baffles me when people say things with such confidence when they are completely wrong. Standing military press is the most honest shoulder exercise in the gym. No seat to lean back on, no leg drive, just moving the bar. Here's the thing. If we compare a standing overhead shoulder press to a seated shoulder press, it is much easier to cheat, use other body parts during a standing press than it is a seated press. A hill that I'm willing to die on is that the seated shoulder press is so much better than the standing shoulder press for hypertrophy that there's really no reason to do a standing shoulder press unless it's just your preference. With a seated shoulder press, the bracing is going to be much better compared to a standing overhead press. You don't have to worry about your lower back. You don't have to worry about your core as a whole. All you have to worry about is just pressing overhead and coordinating the dumbbells or the barbell. If you're using a Smith machine, even less for you to worry about. Careful where you get your information from. Hope this helps.
