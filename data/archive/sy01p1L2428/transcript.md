# HOW TO Cable Pullovers

<https://www.youtube.com/shorts/sy01p1L2428>

Cable pullovers are the best way to grow your lats. Most people do it wrong. This is how you do it right. Step number one, you want to deep stretch through your lats. What I want you to do is make sure that your elbows are near your ears. Step number two will be [music] to internally rotate the elbows first and then start to push down. Step number three, keep your arms long and when you finish the movement, lift your chest through the movement. Lastly, make sure you do it until you cannot even initiate a rep.
