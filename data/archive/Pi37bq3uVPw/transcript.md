# Why does the thumbless grip work so well?

<https://www.youtube.com/shorts/Pi37bq3uVPw>

Why do so many people love to use the thumbless grip whenever they're doing pulling motions compared to grips where the thumb is wrapped around? There are two primary reasons for this. Reason number one, when you fully wrap your thumb around a bar or handle, it actually prevents your hand from twisting. But when you unwrap the thumb, your forearm gains an ability to rotate to a greater degree. In other words, when you unwrap the thumb, you can pull more freely in a direction that's comfortable and appropriate for you. Reason number two, when you wrap the thumb around the bar or handle, the tendency is to put the handle deep into the hand so that you can squeeze it. But when you unwrap the thumb, the natural tendency is actually to allow the bar to drift a little bit higher up in the hand. And when this happens, the fingers actually open up. Now, surprisingly, the looser grip is actually a little bit of a stronger position. And if you don't believe me, just do a test for yourself. Grab a pair of dumbbells and hold them deep in your palm as you squeeze them. and then unwrap your thumb, try the looser grip, and watch how much easier it is to hold the dumbbells. Now, for these two reasons, the thumbless grip is at least worth a try. And if you don't like it as much, you can simply just go back to whatever you were doing
