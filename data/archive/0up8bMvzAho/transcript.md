So what are the main differences between grabbing here on a lat pulldown bar versus here? Whenever our hands are on the more straight portion of the pulldown, it usually means that the loading is going to be a little bit more narrow, which generally speaking, as you pull down, means a little bit more lat recruitment. As you move your hands outward and as you move toward that angled position, there's going to be a little bit more of a frictional force, which is basically, in short, just going to recruit more of the upper back tissues. You can think of this pulldown more similar to a pull-apart type motion, and then you can think of this motion uh more similar to a pull-up type motion or anything that's just simply more vertically loaded and narrow arm path loaded. So the wider that you move, the more upper back tissue, generally speaking, you're going to recruit, and the more narrow that you move, assuming that you keep your arms in front of your body, the more lats you're going to recruit, and oftentimes more elbow flexors. Either one of these grips is good or bad, but rather more or less appropriate given the context of your goals and what you're trying to do with the exercise.
