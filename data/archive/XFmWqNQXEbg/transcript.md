# Overall looks like a solid glute workout!

<https://www.youtube.com/shorts/XFmWqNQXEbg>

Only five exercises in this glute day, which is pretty promising because a lot of people are doing a bunch of redundant exercises. I like the way this workout is starting because I do like putting hip thrust or glute bridge towards the start of the workout because I think they're one of the best bang for your buck exercises for a glute focused training day because they're not going to fatigue you as quickly as other exercises and the glutes have great leverage in that movement. Two big things here, you want to be using straps in your hinge movements and then if you're using an angled Smith machine, you want that angle going away from you rather than towards you for your hinges. Now, this is not your typical Bulgarian split squat. This is a Bulgarian split squat with rotation. What that rotation is going to do is it's going to stretch the upper fibers of the gluteus max a bit more. The most common mistake I see is people just rotating with the spine, but it should be the hips that are rotating as you're going down. Now, unless it's a gluteus medius kickback, I find that cable kickbacks like this are a bit redundant if you're already doing some kind of glute bridge or hip thrust because it's loading essentially the same thing. While this isn't necessarily bad to do in your glute workout, you could do away with this and just do another set of glute bridges or thrust perhaps. If you do want to include some kickbacks, I would take some of the volume from your thruster bridge and put them towards that. Leaning forward like this is going to train the upper gluteus max a bit more than the gluteus medius. If you want to train the gluteus medius with a hip abduction movement like this, then you want the hip less flexed.
