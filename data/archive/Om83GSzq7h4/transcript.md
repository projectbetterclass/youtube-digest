# Why do bodybuilders do “half reps”?

<https://www.youtube.com/shorts/Om83GSzq7h4>

why did Jay Cutler do half reps whenever he was pressing with free weights well the bottom half of the press is really where the dumbbell has a substantial distance to the shoulder and at the top the dumbbell actually comes over the shoulder where there's a lot less resistance on the PCS so you have much higher tension in the bottom half of the range and that tension drops off toward the top where the weight Stacks over your joint so if you're having trouble connecting with your pecs on any sort of freeway press it's a good idea to stay toward that bottom half of the range where there's all the tension just on the PEC
