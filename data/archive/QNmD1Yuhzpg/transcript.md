You don't ever ever need to do this [ __ ] exercise. Waste of time. Put your hands here. Pull. Squeeze. Neither one of these two pull-downs is necessarily better than the other. They just do different things. When you use a chin-up grip, your arms will naturally come more narrow in front of your body. And when your arms are in front of your body and they come up forward, your lats will be able to contribute to a greater degree in pulling the arm downward. But when you turn your palms outward to do the traditional wider grip lat pull-down, you'll take some advantage away from the lats and you'll shift it toward the upper back muscles in the teres major, the rear delts, and the rhomboids. Pulling a little bit wider will still involve a lot of the lower lats, but proportionately will involve a greater amount of upper back recruitment as compared to the chin-up grip pull-down, which gives more advantage to the lats as compared to the upper back. So, if you want to target more of the lats, do the narrower grip pull-down. If you want to train a little bit more of the upper back, you can do the wide grip pull-down.
