# How to: smith machine side bends

<https://www.youtube.com/shorts/a7c_9bJvhfs>

Two most important things with the side bend. One is having the stance foot close to basically as close as you can get it to the bar. A lot of people are trying to distribute weight over here and they're kind of like awkwardly shifting. You basically only need a single leg to do this exercise. Number one, number two. You want to think about this movement as a rib movement, not a shoulder movement. So, a lot of people when they go down, reaching with their arm and then they're shrugging it, right? That's just a shrug. So, let your arm relax, let it be passive. Open the ribs on the opposite side and then crunch the ribs. So, if I'm going down here, I'm thinking open the right ribs, close the right ribs. I'm not thinking drop the shoulder, raise the shoulder. So, okay if there's a little bit of assistance there, but you want to think about this primarily as ribs, not shoulder.
