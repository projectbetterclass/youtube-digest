# Update- got stronger 👍🏽

<https://www.youtube.com/shorts/CEwAeTRqZqI>

I recorded every single session of barbell RDLs in the last five months. I started out with 315 and [music] worked my way back up. It had been nearly a year since I had had barbell RDLs programmed in part due to me recovering from my pec tear. Believe it or not, there was a point within my training within the first five years that I thought I'd never deadlift or squat heavy again. I had a pretty serious back injury that made it hard even doing day-to-day [music] tasks. I had some numbness going down my left leg and any sort of spinal loading would cause a searing pain. It definitely wasn't the most fun, but I stayed in the gym lifting around my injury as best I could. Eventually, things started feeling better, but I would have some flare-ups here and there and eventually those flare-ups became less and less frequent. Staying active and working around any injuries that I've had in my 15 plus years of training at this point has been one of the best things that I could do for my physical and mental health. If you told me during the time that I was recovering from my back injury that we'd one day be repping near 500 lbs on RDLs again, I probably wouldn't believe you. These sessions on average were around a week apart unless I was traveling or was just busy, in which case it may have been a week and a half between sessions. Hope this helped and stay tuned for finally hit 500 by 5.
