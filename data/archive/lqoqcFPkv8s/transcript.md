# HOW TO Y Raise

<https://www.youtube.com/shorts/lqoqcFPkv8s>

Why raises are the best way to develop your rear delts. Most people do it wrong. This is how you do it right. Step number one, take the seat to a 45° angle. Step number two, place your sternum over the head [music] of the bench. Step number three, raise in a Y shape. The last tip is once you complete the movement to its highest position, take your thumbs and ever so slightly turn them out. You'll feel your rear delt activate a little bit more. The rear delt makes your shoulders look way bigger.
