# This will change your back training forever

I wasn't really ever able to connect with and grow my lats until I learned this one insight. Because for years I was doing everything that everyone told me, right? I was pulling my arm down tight to my body. Like people say, "Keep your arm tight to your ribs." I was driving the elbow down toward the hip. I was doing all of the things and all the exercises that people told me to do to train and target and grow my lats, but they just weren't connecting for me. I would feel things like my shoulder and my rear delt, and never really the meat of this muscle. And what really, really changed that for me was an understanding of the lats biomechanics beyond just what happens with the upper arm moving. What I learned was that the lats play a huge role in shoulder depression. And a quick experiment for you to feel this right now as you're watching is see what it's like to just squeeze your arms into your sides. You'll kind of connect with your lat a little bit. But then watch what happens when you squeeze your arm into your side and then reach your shoulder and your armpit down toward the floor. You'll feel such a distinct contraction in your lats, and that's because the lats are really powerful shoulder depressor. They don't just move the upper arm. So in order to really, really isolate your lats on any kind of pull down or row, make sure that you're not just thinking about your upper arm motion and your elbow moving toward your hip, but also that you're driving your armpit down toward the floor as you pull.
