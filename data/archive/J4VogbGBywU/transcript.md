# Instantly improve your cable curl with this tip!

<https://www.youtube.com/shorts/J4VogbGBywU>

Cable curls are a great exercise, but here's one quick way that you can make them much better. Instead of just standing next to the stack or even in front of it unsupported, lean up against the cable column and put the back of your upper arm there so that as you do the curl, the whole shoulder can be supported and you can really really focus just on the biceps and the other elbow flexors as a consequence. When you do this and you lean into the stack, make sure you put your same side foot forward so you can sort of push yourself backward so that you're further supported and don't really have to do any work at all at the shoulder.
