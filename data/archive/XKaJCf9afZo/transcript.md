# Easy squat tips anyone can follow

<https://www.youtube.com/shorts/XKaJCf9afZo>

Can we, as a whole, all stop doing quad bias squats wrong? I see way too many people hopping on the pendulum squat, the hack squat, thinking that they are doing a quad bias squat, but the problem is they put their feet so high up that it no longer is a quad bias squat, or it's not nearly as good of a squat as it should be. This is what you should be looking for when you're trying to set up your foot position. You want your feet low, somewhere around hip width or very slightly outside of that. Feet either pointed forward or slightly out. You don't have to have them pointed way out or externally rotated. You want your knees tracking in line with your toes. You want to be able to achieve that hamstring to calf depth. Now, if you're doing that and you feel your lower back is being pulled up off the pad, your feet are too high. If you're achieving that hamstring to calf depth, your lower back is staying on the pad, but you notice that your shins are moving back towards you at the bottom of the squat, then your feet are still a bit too high. If your knees are moving back towards you at the bottom of the squat, then that is going to lessen the tension on the knees on the quads.
