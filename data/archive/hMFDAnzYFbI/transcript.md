# Standing Calf Raise

<https://www.youtube.com/shorts/hMFDAnzYFbI>

The standing calf raise is the best exercise for training the gastromus. Most people do it wrong. This is how you do it right. Step number one, most people just kind of stand up. What I like to do is round my upper back, stack my rib cage, and tense my abs. [music] This means that I get a lot less strain on my lower back. From that position, I simply [music] squeeze my glutes so my lower back is protected. Step number two, drop until you get a deep stretch in the calf. From here, squeeze the glutes.
