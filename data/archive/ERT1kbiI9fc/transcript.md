# How to train your serratus anterior

<https://www.youtube.com/shorts/ERT1kbiI9fc>

just a simple guide on how to understand the seratus anterior and how to train it the serratus anterior is actually a really big muscle that sits underneath of the shoulder blade alongside the ribs and it spays out through nine different directions in a banana-like fashion its primary functions are to pull the shoulder blade forward and to rotate the shoulder blade upward so what that means is that training it is actually very simple and all the Motions that you're probably already doing like presses whether it's flat decline or incline in addition to lateral raise type motions or Y raise type motions all train the seratus anterior significantly a lot of people tend to try to isolate protraction to train it but in terms of actually integrating ex function along with the upper arm I think it's a much better idea to do pressing motions as well as raising motions to train it pretty directly
