# Do THIS to target your lower pecs!

<https://www.youtube.com/shorts/hsLMUe5quxU>

Here's a simple guide on how to train the lower portions of the pec. Whenever you do an exercise to train the lower pec, you want to make sure that you're using a direction of resistance that is essentially parallel to the direction that the lower pec fibers run. This means picking an exercise that shoves your arm backward and also upward. So, again, your forearm remains parallel to the direction of that resistance. I personally prefer to do presses with cables and machines. With cables, you can be highly specific to the direction of resistance that you want to use. And with machines, you can adjust your body around the machine so that the resistance interacts with you in a very similar way. In either case, you just want to ensure that you're pressing from a slightly high to a slightly low angle, and that you're keeping your arms relatively tighter to your body.
