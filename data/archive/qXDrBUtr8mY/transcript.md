# Elbow flexors

<https://www.youtube.com/shorts/qXDrBUtr8mY>

I am 95% sure that this person is small. And this is just the result of watching too many Tik Toks of science-based lifters who don't know what they're talking about. There is absolutely nothing wrong with programming more than one elbow flexor exercise within a workout. Different elbow flexor exercises bias different elbow flexors. So, there's nothing wrong with doing say a supinated curl to bias the biceps and then doing a reverse curl, which is going to bias the brachioradialis. And no, it's not going to be too much volume because you can very easily just take all the volume that you were going to do for the one elbow flexor exercise and split it between two. If you were going to do three sets, you could do two sets of a supinated curl, one set of a reverse curl, two sets, two sets of each. Totally fine. If you have two or three days between the next time you train elbow flexors, there's absolutely nothing wrong with that. And the thing is, if you can recover from more volume, you're going to make better progress compared to lower volume. The only issue is if you can't recover from the higher volume, then it could affect your progress. Careful who you listen to on here, guys.
