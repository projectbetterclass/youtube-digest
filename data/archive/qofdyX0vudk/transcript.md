The reason I'm separating risk that affects a few companies from risk that affects most companies is here's where we make a distinction: If you're an investor who's diversified, and in corporate finance, all risk-and-return models make the assumption that the marginal investor looking at a company, and we'll come back and define marginal investor in a moment, is a diversified investor.
