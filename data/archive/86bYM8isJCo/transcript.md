# Should you do this on split squats?

<https://www.youtube.com/shorts/86bYM8isJCo>

What the heck is the point of turning into your working leg when you do a split squat? Think about it this way. If you do a split squat and you bring your leg up like this, the part of your butt cheek that's getting the most stretch is the lower portion of the gluteus maximus. However, if you turn your hips into the working leg as you lower, what you're actually doing is pulling the leg across your body. And when you pull your leg up and across the body, the muscle recruitment will shift a little bit from that lower gluteus maximus to the upper parts of the glutes cuz this is the direction that those fibers are now stretching. So, the twisting lunge variation as compared to the straighter lunge variation will target proportionately more of the muscles higher up on the backside of your butt, namely the upper gluteus maximus, the back half of the gluteus medius muscle, as well as the back half of the gluteus minimus muscle. Keep in mind, there's absolutely nothing wrong with a normal split squat or lunge. This is just a great addition and a great variation to add to your training if that's more specific to your goal.
