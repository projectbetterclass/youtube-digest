# How to: Cable Front Raise (Grow your Front Delts FAST)

<https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=Mg8gRq2w99Q>

this is one of my favorite exercises for training the delts the traps and the serratus set this exercise up go over to a cable stack and make the cable origin as low as it possibly can get close to the floor then stand next to it and mimic the arm path that you would use for the exercise the arm path shouldn't be straightforward shouldn't be straight out to the side but rather somewhere in between those two points at a diagonal angle as you grab the cable mimic that same arm path and keep your thumb toward the ceiling throughout the entire range of motion note that the cable basically stays directly underneath of my arm the whole time it's not pulling me outward or toward my body in any sort of different direction cable should essentially be moving exactly parallel to where my arm is moving if you don't find this totally comfortable what you can do is Loop your arm through the handle that you're using and instead anchor the handle slightly above the elbow so that the cable ends up closer to your shoulder this may help some of you who've previously had pain with any sort of raising Motion in addition to being a fantastic exercise to train the delts traps serratus this will be an excellent exercise for any of you that are looking to improve your overall Mobility as well as the general function of your shoulder girdle
