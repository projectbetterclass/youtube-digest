# $69K MRR founder put a rainbow button in his app offering 7 days of free access.

<https://www.youtube.com/shorts/EZ-mpb6b-i8>

The biggest driver of how we ended up building Fast Lane was literally just talking to as many customers as we possibly could. And obviously we're very inspired by figures like Paul Graham and the early YC days who really advocated for this style of building. So, I think since August my co-founder Jock and I have done around 20 calls a week every week. It was really simple. We just had a button in our UI with a rainbow border that said "Book a call with the team if you want 7 days of extra access." And so, people incentivized because they wanted extra access and it gave us a massive leg up because now people are booking calls and we could interview them and ask them all the questions we had ready. So, we just used Calendly, opened up our calendar, and let users click that button. It took them straight to the calendar and they just booked a time. So, we've done 2,000 customer calls since we started customer discovery back then. So, we spent all of our time basically sitting in a room just really harassing people with questions that we had.
