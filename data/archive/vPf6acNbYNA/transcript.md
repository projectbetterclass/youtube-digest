# I LOVE hate comments

<https://www.youtube.com/shorts/vPf6acNbYNA>

As a content creator of 6 years, I absolutely love getting negative comments. I used to hate getting negative comments because they would trigger me, but now I actually love getting them because if I think a comment is negative, it's telling me something important about myself. And as a content creator, I actually think it's very, very important that you use social media as a mirror to understand yourself better. Because typically content creators burn out with exhaustion when they either push away negative comments as if there's no truth to them and they can just ignore them or they really really let it get to them and they start fighting them and they start resisting people who are making negative comments. So if you're a content creator I highly highly recommend whenever you perceive a comment to be negative just ask okay where is this comment true? What is it saying about me? what is it basically affirming that I already believe about myself and then start to actually inspect that belief. So a concrete example for me, I'm someone who was very very sensitive about my physique, the way that I looked and the credibility that it lent to people in the fitness industry. And so whenever anyone would make a negative comment negative about my physique saying that I was small, I would get very very triggered by this. And over time, the more that I actually inspected what was triggering about it, the more I realized, oh, this is just a physical insecurity. Long story short, those comments don't bother me anymore because I've done a lot of inner work on what that actually is in me. And so when someone makes a comment about my physique now, I'm just like, yeah, there are plenty of people who are bigger than me and there are plenty of people who are smaller than me. Um, and so yeah, this this sort of practice of self-standing as a creator is one of the most helpful things to me. And I know from having talked to a lot of you who are creating content that this is something that you struggle with. So, I highly recommend making social media a a sort of spiritual practice of sorts because it's benefited me just as much as any other practice in my life
