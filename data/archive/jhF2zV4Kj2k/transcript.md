# Solid chest day!

<https://www.youtube.com/shorts/jhF2zV4Kj2k>

It's always refreshing to see a chest day on TikTok that doesn't have four different chest exercises because why do you need so many chest exercises? He has an upper pec bias exercise with solid rest times, rep ranges, and a moderate amount of volume. If it were me, I'd probably take a slightly wider grip just so there's a little less elbow bend happening. That way you're less likely to fail due to your triceps. Next up, he has some pec deck flies, which is going to involve more mid to low pec, but does train the entire chest well. I would suggest thinking about bringing your bicep into the side of your pec to finish the rep rather than thinking about bringing the hands together as this is going to effectively shorten the pec, whereas this is not. Some front delt work here. If it were me, I would be stopping the range of motion right around here considering he already did an incline press and this bottom range of motion here is going to involve the upper chest significantly. But maybe he just wants a little bit more upper chest volume. A cuff machine lateral raise. I actually quite like this variation. Those machine lateral raises don't always feel the best, so that's a nice way to improve it. And some heavy tricep push downs to finish off the day. Looks like a great workout and solid physique to show for it.
