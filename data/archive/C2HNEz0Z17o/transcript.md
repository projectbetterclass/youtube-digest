# HOW TO Reverse Cable Curl

<https://www.youtube.com/shorts/C2HNEz0Z17o>

The reverse cable curl is by far the best way to train the forearms and bicep. Most people do this wrong. I'll show you how to do this right. What most people do is they pull straight up. So, they have to overcome inertia here and then they get all the way to the top. Better way to do this is to flatten out the strength curve. What I want you to do is when you pull up, I want you to pull your elbows behind your body and keep the bar as close to your body as you physically [music] can and pull all the way up to the top. Squeeze your forearms and gently let it drop in the exact same way. By doing this, you flatten the strength curve. When you flatten the strength curve, you put a lot less stress on [music] your tendons, ligaments, and joints, which allows you to provide more muscle stimulus to the muscles that you're targeting. And that's how you get massive biceps and forearms.
