# Are the biceps misunderstood?

you know the primary action of your bicep is not actually elbow flexion but instead it's supination next time you train your biceps don't only do curls but start adding in pronation and supination exercises you're going to get the best burn supination of the forearm is not the primary function of the biceps and there are two main reasons for this when the biceps is acting at its greatest distance from the elbow it's acting at about the distance of my thumb from The Joint Center of the elbow but if we do the same thing for forarm rotation you can see that the biceps is basically just on top of the joint when it's pulling on this axis of the forarm it's pulling out a about the length of half of a thumbnail and this leads to reason number two which is that when you go through elbow bending the biceps go through a much more substantial length change as compared to when you just work through supination when you work through supination the biceps barely changes length now it's worth pointing out that no muscle in essence has a primary function because when muscles contract it's not like they pick and choose between their joint actions so when the biceps contracts it both supinates from that more pronated position and it attempts to bend the elbow so if you want to train the supination function just supinate as you do the curl
