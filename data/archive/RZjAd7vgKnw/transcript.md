# Explaining the Arnold pull-down

<https://www.youtube.com/shorts/RZjAd7vgKnw>

why did Arnold do pull Downs like this a lot of people will now claim that this is poor technique but he's actually doing it for a very good reason a normal LP pull down is lighter at the top and much heavier at the bottom but your lat muscles are much stronger when they're stretched and much weaker when they're shortened so this may present a problem which is that your muscles are stronger where the resistance is lighter and your muscles are weaker where the resistance is heavier but you can circumvent this using the technique that Arnold is using what he's doing is he's actually allowing the weight to accelerate upward toward the stretch position which makes these tissues proportionately have to work much harder in the top and then what he's doing is he's yanking out of the top portion of the range so that the momentum of the bar actually makes a little bit lighter in the bottom proportionately Arnold is making the motion heavier at the top where the muscles are stronger and proportionately lighter at the bottom where the muscles are weaker keep in mind that I wouldn't advise that everyone use this strategy it's a relatively more advanced technique that you need to be able to utilize still with some degree of control [Music]
