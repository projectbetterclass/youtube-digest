# Why did jay cutler do this ab exercise?

why did Jay Cutler used to do crunches like this with his legs over to one side the fibers of the internal obliques run in this direction what this means is they not only side bend the trunk but they also rotate the trunk in this direction so when you rotate your pelvis over to One Direction what you're doing is you're actually putting those fibers more in line to perform the Crunch and in this particular case Jay was predominantly training his internal obliques because of this and this is different from a normal side crunch because the normal scy crunch would train the internal obliques which run this way as well as the external obliques which run more this way so if you really want to build up those internal obliques rotating and then doing crunches in that degree of rotation is a great option
