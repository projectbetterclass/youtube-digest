# How to train serratus anterior

<https://www.youtube.com/shorts/l4-zZ4BCUjQ>

Targeting your serratus anterior muscle is really simple and many of you are probably already doing it without knowing it. The serratus anterior is really, really good at pulling the shoulder blade forward in a variety of different directions because it has all these splaying fibers that kind of run around the ribs in a banana-like fashion. Do you want to train the lower fibers of the serratus? Do something like a decline press where you're really allowing your shoulder blades to move forward and downward as you press. If you want to target the more upper portions of the serratus, do something that's a little bit more straightforward like a fly more for your middle and upper chest. This is why in many cases it's really important to allow your shoulder blade to move forward if it needs to or downward and forward if it needs to. Because the serratus is a muscle that really supports motion of the shoulder blade ultimately, which then allows the other muscles like the chest to do the work. But again, you're probably already training this muscle. Just recognize that you really want to allow your shoulder blades to move and wrap around your ribs so that you can train that muscle even more if that's a goal of yours.
