# The reason you can't find good exercises

<https://www.youtube.com/shorts/qzv_NasNjZQ>

The reason why you can't seem to find exercises that really work for you and your body that you feel like you can connect to strongly is because you're so focused on what the exercises look like. So here's how this happens. People start to learn about exercise and the way they do that is by consuming information from other people. Typically people who are demonstrating exercises. People say things like do this for this outcome, put your arm in this position, position your feet in this direction, use this stance width, use this arm path. And all those things are helpful, but you become too attached to them. You take them too seriously, you take them too literally. Instead of taking them as directions that you can experiment in or toward, what you do is you take them in as hard fact. I need to do this and this and this. I need to use this amount of range of motion for this amount of reps with this type of loading and it needs to look like this. And the more and more that you try to focus on what something looks like, the less you can actually be attentive to what something feels like. And so you need to recognize that any bit of advice that anyone can ever give you about an exercise is inherently very limited. The only thing that will actually allow you to discover what exercises work for you and your body and what exercises don't is if you start to pay attention to what feels right for you. Does moving all the way up to the top of the range of a lat pull down not feel like you can actually connect with your lats doing? Great, don't go all the way up. Just use the portions of the ranges of motion in any exercise that really really seem to rhyme with what your body wants. The more and more you do this, the less that you even have any degree of concern over what something looks like because you know that it's exactly what you want because you're so in tune with your system as you're doing it. And you'll realize that it was never the appearance of the thing that actually mattered, more so that the appearance was a doorway into helping you experiment with what ends up being good for you. And the cool thing about this is that your exercises and the way they look can change dramatically over time without your even realizing it because you're so attuned to the flow of your system and what your system actually wants out of every exercise.
