# One of my favorites over the years to build a big lower body

<https://www.youtube.com/shorts/kgEI9Z1Vcho>

Y'all know I love a good single leg leg press. Doing it single leg with the non-working leg on the ground allows me to better stabilize the pelvis, which allows me to get a bit more range of motion, so I can get a better stretch on whatever muscle I'm trying to work. In this case, this is going to be more of a glute-biased leg press, and as you can tell by my facial expressions, I am definitely feeling it. Make sure to brace and pull yourself into the seat, and be extra careful when doing your bilateral both leg leg press not to let your hips lift up and your low back move into flexion, as this will place more of the load on the spine. If you've had issues with that happening, give the single leg leg press a try next leg day. Make sure to like and follow for more.
