# I’ve been using this exercise pairing for years now

<https://www.youtube.com/shorts/5b7FqOY2UCQ>

Here's how you're going to finally grow some bolder shoulders. Everyone and their grandmas already doing regular dumbbell lateral raises, which are great, but they never actually load the delts in their more shortened position or lengthened position. Enter one of my favorite cable delt exercises, the Y raise, which does do a good job of training the middle delt in the shortened position. Don't worry, this can also be done standing with the cable set loaded to the floor if you don't have a dual seated cable machine. If you've never trained your delts through this range of motion, then this could be an excellent addition to your workout. It's also great for your scapula as it trains the low trap as well. I like pairing this with a cable lateral raise with the cables set close to hand height, which is going to load the delts in a more lengthened position. And here is a great setup that you can use if you prefer to do this unilaterally. If you've been stuck at a certain weight on your dumbbell lateral raises, this is a great way to break past plateaus as you can make smaller jumps in weight and you're loading the bottom position, which a dumbbell raise doesn't do well. An easy way to incorporate these two exercises is to take the volume that you were using for your dumbbell lateral raises and just split it between these two movements. The way that I typically incorporate them if I'm trying to grow some big delts is by doing one to two sets of each at least two times a week, anywhere between six to 12 reps. Start with the cable Y raise and then move to the cable lateral raises right after that. Hope this helps and as always, make sure to like and follow for more optimal training tips.
