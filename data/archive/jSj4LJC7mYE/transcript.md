# Lateral Raise DB vs Machine

<https://www.youtube.com/shorts/jSj4LJC7mYE>

The lateral raise machine is by far the best exercise to hit it. The reason why is because it allows you to hit your lateral head without being constrained by grip strength. As opposed to the lateral raise, where you really have to make a huge effort to hold on to those dumbbells. So, the other thing that this machine allows you to do is it lets you go to a higher range with way more control. When you've got dumbbells, as you go up past this point, you don't get as much tension through your delts. What most people will do is they'll just take it up to here. But, if you want to extract the most out of this machine, you want to take it all the way to the top and feel your delts will squeeze. It helps by turning your wrists in at the top. Watch my wrists. If you want massive delts, stop spamming dumbbell lateral raises. Start spamming machine lateral raises instead. They're an absolute cheat code.
