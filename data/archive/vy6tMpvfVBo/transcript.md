# How to quad smith squat

All right, so what I like to do to set up the quad dominant Smith squat is set a couple of plates and basically in line with where this bar is and I always almost imagine my heel in line with the bottom of this bar. So, when I put my foot on this plate, my heel is basically going to line up this way. Not a hard rule, just a starting point. When you get onto the wedges or the plates or whatever you have, you are supposed to feel a lot of toe pressure. So, a lot of people's heels will start coming up and I think the solution to that is to put more pressure on their heels. The reason your heels coming up is because pressure needs to shift toward your forefoot in order for your knee to move really far forward. So, two things. Play around with the foot position, but use that as a starting point guideline. Also, a really good way to make sure everything stays in your quads is keeping that forefoot pressure really until your knee starts to track backward. So, you can think of it like as the knee travels forward, pressure needs to shift toward the toes. As the knee travels backward, pressure needs to shift back toward the heel. Remember, there's not a universal correct way to do this. Um you can use whatever height of plates that you want. Whatever makes it appropriate and most comfortable plus most quad dominant for you.
