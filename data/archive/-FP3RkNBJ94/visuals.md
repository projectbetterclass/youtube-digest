## 8. [03:11] Text explanation of Federal Reserve policy and Treasury bond yields

Fed policy initiatives have a huge effect on the price of bonds. For example, when the Fed increased interest rates in March 2017 by a quarter percentage point, the bond market fell. The yield on 30-year Treasury bonds (T-bonds) dropped to 3.02% from 3.14%, the yield on 10-year Treasury notes (T-notes) fell to 2.4% from 2.53%, and the two-year T-notes' yield fell from 1.35% to 1.27%. The Fed raised interest rates four times in 2018. After the last raise of the year announced on Dec. 20, 2018, the yield on 10-year T-notes fell from 2.79% to 2.69%.
