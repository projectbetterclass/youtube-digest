## 3. [12:05] Nick Sleep's Amazon investment analysis with specific financial figures and returns

Amazon's annualized return from 2004-2022 is 26%. They invested 20% of their portfolio into Amazon in 2004. If everything else had gone to zero, it would be 15.4% annualized over 18 years between 2004-2023 vs. 9.1% for S&P 500. They had many mistakes along the way. It did not matter. The important decision was to buy and hold Amazon forever. Circling the wagons around Amazon was key.
