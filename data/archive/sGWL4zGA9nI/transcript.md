# Exercise Tutorial: Cable Lateral Raise

<https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=sGWL4zGA9nI>

here's a simple five-step guide on how to set up a lateral raise to train your middle delts step number one set the cable around the height of your mid chin step number two Loop your hand through the dandle so that it's on top of the hand and so that you don't actually have to grab the handle step number three step over the cable so that the cable runs through the legs this allows for a little bit more of a natural arm path which may feel better than having the cable behind or in front of your body step number four anchor your opposite hand with a slight lean against the cable column when you do this you should make sure that the cable is roughly 90° from the forearm the bottom end of the range and step number five make sure that when you execute you're raising outward rather than with the intent to raise upward here are three mistakes that people make when trying to execute this motion mistake number one is bending the elbow a slight elbow Bend is fine but anything more than that is likely to create forces that are counterproductive to the goal of training the middle delt mistake number two relaxing in the bottom of the motion a lot of people simply let their arms smooshing against their body stop the range of the motion in the bottom rather than actively resisting through their middle delt the entire time as a cue for this I like to think about not letting my arm get smooshed into my body and mistake number three using a full overhead range if your goal is to train the middle adts utilizing the bottom 90° of this range of motion will be most specific to that goal going above this point isn't necessarily bad but it's where a lot of scapular motion needs to occur and as a consequence the traps will be recruited more when the arm needs to move 90° and above
