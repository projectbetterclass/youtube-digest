# Do THIS For Side Glutes

<https://www.youtube.com/shorts/EfZUSwB9V-E>

A lot of people assume that kickouts are the only way they can train the side glutes. But you don't have to use a cable kickout to train the side glutes. I recently tried this variation out in the Smith machine and ended up loving it. All you have to do is straddle the bar, grab it with one hand, and anchor your opposite leg and same side hand to the machine. You should aim to bend the knee slightly, emphasize turning your belly button into and away from the working leg. You can think of this as loading similar tissues to a kickout that's aimed slightly backward behind the body. So this will be a really good exercise for targeting the back sides of the glute med and the glute med. This is also an exercise that loads both of those muscles in their more stretched position, and an exercise that, unlike a cable kickout, is much more stable to progress.
