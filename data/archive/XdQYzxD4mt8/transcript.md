# You're Spending $50 to Make $10 in Profit

<https://www.youtube.com/shorts/XdQYzxD4mt8>

This is probably one of the biggest mistakes that business owners make. Let's say I sell these for $10, but it cost me $9. And the average person buys 10 pens. So, it's $100 in revenue, but it cost me $90 to sell the 100. But if my team comes to me and says, "Dude, we're killing it. We're spending $50 to get a $100 sale. We should do this all day." But what's really happening? I'm actually spending $50 to make $10 in gross profit. Not a good equation.
