# How to actually crunch

<https://www.youtube.com/shorts/zkQi8rLD8ek>

This is how most people do their rope crunches. They fix their spine angle. They kick their hips back and they basically sit down like they're doing a reverse hinge. Instead, to focus on the abs, fix your knees and your hips and just round and arch at your spine. This will allow your abs to do much more of the work proportionately as compared to the hip flexors like in the prior variation.
