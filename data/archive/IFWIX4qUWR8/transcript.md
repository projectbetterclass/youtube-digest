# Mike Mentzer's training was retarded?

<https://www.youtube.com/shorts/IFWIX4qUWR8>

Just another low volume, highintensity pusher sing for Mike Menser's methods. When are people going to understand that low volume, highintensity doesn't automatically mean training like Mike Menser? Like, no disrespect to Mike, he influenced me a lot in my younger training days. At one point, instead of listening to music during my workouts, I'd listen to his recordings for motivation. >> Get even more motivated. The more it hurts, the more it's going to motivate you. that last rep where you're trying as hard as you can and you barely make it. >> But aside from the general approach of training with very high effort and doing fewer total sets as a result, most of the specific training methods he used were almost certainly not ideal for maximizing gains based on what we know now. He recommended taking every single set to allout failure without exception. He regularly used intensifiers like forced reps, drop sets, and heavy negatives. He was also big on super sets and pre-exhaustion. the training frequency per muscle was anywhere from once every 7 to 14 days at least for the bulk of his career. He recommended very slow rep speeds including intentionally slowing down the concentric. Said the eccentric was more important than the concentric. So yeah, I still have respect for Mike Menser and his push back against aimless high volume pump style training was important and ahead of its time. But there are better ways to actually apply the lowvol high-intensity approach if you want to build muscle as efficiently as possible.
