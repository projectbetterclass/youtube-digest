# Lifting Weights WITHOUT Cardio

<https://www.youtube.com/shorts/8t36QWcreIs>

Cardio just helps gain a greater def calorie deficit. So you can do it through calories, right? The problem is that when you lower your calories too aggressively, it generates a lot of fatigue in the system. And something like slow steadyst state cardio doesn't seem to have that same effect. This allows you to tag on an extra 500 calorie deficit without reducing your intake by another 500 calories. If you train hard enough, you really don't have to do cardio. But for a lot of people, they haven't developed the skill to train hard enough. They essentially have to do cardio to add that extra energy expenditure.
