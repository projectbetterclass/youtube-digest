# HOW TO Rear Delt Machine Fly

<https://www.youtube.com/shorts/p-JwZgcGric>

The reverse pec deck is by far the best exercise for the rear delts. Most people do it wrong. Here's how you do it right. Most people, what they'll do is they won't flare their elbows. They'll keep their elbows quite low and they'll lock their arms out and they'll just go back like this. What you want to do to activate your rear delts is you want to lift your elbows up, keep your chest up, pull your shoulders back, and pull through the rear delt. By flaring your elbows, you get way more action to that rear delt.
