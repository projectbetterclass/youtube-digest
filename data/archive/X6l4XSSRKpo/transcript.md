# Why Face-Pulls Are Overrated

this exercise, the rope face bowl, is probably the most overrated back exercise that there is. Here are two main reasons. Number one, when you get really strong at this exercise, your balance is a major limiting factor. For instance, if you're looking at the video of me trying to pull the full stack, which is super easy for me if I have a brace with my foot, I'm just getting pulled off balance and I can't really actually train the muscles that I'm trying to target. Which leads me to reason number two, which is that even if you do do it on the floor like this with some kind of foot support so that you're not offbalance, the actual grip itself is incredibly limiting because the rope is constantly scrunching your fingers up as you're trying to pull backward. A much more sensible alternative is just something like this, which is literally just a basic widegrip cable row. Number one, because you're stable, it's not load limited. Number two, it's not uncomfortable on your grip. You can just put straps on and your grip won't be a limitation. And number three, you actually probably get more range and more specific stimulus from the target tissues. Not only just because of how much more your arms are able to move in a backward and forward direction, but also because none of those other things are limiting factors. Now, if you wanted to make the argument that doing it like this is some kind of mystical magical rotator cuff magic pill, then that's perfectly fine. But you could also just do it in a much more stable setting like this on the cable row station or with a much more specific external rotationbased exercise like this. In any case, I just think that there are so many better alternatives compared to the rope face pull. And I want to put an end to this weird prioritization of the rope face pull as if it has some kind of superior qualities that other exercises simply don't. Because the reality is there are just many better options. All of which are really easy accessible options that are basic and easy for people to do. Now, if you're someone who is dogmatically attached to the rope face pull because you just really enjoy the way that it makes you feel, nothing against that rationale. Do more rope face pulls if you really like to do them. But just understand that there are limitations to this exercise that can be a bottleneck to progress with those specific kinds of patterns.
