# PULL-DOWN FIX!

<https://www.youtube.com/shorts/G4stSo_ohBk>

this single arm cable pull down is a fantastic option for training your lats but the thing that's messed up the most when the setup is appropriate is just leaning too far into the arm so a lot of people set up well and understand that the arm needs to get pulled forward and up but a lot of times as fatigue starts to set in people start to lean more forward which basically defeats the entire purpose of the exercise the whole point of having the bench away from the cable sack instead of directly under the cable is so that you can load your arm in a stretch position that's more forward and sort of away from the body as opposed to directly overhead if you take yourself all the way out here and then you just sort of lean forward and do it that way it's effectively the same as if you were just sitting down on the regular lap pull down so when you set the bench up and you move farther away from the stack make sure you brace against the bench sit back into the arm so the arm is getting pulled forward and up not overhead and then just pull down from there this is effectively how you're just going to stretch and contract your lats through a more full range as opposed to being up overhead where yeah they stretch but they don't have as good leverage to pull the arm down
