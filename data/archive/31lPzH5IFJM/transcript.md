# That time I froze on stage

<https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=31lPzH5IFJM>

When I first started my speaking career, I had an incredible event to speak at. It was ICMI Showcase. So, this is where they showcase Australia's speakers. And if you do a good job there, you get booked. I rehearsed my thing so much that it was really natural. But because I was panicking and I was freaking out like, man, I was freaking out inside. I walked out mental blank. And the silence got really awkward. Because I just I lost what I was going to do. And in those moments, I believe it's so important to allow yourself to be vulnerable and say it. Like it's it's so powerful because it's humanizing. And then I don't know what led me to that, but I just said, "I'm really nervous." And got a laugh. And I go, "I've been preparing for this so much. I've been so focused on impressing every single one of you here that I totally forgot how I'm meant to start my presentation. Can I walk out again?" And everyone was like, "Ooh." And everyone clapped the hell out of that for me. And my career actually propelled. The audience loves humanization. So, by acknowledging it, I came out and did it again. It really changed my perception. Because every other speaker was so polished, I had a mistake, it made me human. So, I think in moments like that, dare to be vulnerable. It's a power that we don't tap into enough.
