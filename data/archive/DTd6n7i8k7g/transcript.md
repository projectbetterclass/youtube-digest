# Don't Do THIS on RDLs!

let's talk about why elevating your toes in an ardio might not be the best thing for your hamstrings when you straighten your knee and you pull your toes up like this you're doing is you're putting maximal tension in the gastrock or the outer calf this is why straight knee calf raises are a great exercise for that muscle but when you're doing something like an RDL where you want the range of motion to come from the hamstrings and the hip using a straighter knee position with the toes up may actually limit your ability to stretch the hamstrings a lot of people feeling a lot of stretch sensation around the back of the knee with the toe elevation might just be feeling their calf stretch rather than the bottom of their hamstrings so instead I just recommend using the floor instead of trying to elevate the toes just keeping your knees in a more straighten position so that you can maximally stretch your hamstrings without running into any stretch issues in the calves my favorite straighty hinge variation is actually the 45° hyperextension you can make it really specific to targeting the hamstrings if you put something underneath of the hyperextension and just use the bottom portion of the range of motion
