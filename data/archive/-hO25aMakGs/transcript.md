# Don't Have a Pull-Over Machine? Do This.

<https://www.youtube.com/shorts/-hO25aMakGs>

The machine pullover is a fantastic exercise to train the lats, but very, very few gyms have lat pullover machines. So, here are three alternatives that you can try that you probably haven't before. Option number one, find any normal cable pulley stack. Set the cable so it's somewhere heaviest in the mid-range and make sure that you're actively using your feet to push your body backward into the cable column so that you're stable while doing it. Option number two, if you have the access, pull a bench up to any normal cable column and just do the pull over that way. And if you find that you get pulled up and out of the seat doing it two arms, you can simply just do it one arm at a time. And option number three, which most closely mimics the pad feeling of the pullover, is to loop your hand through some kind of dehandle webbing or strap if you have one. Use your opposite hand to brace up against the cable column and do the pullover that way. All these three options are fantastic and you should choose the one you like the most.
