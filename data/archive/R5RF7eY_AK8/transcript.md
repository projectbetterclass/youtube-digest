# How To Target Quads vs Glutes On Leg Press

<https://www.youtube.com/shorts/R5RF7eY_AK8>

To target your quads in a leg press, do these three things. Number one, keep your feet lower on the platform. Number two, keep pressure more toward the balls of your feet. Number three, allow your knees to bend as full as comfortable. To target your glutes in a leg press, put your feet higher up on the platform. Keep foot pressure more through the heel and allow your knee to be driven toward your chest.
