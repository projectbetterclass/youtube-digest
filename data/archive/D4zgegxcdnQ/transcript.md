# Triceps Machine Problems?

<https://www.youtube.com/shorts/D4zgegxcdnQ>

these kinds of machine triceps extensions can be a great exercise to train the triceps but the biggest downside to them is that they're highly unstable especially toward the squeezed position of the triceps and the reason it gets so unstable is because as you extend your elbow more and more the resistance actually moves twice the distance from the shoulder as compared to the Elbow so it just wants to lift the whole upper arm upward so there are two main solutions to this the first is that you can use your opposite side hand to stabilize the working arm by shoving down on the working arm's bicep this will allow you to use more of the total range of motion in a highly stable environment next thing you could do is instead of going all the way down you can really just use this first half of the range because the resistance really at that point just wants to smush the hand into the shoulder
