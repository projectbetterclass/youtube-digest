# Suggestions for growing the pecs

<https://www.youtube.com/shorts/Co1ffvhFHK0>

When people get shredded, you can see the separation in the pecs that form the three different divisions that make up the pecs, the upper, the middle, lower pec. And this gives you guys a really good idea of why I suggest focusing on growing the mid to low pecs rather than prioritizing straight incline pressing or anything that's going to bias the upper chest over the mid to low. The upper pec, which you can see there, makes up a much smaller portion of the pecs as a whole. So, if you're a beginner to intermediate lifter who has not built up a significant amount of pec mass already, then I would suggest focusing on growing the pecs as a whole and not really worrying about biasing the upper chest so much. This means more flat pressing or slight incline pressing at say 15° rather than sticking with straight incline pressing or low to high flies. Once you've actually built up a decent chest, then you can worry about biasing the upper pecs more. And this is not me saying that you should never incline press. I'm just saying that you shouldn't be solely focusing on biasing the upper pecs. If you want to do say a flat press on one of your chest days and then an incline press on the other or say a flat press and then a low to high fly, I think that's totally fine. Just don't make it all upper chest.
