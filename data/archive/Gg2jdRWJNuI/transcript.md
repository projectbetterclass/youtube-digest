# Fix your pull-down (with one change)!

<https://www.youtube.com/shorts/Gg2jdRWJNuI>

you ever do pull downs but all you really feel are like your forearm and your bicep instead of your back here's a super simple tip to fix that first grab two of these D handles then Loop each of them around either side of the bar once they're on grab them in a neutral grip so that your palms are facing one another and then before you pull down lean back first and pull your elbows to your hips and make sure that as you go through the range of motion that you make sure you keep your lean backward this will keep your forearm in line with the handle the whole time which is where you want it
