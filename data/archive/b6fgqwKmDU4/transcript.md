# STOP pulling narrow for lats?

<https://www.youtube.com/shorts/b6fgqwKmDU4>

Here's why you should stop using a narrow arm path to target your lats. A lot of people when they use a narrow arm path end up actually just pulling to their side like this. But where you really want to aim to target your lats is more like the front of your ribs. So rather than thinking narrow or to the sides, I tell clients to aim their elbow in front of their body like in their lower ribs and they immediately are able to find those lats because a lot of people end up pulling into their rear delts or even in their traps when they start to get even slightly away from their rib cage because you have to remember those lats are running in and up and underneath really really tight. And so emphasize that by basically pulling your elbow in front of your body and not worrying about getting back here because getting back here is just going to lose all that tension that you had. And then make sure that you're keeping an intention to keep your arm in front as you're coming forward. So think aim to the low ribs and then keep that in alignment as you're doing the stretch into the eentric.
