# Is pressing like this bad for your neck?

<https://www.youtube.com/shorts/RUxE4W1XOww>

Why do people lift their necks up like this whenever they are doing bench press? Is it a bad thing to do? Should you avoid it? Well, the first thing to know is that it ultimately has to do with your scapula moving forward and backward and your scaps relationship to your neck. When you lower your arms in a press and your arm needs to go back behind your body, what happens is your scapula needs to move inward and upward toward your spine. When things bunch up like this, there's a lot of tissue that actually can push the neck forward. So, allowing the neck to naturally move a little bit forward as you lower in your press can actually be a more comfortable strategy for some people in order to fully retract the shoulder blades in the bottom. Now, it must be known, this is not a good or a bad thing. This is just a thing that may be appropriate or not depending on you and what's comfortable versus what's not. Just know that it's a completely normal natural thing that some people like and that you can absolutely benefit from trying out. Now, if you don't like it, it's uncomfortable, and you're not doing it sort of naturally, I wouldn't focus on it too much, but still it is just another technique like any other that you may find beneficial to try out. Just remember, it's not good or bad, it's just individual.
