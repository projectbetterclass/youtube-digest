# The complete forearm training guide

<https://www.youtube.com/shorts/yUj2x09Mv-o>

here's a simple guide on how to understand and train all of the different parts of the forearm there are two major categories of forearm muscles number one are the Muscles that bend the wrist number two are the muscles that extend the wrist the Muscles that bend the wrist run up into the inside of the elbow and the muscles that extend the wrist run up and outside of the elbow in order to train either group we can use cables or dumbbells to train your entire Forum with the dumbbell simply find a bench grab a dumbbell and anchor your entire forarm on the bench with your hand hanging off there you can simply perform wrist flexions and wrist extensions with a dumbbell Off the Bench remember that when you're doing the flexions you want to kind of direct your hand toward the inner side of the elbow and when you're doing the extensions you kind of want to direct your hand toward the outside of your elbow you can also use a cable to train these same motions but the benefit of the cable is that you don't need to actually grab the handle itself you can simply Loop your hand through the handle and load the wrist more directly
