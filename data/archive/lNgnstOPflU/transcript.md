# Can you actually isolate your outer calves?

<https://www.youtube.com/shorts/lNgnstOPflU>

[Music] Is this actually true? Yes and no. Yes, because the available but very limited evidence that we have on this topic suggests that it is. There was a study in 2020 that said that toes in trains more the outer calf and vice versa for the inner calf. However, to represent these things as onoff switches is totally inaccurate. In reality, all of these variations train the entire belly of the calf. So, what you should do is pick the foot position number one that is comfortable and number two that is sustainable as you add more load to your calf raises over time. My personal opinion, I wouldn't worry about the toes in toes out thing. Pick the one that is comfortable regardless of the foot position. You'll be training your entire calf. Just make sure you're doing some kind of straight knee calf raise and some kind of bent kneecap raise and you'll have everything covered.
