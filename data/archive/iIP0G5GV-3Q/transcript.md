# A simple PHONE hack to practice communication skills

<https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=iIP0G5GV-3Q>

Susan's question: How do we increase our normal speaking volume? One of the greatest techniques that I've discovered for behavioral change is to use our phone. Now, we know for a fact that human beings look at their phones hundreds of times a day. So, when I was early in my communication journey and I wanted to improve certain things like volume, for example, I would use my home screen and I would use the screen saver there to remind me of the things that I'm working on. So, for me, a big one was volume. Another big one was facial expressions. Another big one was storytelling. And then another one for me was to be more precise. Because when I used to communicate back then, I used to be all over the place. I wanted to be more precise. So, I'd have these reminders on my phone. And if we're looking at it 4 500 times a day, that's 4 or 500 reminders that we're giving ourselves every single day to try out this new behavior. And then often the next question people ask me, Susan, is they say this, they go, "Vin, uh when do I practice this? When do I practice using more volume?" Answer's simple. Anytime you talk to anyone. Anytime you open your mouth. Even when you're ordering a coffee. What you'll find is by having that reminder, it's going to encourage you on a daily basis, multiple times, to practice using more volume. And then as you do it more and more and more and more, it gradually becomes a habit. And then your habitual volume will start to increase.
