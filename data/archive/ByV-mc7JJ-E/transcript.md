# The #1 split squat mistake

<https://www.youtube.com/shorts/ByV-mc7JJ-E>

A big mistake I see made on split squat setups is using a bench to support the back leg rather than having the foot lower on the floor or an angled surface like the one I'm using here. The reason being when the foot is anchored too high, a lot of times what happens is people get discomfort in their ankles, knees, hips, and spine, and that usually ends up compromising the mechanics of the exercise and also the comfort of the exercise. So, you don't necessarily have to use a wedge and a box like I'm using here, but just basically put your foot lower as opposed to putting it up onto a bench and you'll probably feel a lot more comfortable that way.
