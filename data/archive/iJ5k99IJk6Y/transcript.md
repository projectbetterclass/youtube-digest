# How are these back exercises different?

<https://www.youtube.com/shorts/iJ5k99IJk6Y>

How is leaning forward in a seated cable row different from staying upright? When you're upright and the resistance is pulling your arm forward, the upper portions of the lats as well as the entire upper back get a really good amount of work. What happens when you lean forward is the resistance actually just moves more vertical relative to your shoulder and torso. And so if the resistance is more vertical relative to the arm and torso, the lower portions of the lats and the lats as a whole will do more proportionate work as compared to the upper back. So both the normal horizontal row as well as the lean forward row will target both the lats and the upper back. The version where you're leaning forward will be more lat specific and the version where you're more upright will be more upper back specific, relatively speaking.
