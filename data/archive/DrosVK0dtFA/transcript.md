In a typical barbell bench press, the bottom position is going to be a lot heavier than the top. This is because the bottom position applies a torque much farther away from the shoulder and elbow, whereas the top position is much more stacked. This is typically why you see bodybuilders doing partial reps in the bottom position. And the top half of the motion tends to be much more triceps involved, whereas the bottom position tends to be much more chest involved. So, if you want to bias your chest, you can really just use the bottom half.
