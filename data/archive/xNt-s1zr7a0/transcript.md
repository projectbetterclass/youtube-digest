# HOW TO Cable Row

<https://www.youtube.com/shorts/xNt-s1zr7a0>

The cable row is by far the best exercise to develop your upper back. Most people do it wrong. This is how you do it right. Step number one is ensure your shoulders are in front of your hips. Step number two is when you pull the weight, make sure you flare your elbows. And the last one is make sure you stretch the weight, but when you do it, you want to keep your chest up. When you keep your chest up, it allows your rhomboids to stretch even more. Most people think when they fold their torso forward like this, they get a deeper stretch, but they're actually getting a deeper stretch in their lats, not their rhomboids.
