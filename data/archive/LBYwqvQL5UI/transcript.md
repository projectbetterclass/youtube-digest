# How to Structure a Pull Day (in less than 90 seconds)

<https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=LBYwqvQL5UI>

here's a simple guide on how to structure a pole day to Target all of the different portions of the back first start off with a motion that's more of a horizontal chest supported row to Target more of the upper portions of the lats as well as the traps and rear delts when you do a motion that's specific to the upper portions of the lats you want to lean forward a little bit so that the loading angle isn't perfectly horizontal with the Torso but rather slightly vertical exercise number two is a vertical lat exercise meaning an exercise that can Target the lower portions of the lats this is one example of many different that you could do but I personally prefer to do these single arm so that I can stretch my arm slightly up and across my body rather than just straight up to the ceiling make sure that when you do this you have a strong anchor in both the thigh pad and the opposite hand and that you're stretching your arm up and overhead comfortably the third exercise I'll do is something that's more of a row but that is slightly low to high as compared to the first variation we did which was slightly high to low even though a row like this isn't chest supported it's stable because we're leaning backward and we have our feet really directly in line with where the cable is coming from the intent with this motion is to really train the upper portions of the back mainly in the mid traps the upper traps the rhomboids and the rear delts and lastly to finish off something specific to the rear delts I personally love reverse flies done just in front of the body where the cables are roughly 90° from the arms throughout a majority of the range of motion while the rear delts will be involved in most pull downs and rows it's probably a good idea to have something that's more specific to them where you can really focus on making them the limiting factor of the movement
