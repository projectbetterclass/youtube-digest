## 4. [06:05] Complete 7-step framework for finding intrinsic value

FINDING INTRINSIC VALUE
Step 1: How much money is this business printing each year?
Step 2: How fast is this company growing each year?
Step 3: Use the growth rate to predict the next 10 year's cash flows.
Step 4: Imagine we'll sell the business in 10 years (and receive a big one-time cash flow)
Step 5: Discount all future cash flows by our desired rate of return (15%).
Step 6: Add together the discounted cash flows together to find the intrinsic value.
Step 7: For 'per share' data, divide the intrinsic value of the company by the shares outstanding.
