# When you catch bro doing this lift...

<https://www.youtube.com/shorts/sTB_be5vIRs>

My favorite way to actually train external rotation is to put a cable high, jam my arm up against the column, and do the external rotations this way. If you don't have a cable column or something similar, what you can do is use a dumbbell and prop your arm up on your side like this. Or you can lie sideways, have your arm at your side this way. In any case, you need to actually both achieve motion and force. This only achieves motion and not the right force direction because it just loads the elbow instead. If you want to learn how to train external rotation properly, check out the related video linked to this
