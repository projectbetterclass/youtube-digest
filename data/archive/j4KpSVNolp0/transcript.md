# Improving the Sam Sulek facepull

<https://www.youtube.com/shorts/j4KpSVNolp0>

this face P variant that Sam suic has popularized is a great variant for training the rear delts but you can improve it a lot with just one small change rather than laying down and having the bench behind you you can angle the bench upwards so it's actually supporting your chest rather than your back and the reason this is important is because the resistance is actually pulling us upward meaning that we need something in front of us rather than behind us to support our bodies if you angle the bench upward so that it's actually on your chest and the resistance direction is this way you just get pulled into the bench and you're really stable that way and then as a consequence you don't have to manage anything through the trunk and you can just focus on pulling against the resistance just for the rear delts
