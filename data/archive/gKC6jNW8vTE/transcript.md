# Improve the cable pull-over in .23 seconds

<https://www.youtube.com/shorts/gKC6jNW8vTE>

If you normally do your cable pullovers like this and you are looking for an alternative or maybe this motion just doesn't feel right to you because you basically feel like your abs and your hips are trying really hard to control the motion. Here's a sweet alternative. All you got to do is turn around, put your back up against the cable column, keep the cable where it is, and do the pullover that way. Notice that I put my feet pretty far forward so that I can basically just drive myself right back into the pole. One more thing that you might want to do is if the load gets high and your back starts to hurt, put a sweatshirt or some kind of pad on the pole itself so that it's nice and comfy.
