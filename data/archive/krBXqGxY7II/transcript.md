# What's the point of drag curls?

<https://www.youtube.com/shorts/krBXqGxY7II>

What's the point of doing a drag curl instead of a regular curl? In a regular curl, the upper arm, it stays still as the forearm moves, but in a drag curl, the upper arm actually moves backward. How does the upper arm moving backward change the motion? The biceps, represented in pink here, actually cross the front of the shoulder, which means that they can actually pull the shoulder slightly forward. So, when you're doing a drag curl, you're actually creating the opposite motion. Meaning that even though the biceps are shortening at this attachment, they're actually lengthening now at the shoulder. And what this means is that in the drag curl, the biceps actually become less involved compared to the other elbow benders, namely the brachioradialis and the brachialis. So, if you want to target your biceps more, just do the regular curl. If you want to target your other elbow benders more, do something more like a drag curl.
