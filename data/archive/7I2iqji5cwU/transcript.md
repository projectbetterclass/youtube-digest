# Feeling your quads during glute bridges? Try this fix!

<https://www.youtube.com/shorts/7I2iqji5cwU>

why do you feel so much recruitment in your quads when you're doing a hip thrust instead of just your glutes so whenever we're doing a glute Bridge the angle of the shin is really going to determine what gets recruited most if my shin stay 90° from the floor throughout the entirety of the range of motion at the bottom and at the top I'm just going to be able to push directly downward but if my feet start to move too far away from me then my feet are effectively trying to resist this sliding Force the whole time as I'm going through this motion what this does is it starts to recruit a lot of the quads instead of just the glutes in addition if my feet are too far underneath of me and my tibas are angled backward that will create a ton of motion at the knee before I'm actually moving upward with the hips so in either case fix the shin so that they're constantly 90° from the floor and as you lower and as you raise make sure that they don't move away from 90° in addition make sure that you're actually pushing through the floor through the heel and not the toes if you push through the heel that'll create a force that's really just hip dominant but if you push through the toes that'll start to create a force that's more quad dominant
