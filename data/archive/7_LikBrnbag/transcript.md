# HOW TO Preacher Curls

<https://www.youtube.com/shorts/7_LikBrnbag>

The preacher curl is by far the best way to have giant arms. Most people do this wrong. This is how you do it right. Most people will sit on the seat and lean their armpits all the [music] way into the bench. The problem with this is it doesn't flatten out the strength curve. When you have a flat strength curve, you don't have to overcome the initial inertia of the weight. And the problem with overcoming that inertia is it puts a lot of stress on tendons, [music] joints, and ligaments before you get the appropriate amount of muscle stimulus. Flat strength curves means [music] you get the most stimulus on the cell that you're targeting. Hence why we want to flatten the strength curve. Step number one, shoulders over your elbow and round your upper back for max stability. Step number two, pull [music] the elbows in so they're not flared out. Step number three, curl the wrists and curl all the way to the top until you can no longer go any further. Once you get to this point, simply lower the weight until [music] it can no longer be lowered any further. Remember, always extend slowly and milk the eccentric. Do this for giant arms.
