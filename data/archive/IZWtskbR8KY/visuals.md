## 6. [03:47] Text definitions of three financial metrics: Positive Operating Income, Positive Net Income, and Positive Operating Cash Flow

Positive Operating Income = the operations of the business are profitable after considering operating expenses such as COGS, wages, depreciation etc. Positive Net Income = the company makes money after considering all expenses.
