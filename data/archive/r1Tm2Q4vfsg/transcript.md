# Stop doing these curls

<https://www.youtube.com/shorts/r1Tm2Q4vfsg>

If you're doing a dumbbell curl and this is the line of resistance of the dumbbell at the bottom and toward the top, the only thing that moving your arm forward is actually doing is increasing the distance of the load to the shoulder. In other words, look at the distance between this and the elbow. If you move it from here to here, there's no difference. But the line of resistance is actually moving much farther away from the shoulder in this position as compared to this one. So by holding your free weight out here, the only thing you're actually doing is increasing resistance on the shoulder and not the elbow with the biceps are actually doing.
