All right, so if you are performing shrugs at this angle right here where you're staying relatively upright and the load is pulling straight downward like this, you'll be biasing proportionately the upper traps. In other words, if something is pulling all this stuff directly downward, the shrug directly upward toward the origin of the upper traps. But, if you hinge forward like this and the load direction is now proportionately more horizontal, you're going to shift the focus from the upper traps here to now more of the middle traps here. So, if you were using something that was pulling you slightly forward in this upright position, you'd have to pull backward and upward moving more in the direction of the middle traps. So, think about it this way, if your shoulders are loaded straight up to down, that's going to be more upper trap focus. But, if the load is pulling you more forward to backward, but also with that upper component, that's going to be more middle trap focus. Not like one is better than the other, they just do different things. So, pick the option that most aligns with your muscular goal and what you're actually trying to do.
