# How to give honest feedback without sounding rude

<https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=zyS4nckeWBY>

With honest communication, how does one stay firm and get the point across while still being nice and polite without being overbearing? Look, it's all in delivery. Let's say for example, I wanted to give someone some feedback on their punctuality. I could be like this. I can go, "Hey Susan. Hey look, I I just wanted to call out that, you know, you came into work a little bit late today. And look, while that's okay a few times, you've been late consistently this entire week. And I wanted to just to call that out because it's important that we open the business at 9:00 a.m. because we have customers normally coming into the store at 9:00 a.m. Is there anything you wanted to share with me? Is is there has there been a problem why you haven't been able to come in and and be on time at 9:00?" And when you can talk to people with that kind of melody, it's not as threatening. Whereas a lot of people when they have difficult conversations, their delivery suffers. Sometimes they can sound a little harsh and they can sound like, "Susan, you're always late. You've been late every single day this week. We open the store at 9:00." And notice I'm saying the same things, but I've changed up my delivery. And when you deliver it like that, the other person immediately get gets upset now. Then you just create a conflict. So to me, so much of it is to do with delivery. So many of the arguments that we get into, they are a result of delivery problems.
