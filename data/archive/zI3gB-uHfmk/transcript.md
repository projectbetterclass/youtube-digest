# Straight or bent knees in an RDL? (The differences)

<https://www.youtube.com/shorts/zI3gB-uHfmk>

What's the difference between doing an RDL with your knees straight versus bent? When you keep your knees straighter in an RDL, what you do is you stretch the hamstrings around both the knee and the hip at the same time. What this means is that the hamstrings being stretched the most is actually what stops the motion. If you allow your knees to bend a little bit, your hamstrings aren't as stretched. And because your knees are bent, you actually get more range of motion at the hip, specifically in more range of motion at the glute. So, when you allow the knee to bend a bit more, that's going to be a glute-dominant motion. When you keep the knee straighter and you stretch the hamstrings more, then it's going to be a hamstrings-dominant motion. Both motions will recruit the hamstrings and the glutes to a significant degree, but what you choose should depend on what your primary goals are.
