# Cope harder

<https://www.youtube.com/shorts/D-X7ZQCunec>

What's with this new trend I've been seeing where people will post their physique and say something like, "This is what a physique that's been lifting for 8 years, 4 times a week, 1 hour in the gym looks like." And it looks like they're maybe in their first 6 months of training. Then the comment section will be split saying things like, "Oh, thank you for posting this realistic transformation. It makes me feel so much better about the progress that I'm essentially not making." And then other people will be like, "This is terrible. How did you make such little progress?" Honestly, I kind of side with the second half of those comments there because it really doesn't take a ton of time in the gym to make good progress. And the post that I've been seeing, it seriously looks like these people have been lifting for maybe 3 to 6 [music] months. The cope seems to be rising to dangerous levels, guys. I also really dislike when I see people blaming their lack of progress on their genetics. Like, yes, genetics do play a huge role in how fast you will grow, but it's not an excuse for you making very little progress in years of training. Are there some people out there who probably don't respond very well at all to strength training? Yeah, sure. But it's a very, very small percentage. So you're probably not one of them. You're probably just coping.
