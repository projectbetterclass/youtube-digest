# General warm up recommendations

<https://www.youtube.com/shorts/rIM_CcUNTJU>

This is how I would recommend warming up in the gym to reduce risk of injury, make better gains, and not waste a bunch of time during your warm-up sets. Total number of warm-up sets depends on the exercise, where I'm at within the workout, what workouts I fit before that, and how I'm feeling going into that exercise. Typically, I'm doing anywhere from one to four warm-up sets with two to three being the average. If this is a big compound movement where I'm using a lot of weight, then I will typically be doing anywhere from three to four warm-up sets. Take an RDL, for example. If it is an exercise that is not going to require me to brace quite as much, and I'm not using quite as much weight, like a lateral raise, for example, then I may just be doing two to three warm-up sets. Let's say I already trained the shoulders prior to doing that lateral raise. I did an overhead press already. My shoulders are already pretty warmed up. I might just do one warm-up set at 80% of the weight that I'm going to be using for my lateral raise working set. When it comes to the rep range you're using, I typically like staying anywhere from two to 10 reps. If I'm feeling rather cold, then I'm sticking to around eight to 10 reps for my first warm-up set, and then typically after that, I'm moving down to two to three reps for the following sets leading up to my working set. Between those sets, I am resting minimally just enough to make sure that I'm not breathing heavily in between those sets, but you really shouldn't be if you're only doing two to three reps. First warm-up set, I'm typically using anywhere from 40 to 50% of the load that I'm going to be using on my first working set. Then on that last warm-up set, it should be around 75-80% of the working weight that you're going to be using. It's very important on your warm-up sets, especially the last set, that you are being forceful on the concentric of the movement to properly prepare you for the working set.
