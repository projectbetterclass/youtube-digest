All right, so if you're doing preacher curls at an arm angle like this, what you're going to notice is that the bottom position is really, really heavy. The stretch position is really, really heavy. This is because the weight or whatever you happen to be using is really far from the elbow in terms of the load and the distance relative to the working joint. And it's also going to be really light at the top. You see there's a very close stacking where the weight or whatever you're holding is close to being on top of your elbow. So, this is going to be a really, really good variation for training more elbow straightened position. But, alternatively, if you sit down or do a standing curl, and now all of a sudden this is where the load is applied, you'll notice that the bottom position is much lighter and the top position is much heavier because now the weight applied is much farther from the elbow. So, these two exercises are actually complimentary and that the first variation trains the bottom position more and the second variation trains the top position more. Neither one of these is necessarily better than the other. It's just that they do different things. And you should experiment with whichever one you feel more connected to and stick with that one.
