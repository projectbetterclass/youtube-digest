# How to train triceps without cables

<https://www.youtube.com/shorts/SGQGRROeJoI>

how can you train the triceps if you don't have access to cables my favorite options are to use dumbbells and resistance bands to set up an overhead extension set a bench to around 60° grab a single dumbbell and lay on the bench sideways with your opposite arm make sure that you're hugging the bench for stability and with the working arm angle it around 90° from the floor from there the motion is just a regular overhead extension option number two is a dumbbell triceps press to set this up angle a bench around 10 to 15° grab two dumbbells lay down and keep the arms close to the body as you bring the dumbbells close to your shoulders this kind of looks like a regular press but if you look at it from this angle what you'll notice is that the dumbbells are very far from the elbows and very close to the shoulders this essentially makes this a triceps press and will load the triceps significantly in their most stretch position option number three is just a regular banded triceps extension so if you have access to a band simply anchor it above your head and just do single arm extensions with the paws at the bottom to emphasize the squeeze position with all these three options you'll have all of your triceps training covered without access to any cables
