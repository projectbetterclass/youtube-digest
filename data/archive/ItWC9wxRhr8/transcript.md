# One of the best reasons to do mostly free weight exercises

<https://www.youtube.com/shorts/ItWC9wxRhr8>

This is actually one of the best reasons to do mostly free weight exercises. I try to do barbell exercises for more compound exercises because I tend to switch gyms often and it's hard to track weight in different machines. Yes, if you are somebody who often travels for work or just moves between gyms a lot, then doing more free weight exercises can be extremely beneficial. It is much harder to try and progressive overload when you're switching between a bunch of different machines at different gyms. But, 135 lb is always going to be 135 lb on a barbell, whether you're in LA or you're in Virginia.
