## 4. [02:41] Text from Berkshire Hathaway shareholder letter discussing capital-allocation decisions

In 58 years of Berkshire management, most of my capital-allocation decisions have been no better than so-so. The Secret Sauce: In August 1994, Berkshire completed its seven-year purchase of 400 million shares of Coca-Cola. The total cost was $1.3 billion. The cash dividend from Coke in 1994 was $75 million. By 2022, the dividend had increased to $704 million.

## 5. [06:17] Text from shareholder letter discussing share repurchases and value-accretive pricing

The math isn't complicated: When the share count goes down, your interest in our many businesses goes up. Every small bit helps if repurchases are made at value-accretive prices. Gains from value-accretive repurchases benefit all owners. When you are told that all repurchases are harmful to shareholders or to the country, you are listening to either an economic illiterate or a silver-tongued demagogue.

## 6. [09:52] Text from shareholder letter with highlighted tax statistics

During the decade ending in 2021, the United States Treasury received about $32.3 trillion in taxes while it spent $43.9 trillion. The $32 trillion of revenue was garnered through individual income taxes (48%), social security and related receipts (34½%), corporate income tax payments (8½%). Berkshire's contribution via corporate income tax was $32 billion during the decade, almost exactly a tenth of 1% of all money that the Treasury collected.
