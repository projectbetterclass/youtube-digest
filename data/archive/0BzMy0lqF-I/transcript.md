# Why is this tbar row SO heavy?

<https://www.youtube.com/shorts/0BzMy0lqF-I>

Those of you who have used this row machine know that it has undoubtedly squashed your ego like a little itty-bitty bug. And even people, the biggest [ __ ] in the world like Nick Walker, are only using about 50 lb per side, 100 lb total. So, what's the deal here with this machine and why is it so humbling? Well, this really just comes down to the physics of it. You see, most T-bar row machines make it so that the handles are very close to the plates. This means that the plates and you have similar leverage against the axis of the machine, at least compared to this machine. In this machine, the weight is almost twice the distance from the axis of the machine than where it's rotating compared to where you are pulling on the machine. This means that the weight has a lot more leverage than you do. Couple this with the fact that the handle can move and that you can pull at very acute angles, the bottom position feels like your shoulder blades are going to tear off your body. I absolutely love this machine because of how heavy it is in the bottom position.
