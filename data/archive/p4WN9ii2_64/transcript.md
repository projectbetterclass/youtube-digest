# How to train the teres major:

<https://www.youtube.com/shorts/p4WN9ii2_64>

here's a simple guide on how to Target and train the Terry's major muscle the Terry's major muscle is located here it attaches to the outside of the shoulder blade and the upper arm so in order to lengthen it these two things need to separate and in order to shorten it these two things need to come closer so any motion where you're pulling your arm in toward your body is going to be a great option for the teres major this could be anything to wider pull Downs single or double arm cable pullovers or some kind of row pull down hybrid done a little bit tighter to the body the most specific out of all these is probably the cable pullover Motion in order to set these up you want to stand around the middle of a cable stack set both cables high that close to the top position they're close to 90° from your arms then basically just pull down to where your arms are roughly parallel to the floor
