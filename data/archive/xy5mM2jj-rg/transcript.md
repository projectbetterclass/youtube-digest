# Is rounding your back BAD?

<https://www.youtube.com/shorts/xy5mM2jj-rg>

this rounded back position, we all know is the death of your back. There is nothing inherently wrong with rounding your back in a deadlift. Instead of avoiding rounding your spine altogether, you should try to build some resilience to those positions by training them directly. Jefferson curls and back extensions off of a hyperextension are my two favorite ways to do this. Both are very stable options and both you can easily load and progress over time. So, instead of fearing the rounded back position, just train it and become resilient to it over time.
