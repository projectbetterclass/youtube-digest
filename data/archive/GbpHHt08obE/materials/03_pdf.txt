Session	9:	Post	class	test	solutions	
1. d.	All	of	the	above.		You	need	all	the	conditions	to	hold	for	optionality.	
2. b.	$10.			If	the	stock	price	goes	to	$65,	the	cash	flow	on	the	bought	call	will	be	
+$15	(65-50)	and	cash	flow	on	the	sold	call	will	be	-$5	($60	-	$65).	The	total	
cash	flow	will	be	+$10.	
3. d.	The	American	option	should	trade	at	the	same	or	a	higher	price	than	
the	European	option.	The	American	option	provides	everything	that	a	
European	option	does,	with	the	added	benefit	of	being	able	to	exercise	early.	
With	many	listed	and	traded	options,	that	benefit	is	worth	nothing,	since	
early	exercise	is	sub-optimal	(you	can	sell	the	option	for	a	higher	price).	
However,	if	there	is	the	possibility	of	early	exercise	exists,	the	American	
option	should	trade	at	a	higher	price.		
4. c.		$16.67.		To	set	up	the	replicating	portfolio,	you	need	to	solve	the	
simultaneous	equations.	Since	the	call	with	a	strike	price	of	$40	will	have	a	
cash	flow	of	$35,	if	the	stock	goes	to	$75,	and	a	cash	flow	of	zero,	if	the	stock	
goes	to	$25.	75	D	–	1.05	B	=	35	and	35	D	-1.05	B	=0,	Solving	for	D	=	0.70	and	
B	=	$16.67.	
5. c.	$1.90.	d1=-0.39->	N(d1)	=	0.3485,	d2=-0.59	->	N(d2)	=	0.2778.	Value	of	
call	=	45	(.3485)	–	50	exp-(.03)(5)	(.2778)	=	$1.90	
6. c.	$14.00.	d1=0.41->	N(d1)	=	0.6603,	d2=-0.48->	N(d2)	=	0.3152.	Value	of	
call	=	45	exp-(.015)(5)	(.6603)	–	50	exp-(.05)(5)	(.3152)	=	$14.00
