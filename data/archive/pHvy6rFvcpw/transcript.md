what are the differences between a vertical and a 45 degree leg press so here I have a 45 and a vertical leg press and what I'm going to do is I'm going to equate the position of the foot plate so that it's easier to see a comparison between the two is it the same relative joint angles that we just had before the first thing that you'll notice on the 45 degree leg press is that the starting hip angle is much greater than that of the vertical leg press as a consequence of this people tend to run out of range of motion at the hip very early on in the vertical leg press because they're at end range hip flexion pretty much when they're starting the motion on a 45 degree leg press we have all this room to travel through because we're starting at this more reclined angle even if we start the 45 degree leg press at an angle that's very similar to the vertical leg press the outcome is still going to be very different because of the direction of the foot plate relative to the individual's trunk and legs in addition with the 45 degree leg press we actually have the option to move our feet downward whereas in the vertical leg press if we move our feet below where our hips are we're basically going to be doing some sort of acrobatics trick so the 45 degree leg press in other words it's basically just a better version of the vertical leg press because it has all the same upsides with a lot fewer of the downsides
