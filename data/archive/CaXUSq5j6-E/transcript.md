# Beat 99% of People by Mastering These 3 Things

<https://www.youtube.com/shorts/CaXUSq5j6-E>

You can beat 99% of people if you can master the shame of rejection, the boredom of repetition, and the pain of feedback. You can beat almost anyone at anything if you're willing to fail 20 times in a row, look stupid in front of people that you care about, and keep going long after it stopped being convenient.
