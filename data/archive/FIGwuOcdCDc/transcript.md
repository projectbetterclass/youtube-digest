Whenever you do any kind of curl motion, whether your palm is up, down, or somewhere in between, you're always going to be training the brachiialis muscle. In other words, whether your palm is up, down, or somewhere in the middle, it's not actually affected by where your palm is. So, while it is true to say that a palm up curl is more biceps, and a palm down curl is more brachioraiialis, the brachiialis muscle will contribute equally in both cases.
