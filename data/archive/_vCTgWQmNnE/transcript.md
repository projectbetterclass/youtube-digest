Somewhere along the way we have lost the plot. There are so many of these peptide influencers out there now that are just pushing peptides instead of focusing on what really matters for people who want to make a lifestyle change because here's the thing, can peptides be extremely beneficial for certain people? Yeah, sure. But one, we don't have enough research on peptides. We don't know what the long-term effects could potentially be. And then two, if you don't actually make lifestyle changes, those changes that the peptides are making for you are not going to stick. You start taking a peptide for fat loss. You lose a bunch of fat. You're not going to the gym, so you probably also lose a bunch of muscle as well. You stop taking the peptide and your appetite comes back. You gain all the fat back because you made no actual lifestyle changes. You didn't form any healthy habits. Instead of starting your fitness journey by ordering peptides from a shady source that may or may not be legit, maybe focus on the lifestyle habits that will actually make a difference in the long term like cleaning up your diet, getting on a proper strength training program, things like that.
