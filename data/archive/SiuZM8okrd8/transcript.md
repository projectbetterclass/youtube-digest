# Do you use a bench for Bulgarian Split Squats?

<https://www.youtube.com/shorts/SiuZM8okrd8>

Do Bulgarian split squats make you feel extremely wobbly, unstable, and hard to coordinate? Well, if so, then using a bench may be contributing to that. There are a lot of vertically challenged people using platforms that are too high for them. If you're trying to keep your torso very upright and your platform is too high, this may lead to some lower back extension. And it can be difficult to stabilize on the top of your foot. This could also limit the range of motion on the front working leg since the back leg is going through such a big stretch. Instead, I would recommend trying a lower platform height of around 6 to 12 in. You'll You'll likely feel much more stable being able to push into the ball of the back foot versus trying to balance on the top of it. Now, if you're a vertically blessed individual, then using a bench may feel just fine to you. And in that case, I would say go for it. I also like setups like this as I find it a bit easier to stabilize using the top of the ankle versus rolling around on the top of the foot. So, if you've had trouble with this, make sure to try out these tips next leg day, and as always, make sure to like and follow for more.
