# Finding interesting stories in your life

<https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=_7eaYMig71E>

storytelling is one of the most important skills you can learn when I teach storytelling to my students they always ask me one question and it's the same question I used to ask as I was learning storytelling how do I find stories in my life that are more interesting and my mentors and teachers would say one thing and it was a bit of a hug and a punch in the face at the same time if you want to be able to tell more interesting stories you need to live a more interesting life and the reason why it felt like a bit of a punch in the face to me was because I thought to live a more interesting life I had to jump out of a plane sell my house and buy a van and travel the world I had to quit my job and become a zen master I've learned that no you can do those things if you want to or you can do small things small changes in the way you live can actually help you become more interesting for example let's say you're going to the movies instead of picking a movie that you know about already just go in there and randomly pick a movie you know nothing about or you go to a restaurant instead of you picking the food let the waiter or the waitress pick the food for you and finally the next time you're in line you're on public transport talk to somebody you don't know these are the Little Steps we can take towards living a more interesting life and as we live a more interesting life we become better storytellers otherwise what happens when you meet with your family and your friends they ask you how was your day and what do we all say inevitably we say ah same old same old tell me in the comment section below what small changes are you going to make to how you live your life to allow you to become a better Storyteller tell me in the comment section below
