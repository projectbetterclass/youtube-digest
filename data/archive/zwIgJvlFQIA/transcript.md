I have gotten a lot of people very lean in my decade plus as an online coach. So, I'm going to give you guys one of my favorite ways to get lean fast. Let me preface this by saying that this is going to be much easier for people who already have a good understanding of tracking calories, macros, eating healthy, that way they stay satiated, have good energy and performance in the gym, and then also don't get very hungry. If you are not those people, this is going to be a lot harder for you. I'm just going to be honest. The first thing that you need to do is figure out your maintenance. If you don't already know that, this may not even be for you. There are plenty of videos on that and I have videos on that already, so just go ahead search them up. From there, you are going to make a 750 to 1,000 calorie reduction. You are going to eat at that for 3 to 6 weeks. Now, I've had some people who can tolerate that for much longer. They don't get very hungry. Their energy is still good. They're performing well in the gym, but most people are not that. Ideally, you should be stopping that before you get really hungry, food focused, lethargic, and start losing performance in the gym. If you have a deadline that you're trying to hit like a bodybuilding competition, for example, then those things may be unavoidable because you're already pushing your body to extremes. For everybody else after that stopping point, I want you to add back in 200 to 300 calories. You will be eating at that again for around 3 to 6 weeks, then you're going to repeat adding back in 200 to 300 calories. Even with those additional calories, you should still be well within a calorie deficit. Throughout this, I would keep your protein the same and I would be adding back in carbs and fat. Once you get to a point where you've added calories back in and now you're around maintenance, I would just hang out there for 4 to 10 plus weeks to give yourself a break physically and mentally. You can repeat that process until you're sub say 15% body fat, you've got good visible abs, and then from there, I would just follow a more moderate deficit of around 400 to 500 calories. That is if you are trying to get really, really lean. Hope this helps and as always, make sure to like and follow for more optimal training tips.
