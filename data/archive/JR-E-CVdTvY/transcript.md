# How to: overhead triceps extensions

<https://www.youtube.com/shorts/JR-E-CVdTvY>

Here's a step-by-step tutorial on how to set up the overhead or katana extension. First, set the cable height right at about butt cheek level or slightly above. Then rotate your arm out, bend your elbow, and shove your elbow up toward the ceiling, and then mimic that position basically just with the cable in your hand. Since I'm training my left arm here, I'll want to put that cable basically right behind my right butt cheek. Now, if you see me from the back and I'm training my right arm, the cable is next to my left butt cheek and it's also sort of scraping against the opposite side shoulder blade. If you look at someone doing this exercise from this perspective, you want to make sure that you basically can't see the cable from behind the upper arm. So, that's pretty much it and hopefully that was helpful.
