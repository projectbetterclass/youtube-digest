# Is 2 sets per workout enough to build muscle?

<https://www.youtube.com/shorts/UBT3-A2maI0>

What's the least amount of work you can do and still [music] make gains? Most people do three sets per exercise, but what if you only did two sets? Would you make half the gains? Or what if you only did one set? Would you make any gains at all? Well, there's science to answer this. For strength, just one heavy set done two to three [music] times per week will build strength in most people. Here's a study showing that. And for any muscle to grow, just four hard sets per week can spark measurable muscle growth. Four sets per week for your chest that could look like this. Two hard sets of flat bench on Monday, two hard sets of cable flies on Thursday, done. Remember, six time Mr. Olympia Dorian Yates built his chest off exactly that, four sets per week. That may not yield maximum gains for you, but it will yield noticeable gains. Two sets per workout might sound ridiculous. Like I did two sets, you dumb [ __ ] Like you caused a maintenance level of hypertrophy. But people really need to stop talking about training volume as if it's just a total number of sets you do. Your muscles don't count sets, they only experience tension. And that tension only gets high enough to drive significant growth on the difficult reps [music] as you start approaching failure, and it increases the closer to failure you get. So a set is not a set. If someone is training very hard at a legit zero to one rep shy of failure, they can be subjecting the muscle to more total tension with two sets than someone doing six sets with three [music] to four reps in the tank. Once you understand that volume is really about the overall tension generated rather than just how many times you did some reps, took a rest, and then did some more, this won't seem crazy at all. Now, I'm not saying that you have to do only two sets per workout or four sets per week, but if those sets are fully dialed in, it can do a lot more than just produce noticeable gains. I personally built 95% of my physique off just a handful of hard sets weekly. It's the same method I teach inside Train With the thousands of guys are making gains on right now. You probably don't need more sets, you just need to improve the quality on the ones you're already doing.
