# Top 3 Triceps Exercise for GROWTH

<https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=cmvNWjAj0k4>

here are my top three triceps exercises that you can do to grow bigger triceps exercise number one is the triceps cable press to set this exercise up angle the cable slightly above the height of your head grab the inside loop of the handle with your palm and lean forward so that the cable is angled slightly behind your head to execute the motion make sure that your hand is directing inward toward your shoulder and that you're allowing your elbow to flare outward and backward you should treat this like a press and not like a regular triceps extension so you want to allow the upper arm to move exercise number two is the cross body cable extension to set this exercise up grab two cables with the right arm grab the left cable and with the left arm grab the right cable from there it's just a regular triceps extension with your arms held static at your sides make sure that the cable is parallel to the direction that your forearm is moving throughout the entire range if the cable station you're using is too wide or one side is occupied you can also just do it single arm and set it up in a very similar way exercise number three is the single arm overhead extension to set this exercise up angle the cable around the height of your hip and step slightly in front of it so that the cable when you move your arm overhead is essentially brushing on the opposite side shoulder blade when you get set up the cable should approximately be parallel to the position of the upper arm when your arm is over your head make sure that you're keeping the upper arm fixed and that the only thing that's moving is the forearm and the hand if you find this position uncomfortable you can simply just lower the angle of your arm and raise the cable up slightly higher
