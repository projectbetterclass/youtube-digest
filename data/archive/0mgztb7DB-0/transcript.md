been on a bit of a neutral grip pull down kick like I don't really like the straight bar right now but something about having my hands from here to here I'm pulling way more lats so when we assume a pull down grip that is Palms away from our face and we pulled down on a fixed bar our arm path is actually limited to being wider meaning that we can't really bring our arms in front of our bodies because to do that we have to turn our palms this way when we assume a neutral grip where our palms face each other or even where our palms face our face our arms naturally have to travel in front of our body if we're using the fixed bar if our arms travel in front of our body our lats actually gain a mechanical ability a better mechanical ability to pull the arm down another way to think about it is that the fibers of the upper back kind of run out this way so they will guide the arm path out to the side and lats run off of the ribs a little bit forward so if the lats run more front to back and we move the arm in a more front to back kind of a way that will recruit more lats and if the upper back runs in a little bit more in to out kind of a way and we move the arm more into out that will recruit more upper back
