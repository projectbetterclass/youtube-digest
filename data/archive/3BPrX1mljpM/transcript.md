# HOW TO DB Incline Fly

<https://www.youtube.com/shorts/3BPrX1mljpM>

The dumbbell incline fly is by far the best way to train the chest. Most people do this wrong. This is how you do this right. Step number one, chest up. Step number two, with your elbows bent, allow your arms to drop as far as they can go. From this point, allow yourself to straighten your arms out until you feel a stretch in [music] your arms and in your chest. From here, bend the elbows again until you can use your chest to lift the weight back up again and complete the movement. The movement should look like this. >> [music]
