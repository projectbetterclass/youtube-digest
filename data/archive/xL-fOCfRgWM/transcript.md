# Low Rep Isolation Exercises = Bad Idea?

<https://www.youtube.com/shorts/xL-fOCfRgWM>

Everyone tried to warn me that doing heavy isolation exercises in the four to six rep range was risky, but I didn't listen. And in 2023, I ended up flying to London for tricep surgery. Now, those two things have absolutely nothing to do with each other. And I still do heavy isolations in the 4 to six range because it's not inherently dangerous, and I just prefer lower reps because they're more efficient overall. Higher reps are fine, too, especially if you're a beginner who's still dialing in their technique. But as long as you're properly warmed up and your joints feel good, there's nothing wrong with lower rep curls, push downs, leg extensions, or even lateral raises, you won't get as much pump, burn, or soreness compared to high reps. But none of those are direct indicators of a productive workout anyway. What really matters when it comes to stimulating growth is training with high effort close to failure and progressing the load over time. And that can be accomplished whether you're doing five reps per set or 15.
