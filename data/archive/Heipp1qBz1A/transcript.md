# Where to put your feet on a leg press

<https://www.youtube.com/shorts/Heipp1qBz1A>

How does muscle recruitment change when you change your foot position on a leg press? All right, here's a really simple rule of thumb to follow to be able to figure this out. If your shin is 90° perpendicular from the foot plate in the direction of the foot plate, you're going to have more of a hip biased leg press. And if your shin moves away from 90°, whether it's lower or whether it's at a more extreme higher, then you're going to have a slightly more knee dominant leg press. So again, 90° more hip, away from 90° in either direction, more knee. With any leg press though, especially taken to a high degree of fatigue, you're going to have a lot of quad loading and a lot of hip loading. My advice would be to just find the comfortable foot position for you and just go to town on some hard sets.
