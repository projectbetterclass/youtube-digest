# Peptide influencers need to stop

<https://www.youtube.com/shorts/1viZnrfWO9o>

All right, this peptide shit's just gotten completely out of hand at this point. Every time I log in, there's another influencer in my feed raving about some compound they probably heard about on a podcast 3 weeks ago, and now they're out here just shamelessly shoving their affiliate link in everyone's face as if they're even remotely qualified to be recommending what people inject into their bodies. Now look, I'm not here to tell you that peptides are bad, okay? It's a broad category with a lot of different compounds being used for different things, and some do seem to have legitimate use for certain people. But what I am against is these reckless fitness coaches promoting the stuff to their audience like it's a protein bar. These are injectable compounds, a lot of them have little to no human research, unclear long-term risks, often sourced from random labs with zero oversight, so you don't even know what you're actually taking. And who's buying this stuff? Half the time it's beginners who don't even have the basics nailed yet, and now some clown with a ring light has them convinced that the answer is to start pinning. That's where the industry is at right now, and honestly it's just getting ridiculous.
