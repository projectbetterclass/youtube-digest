Things to keep in mind with wedges. Keep the feet in line with the wedge, okay? So, if I have one long wedge, I'm not going to angle my feet out this way, right? Because that's going to shove my ankles inwards, going to create all sorts of other problems for me, okay? So, if you do have a wedge that is long this way, keep your feet in line with it. But, if you have two, you can angle your feet out in line with it. Either way, my feet are still in that same line, okay?
