## 6. [08:58] Detailed text about G7 global minimum tax deal with specific tax rate information

Bigger picture: In its current form, the deal would require that companies pay at least a 15% tax on income, regardless of where they are based, making it less advantageous to relocate operations to countries with lower tax rates. The rules would apply to multinationals that have a profit margin of at least 10%, while governments would share the right to tax 20% of profits above that threshold. For example, an online company that has no physical presence in a country, but has significant sales there via digital advertising, would be obliged to pay some taxes to the government of that nation.
