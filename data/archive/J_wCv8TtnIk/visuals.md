## 1. [00:29] Text slide explaining laddered investment approach concept and how it works with staggered maturity dates.

A laddered investment approach is based on allocating portions of your total investment and staggering maturity dates so that each portion of your portfolio matures at regular intervals. Each rung of the ladder represents a specific maturity date. The more 'rungs' you have the more you reduce your exposure to interest rate fluctuations.
