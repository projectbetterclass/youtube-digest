So is the Cuban press actually a good choice when it comes to getting that round cap look to your delts? Well, whenever you see a combination movement like this, you need to ask where the weakest link is since that's where you'll end up hitting failure first. You can overhead press more than you can upright row, and you can upright row more than you can externally rotate. Now, you'll feel this exercise a lot because there's more muscles involved and you're probably doing higher reps, but that increased sensation doesn't automatically mean there's more actual tension on the targeted muscle. You'll always be limited by how much you can externally rotate, meaning your rotator cuff will get the most stimulus here, not your front or side delts. To maximize shoulder gains, you'll be way better off just doing a standard overhead press with heavier loads where your delts can actually produce force at their full capacity.
