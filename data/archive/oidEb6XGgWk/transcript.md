# AVOID side bends???

<https://www.youtube.com/shorts/oidEb6XGgWk>

Stop doing weighted side bends at the gym. If I see you doing this, I'm going to find you and I'm going to slap you. This isn't doing anything for your obliques. Side bends for your obliques are a great exercise to do. Your external oblique runs in this direction and your internal oblique runs in this direction, meaning that they cross in this X-like fashion. So, when both of them contract, they take the ribs and the pelvis and they smoosh them together. And so, if you have a weight on one side of your body, which is opening this side, this pull of the load is directly stretching those obliques. And if you side bend and crunch against them with a load still on the side, you still have a substantial amount of resistance on these obliques. Do your side bends. They're a great exercise.
