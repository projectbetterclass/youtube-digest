# Fix your chest supported row!

<https://www.youtube.com/shorts/5IVM53VWQO0>

You ever finished a set of chest supported rows and felt like you were just going to suffocate? If you have, here's a quick tip for you. A lot of times people that feel a breathing restriction are simply placing the pad too low on their rib cage. The lower portions of the ribs are the ones that are really flexible and allow for a lot of expansion and compression during breathing. The sternum and the ribs up here, however, are much less movable and much more rigid. What this means is that if you move the chest pad higher, you'll still have all this room down here to actually breathe and move the ribs, all the while still being rigid and stable having used the chest support up there.
