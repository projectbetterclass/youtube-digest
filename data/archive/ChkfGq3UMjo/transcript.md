# You should NEVER do these push downs???

<https://www.youtube.com/shorts/ChkfGq3UMjo>

Using supination, in other words, an underhand grip for any tricep training at all is extremely dumb as it has no hypertrophic benefit or mechanical difference in comparison to using a neutral grip and will only just make your training worse. There's absolutely nothing wrong with wanting to actually do a palms up push down. In fact, many of you may feel much more comfortable doing them this way. In order for grip not to be a limiter, you can simply just loop your hand through and do them this way so that you can still use a palm up grip without having to worry about losing any kind of output in the context of the push down.
