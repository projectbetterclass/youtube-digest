# Iron Condors: Profiting from High and Low Volatility

<https://www.youtube.com/shorts/TB027uj8zUI>

The iron condor strategy is good for two different scenarios. First, if you think a stock will go crazy in one direction, but you don't know which direction, the iron condor strategy makes money from very big moves. Direction doesn't necessarily matter. Now, number two, if you think a stock is not going to do much if it moves around, but it doesn't really rise or fall too much in either direction, then the iron condor strategy can also capitalize on this type of movement.
