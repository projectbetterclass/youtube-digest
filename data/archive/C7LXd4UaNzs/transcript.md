# This Always Happens to the Market after 10AM

<https://www.youtube.com/shorts/C7LXd4UaNzs>

When the market opens up, it's unlikely to fall 1% after 10:00 a.m. Okay? Listen to that one more time. After 10:00 a.m., if the market is up a percent, it's unlikely to end the day at flat. If it's down 1%, it's unlikely to end the day at -2%. If it's down 2%, it's unlikely to end at -3%. At 10:00 a.m. is the most important inflection point because the first 30 minutes of trading in the option market or just in the general stock market as a whole is the most volatile. So, after the first 30 minutes, you get a really good sense of where the market direction is after that point.
