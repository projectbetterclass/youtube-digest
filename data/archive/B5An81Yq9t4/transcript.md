# Hip thrust machine

<https://www.youtube.com/shorts/B5An81Yq9t4>

Barbell hip thrusts are the bane of my existence, or they were before my gym got one of these hip thrust machines. And if you do have a machine for hip thrust bridges, I'd highly recommend using it. If your gym does not have one, then use a Smith machine instead. That way you don't have to deal with setting up a barbell. And if I do have the option, I really prefer doing a cable loaded machine rather than a plate loaded. That way I only have to add a little bit of plates rather than putting all this weight on. That's one of the biggest time sinks in the gym for me is just putting plates on and then taking plates off, and it just wastes so much energy.
