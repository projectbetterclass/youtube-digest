# Chest Presses VS Flyes

the difference between a freeway press and a machine fly whenever we do a freeway press the load pushes our arm directly behind our body so when we get to the bottom there's a lot of load on our arm but when we get to the top there's pretty much no resistance on the arm when we do a machine fly there's a lot of resistance in the bottom when we get to this fully squeezed position you can see that the load is still 90° from the arm so what this means is that the pecly loads more of the total range specifically in the short position but because the elbow is straightened in a fly it typically can't get as far back behind the body so if you want to be more specific to training the lower peack the middle peack or the upper peack do something like a fly or a single arm press where you're loaded at that end range short position if you want to train more of the total PEC train something where you can get your arm as far back behind your body as you can so that all of the pecs can stretch and act against the resistance neither the fly or the Press is necessarily better than the other which means that you should just pick whichever one more closely suits your goals
