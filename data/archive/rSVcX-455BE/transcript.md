# HOW TO Pull-ups

<https://www.youtube.com/shorts/rSVcX-455BE>

Pull-ups are by far the best way to grow your arms and back. Most people do it wrong. This is how you do it right. So, step number one is you want to hold the bar in the palm of your hand, not in your fingertips. If you hold in your fingertips, your forearms will gas out. If you hold in the palm of your hands, you'll be able to hook into it far better. Step number two is you want [music] to pull with your shoulders, not with your arms. Pull your shoulders back to initiate the movement. And when you can't pull your shoulders back any further, then pull with your arms. Last step is make sure you control the negative. Most people just drop into it. What you want to do is let yourself down slowly. [music] This is called milking the eentric. Use these tips for gymnasts sized arms.
