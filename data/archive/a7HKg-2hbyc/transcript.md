# The problem with fitness social media

<https://www.youtube.com/shorts/a7HKg-2hbyc>

Sometimes I like to imagine if the people who were engaging in all this fitness social media drama and online beef were standing in front of each other, would they still be saying half of the things they're saying on social media or would they be saying it with the same vigor that they're saying it online? The same the same type of disrespect. And the answer is probably not. I think people would be a lot more respectful in person and I think that that's what we need to come back around to. Remember that you were still talking to a human being and that just because it's online and you cannot be physically assaulted that you shouldn't just, you know, let things fly. At the end of the day, we are talking about exercises. We're talking about exercise programming. There's nothing that serious that we need to be getting all upset and a huff about online. The fact that there are actual beefs starting between certain creators and I see so much back and forth between two, three different people. It's crazy to me. Can you just imagine what this would look like in person? We've got 30 40-year-old men arguing with teenagers and if this were happening face-to-face, I don't think it would happen like that, but I do think that the mental imagery is funny. I do think that a lot of people are falling for this rage bait that these creators are putting out there, but I also think that rage bait is incredibly corny and cringey and the fact that there are adults even engaging in that is just silly to me. Oh, dude, I didn't even mean that. I was just rage baiting you, bro. You fell for it, bro. What are we doing on here, guys? You're a grown man. Stop it.
