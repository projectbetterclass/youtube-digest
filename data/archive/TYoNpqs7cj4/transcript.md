# Call Option EASY Explaination on Chart #optionstrading

<https://www.youtube.com/shorts/TYoNpqs7cj4>

Can you guys guess the strike price of this option? I'll give you a second. All right. If you said it was 100, that is correct. If you look at 100, we are below zero profit. How much are we below? Okay, 200 is correct. How much did the call option cost? If you said $200, that is correct. What is our break even? Again, break even means what does the stock have to do for our option to make $0? As you know, the stock is currently $100. If you said 102, that is correct. If you said 300 or something else, the reason that is wrong is because each option is 100 shares. So you divide $200 that you paid by 100 shares to get $2. You can see the line touches zero between 100 and 105. Therefore, the break even is $102.
