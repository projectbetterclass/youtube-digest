## 8. [06:05] Article explaining Certificate of Deposit definition and features

What Is a Certificate of Deposit (CD)? A certificate of deposit (CD) is a savings product that earns interest on a lump sum for a fixed period of time. CDs differ from savings accounts because the money must remain untouched for the entirety of their term or risk penalty. CDs usually have higher interest rates than savings accounts. KEY TAKEAWAYS: Top-paying certificates of deposit (CDs) pay higher interest rates than the best savings and money market accounts in exchange for leaving the funds on deposit for a fixed period of time.
