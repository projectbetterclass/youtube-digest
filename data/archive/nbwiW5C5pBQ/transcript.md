Breaking news study, guys. Creatine builds muscle even if you don't work out. Researchers had participants supplement with either 10 grams of creatine or a placebo, with some of them following a training program and others not. But there's a catch that a lot of the accounts posting these hyped up headlines leave out, which is that lean mass is not the same thing as muscle mass. When body composition is measured on a DEXA scan, lean mass is simply any type of body weight that isn't fat or bone. And since we know creatine increases water retention inside the muscles, there's a pretty good chance that's where most, if not all, the lean mass gain was coming from. Now, that's not a bad thing. It's actually one of the benefits of creatine and gives your muscles a fuller look. But it's obviously not the same thing as building actual muscle tissue. Creatine is still one of the best supplements to include in your program for improving training performance, body composition, and possibly even cognitive function.
