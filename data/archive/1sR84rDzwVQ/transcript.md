# Try this triceps training hack!

<https://www.youtube.com/shorts/1sR84rDzwVQ>

here's a tip for training the triceps that you probably never heard next time you go to do a push down motion use the opposite hand to brace against the opposite side bicep so that you can actually shove into it as you're going through the extension a lot of times when people do extensions without a brace their shoulders move it around while their elbow is bending and extending so this is just something that will give you more stability and thus more output and focus on the elbow as opposed to the shoulder and it feels like if it's too much work for the opposite hand pushing into the arm you can actually just use a brace to brace that arm too so what that would look like is extension brace elbow up against the cable column itself just go through the extensions that way
