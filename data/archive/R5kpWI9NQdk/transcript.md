# How NOT To Leg Press...

<https://www.youtube.com/shorts/R5kpWI9NQdk>

Three things in this world are guaranteed. Death, taxes, and that no one gives a how much you can leg press. If you're one of those people who loads up the machine with a million plates aside trying to look impressive and then proceeds to do this, or even worse, this, consider swapping out your current gymware for this attire because it would probably suit you much better. Stop being a clown and just use an appropriate amount of weight you can actually handle and without cheating by pressing your hands into your knees. There's no one ideal range of motion on the leg press for everyone since it depends on your own mobility and what you're specifically trying to target. But full-blown ego lifting is never the answer and will probably just result in a worse hypertrophy stimulus along with a higher chance for injury.
