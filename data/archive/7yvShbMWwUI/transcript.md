# Is low protein actually the reason you’re not making progress? In most cases no.

<https://www.youtube.com/shorts/7yvShbMWwUI>

I'll be honest with you guys. As an online coach who's worked with nearly a thousand people one-on-one, it's very unlikely that low protein is the reason that you're not making progress. And if it is the reason you're not making progress, it's incredibly easy to fix. If you're having a lot of trouble eating a gram per pound of protein, which honestly isn't that hard, but I know some people don't have a huge appetite, there is research showing that you can eat around .8 grams per pound of protein and still make just as good progress. For somebody who's about 150 lb, that's 112 grams of protein. You could very easily split that into say three meals and then have one protein shake with a bit of milk, for example. That's about 4 oz of chicken per meal, which is not a large amount of chicken at all. And even if you go to say .7 grams per pound of body weight, you're still going to be able to make progress assuming you're doing everything else right. The areas that I see people messing up in most that's going to make a larger impact in their progress in no specific order is their consistency in actually going, intensity in the gym, not logging their lifts, running terrible programs, and not resting long enough between their sets.
