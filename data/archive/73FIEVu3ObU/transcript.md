# Finishers aren’t worth doing and if you think otherwise I’d love to hear why!

<https://www.youtube.com/shorts/73FIEVu3ObU>

It is never a good idea to include finishers within your workout if your goal is hypertrophy, growing your muscles. And here's why. Here's what I typically see when somebody says they're doing a finisher. They're essentially doing a lighter version of an exercise that they did earlier within that workout to burn out and completely fatigue the muscle where it feels like they have a crazy pump and they just can't push anymore. Maybe they're doing drop sets, but it could be something like doing say goblet squats after you've already done heavy squats that day with another exercise or doing push-ups after your chest workout. People like finishers because they feel very tired afterwards. They get a good pump in the muscle, but those things don't equate to more growth. All you're doing at that point is adding more fatigue and making it take longer for you to recover. The logic behind finishers doesn't really make sense if you start to think about it because it implies that you weren't doing a good job of stimulating the muscle on the earlier working sets. I think that part of the reason that people like finishers is the misconception that you have to feel tired and drained leaving the gym where that's not the case at all. With the workouts that I program, I typically leave the gym feeling even better than when I walked in. So yeah, skip the finishers where you're just doing less efficient exercises because nobody needs to be doing 50 rep push-ups when they just bench 225.
