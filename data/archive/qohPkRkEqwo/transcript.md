If you're doing this correctly, your forearm should be roughly parallel to the floor. Number two, in addition to this, lean slightly forward with your torso. leaning slightly forward will make the angle a little bit more naturally lat specific so that it's easier to drive your elbows in a slightly downward direction rather than in a backward direction. This will also make it really increasingly awkward for you to pull your hands toward your chest, which will limit the first mistake.
