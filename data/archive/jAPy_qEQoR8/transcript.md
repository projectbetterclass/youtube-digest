Gym pop quiz, let's see who gets this right. I don't have the most faith, but come on, prove me wrong. What is the biggest difference between these two push-down variations? We have a kneeling push-down for push-down one, and we have a standing push-down for push-down two. Pay close attention as to what you think the difference is. This isn't a trick question. Is it that one is kneeling and two is standing? The resistance profile, one works the long head, two works the medial and lateral head. Or there is no significant difference. Got your answer ready? Well, answer in three, two, one. Let's see who got this right. If you said B, the resistance profile, then you were correct. The kneeling variation has much greater resistance in the elbow bent lengthened position, and the standing variation has greater resistance in that lockout shortened position. Neither variation is bad, and you could even include one set of each [music] on your tricep day. The kneeling also isn't that important. I was only doing that because the rope attachment was so long. What's most important for this type of push-down is your distance to the cable and keeping the cable mostly vertical throughout the ROM. While both of these work all three heads of the tricep, the medial and lateral head may have better leverage in that lockout position. So, using the standing variation like this and loading that position more may be beneficial. And the long head may be better worked by loading that fully bent elbow position more, but like I said, both of these do work all three heads of the tricep.
