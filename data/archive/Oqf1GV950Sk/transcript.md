Damn, JPG lost it, no straps. You do not always need to use lifting straps for your pulling exercises. If you have a good grip, your grip is not failing, it's not what's failing first during the exercise, you don't have to use straps. There are certain machines, certain handles that are designed well enough that you really don't have to use straps when using them if your grip is strong enough. My general rule of thumb for using straps is if at any point during your set you start to worry about your grip, just use straps. There are certain exercises like shrug movements or deadlifts where I think the weight is typically always going to be heavy enough to where the person should be using straps if they want to get the maximum number [music] of reps. Doing shrugs, the weight is going to be rather heavy because the ROM is rather short. The traps and rhomboids can handle a good amount of weight, so I typically always recommend using straps for things like that. Deadlifts, your grip is never going to be stronger than your hip extensions are going to be, which is why you guys always see me using straps for these. Oh, but won't unnecessarily activating the forearm muscles take away from motor unit recruitment of the target tissue? Yet, somehow I still continue to progress on those pulling movements.
