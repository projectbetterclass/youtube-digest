This here is why letting go is so simple, but it's not easy because loosening your grip means confronting the fear that's been running the show, the fear that's been conditioned into you from birth. But guess what? That fear is a bluff. Now, there are three steps to letting go. Step one is be aware of what you want to let go of. And this requires you to confront your inner demons. All those things you've buried deep in your subconscious. It's about shining a spotlight on the negative. And people who are hooked on positive thinking hate this, but unless you're willing to face what's lurking in the .dark, you'll never truly be free. Step two is allow yourself to experience it fully. This is where you need to focus on the associated sensations, because letting go isn't something that happens through thinking, it happens through feeling. You have to get out of your head and into your body, and it's not about changing or fixing or getting rid of, it's about surrendering. Moving towards those sensations instead of running away. And lastly, step three is let it come up and let it out. And notice how I said let, not do. Letting go isn't an action that you take. It's something that happens when you stop interfering.
