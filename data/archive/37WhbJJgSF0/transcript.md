# Don't Make This Pull-Down Mistake!

<https://www.youtube.com/shorts/37WhbJJgSF0>

[Music] whenever you're pulling something backward or downward you need something in front of you to prevent your entire body from getting pulled forward the simplest way to do this would be to bring a bench over to a cable stack if you're doing it two arms if you don't want to do it two arms you can simply use your opposite side hand to brace against something forward so that you can pull with the opposite side hand without getting yanked there are ton of different ways to do this on a cable column and all that ultimately matters is the principle of using the opposite side arm as you push in the direction that the cable is pulling you this makes it so that your body doesn't get tugged all over the place so that you can maximize for tension in the pulling muscles
