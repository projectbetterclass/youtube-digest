# Triceps training: rope vs straight bar?

<https://www.youtube.com/shorts/9a1wsbPjz4U>

what's the difference between using a rope to train triceps and using a straight bar to train triceps when you use a rope to train triceps you have some freedom to move your hands but despite this you're still using one rope and they ultimately connect to one point up top the more you spread the Rope apart and the more that the handles move away from one another the more disproportionately difficult it gets on the other hand the fix bar doesn't allow your hands to move at all in comparison to the Rope its difficulty doesn't change significantly throughout the range of motion but it does come at the cost of not being able to freely move your elbows in the direction that you want this is is often why you see people doing something that looks more like a dip as opposed to a tricep extension with a fix bar in either case whether you're using a rope or a fix bar your elbows can't necessarily naturally be loaded as they might want to be so instead what I recommend is using a single cable or using two individual cables to load the elbows when you do this you can load the arm across the body as opposed to straight up and down this ends up being a much more natural path to load the elbow and as a consequence people shoulders and elbows tend to feel better
