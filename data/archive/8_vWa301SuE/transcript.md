# HOW TO Recover From Injury

<https://www.youtube.com/shorts/8_vWa301SuE>

This is how you actually recover from injury. People will injure, let's say, the hip and then they'll get a bunch of massage to it and nothing will happen. But once you massage it, you can now use the hip in a painfree motion and you fundamentally have to train it back to full strength for you no longer to have that problem again. Imagine it's a gate to a field. And on the other side of this gate is being injury-free. And essentially when people do massage and they do all these treatments, they simply open the gate and they close the gate. They don't [ __ ] walk through the gate. The way you walk through the gate is with movement. You use massage to allow you to have pain-free movement. But fundamentally, movement is what sets you free.
