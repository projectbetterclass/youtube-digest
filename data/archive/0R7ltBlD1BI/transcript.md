# You don't need to hit every muscle twice a week

<https://www.youtube.com/shorts/0R7ltBlD1BI>

You don't need to hit every muscle twice a week to make great gains. Everyone repeats it like it's some kind of special rule and acts like anything less means you're leaving tons of growth on the table. That's just not true and you can probably go lower than you think. Now, hitting a muscle two or even three times a week works really well. For example, upper lower four days a week or full body three days a week. Those are actually the two most popular programs inside my Train With app. But as long as total weekly volume is matched, the long-term difference between higher and lower frequencies is probably pretty small overall. One and a half times a week is still very solid. That could be rotating through upper lower three days a week or push pull legs four to five days a week. And I know this is blasphemy to say nowadays, but yes, even once per week can still build a great physique. In fact, I built the majority of my muscle in the first five years doing exactly that because I'm old and training each muscle once a week was pretty standard back in the early 2000s even if it wasn't optimal. If someone tries to tell you that lower frequency training is trash or that it's for maintenance only, that's not what the research shows and it's not what decades of real world results show either. Pick a split that fits your schedule that you enjoy and can stick to and stop worrying about whether every muscle is getting hit exactly twice every seven days.
