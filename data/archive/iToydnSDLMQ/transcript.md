# Insane fitness industry standards...

<https://www.youtube.com/shorts/iToydnSDLMQ>

It's honestly crazy how much the fitness industry can alter your perception of what an impressive physique looks like. I remember watching Fight Club as a young skinny teenager and thinking Brad Pitt was basically god tier. Whereas, you know that if this guy was on social media now giving muscle building advice, half the comment section would be roasting him into the ground for looking like he hardly even lifts. Same thing for Tobey Maguire and Spider-Man or even Daniel Craig's 007. I mean, obviously he looks great, but in terms of fitness influencer standards, he'd hardly even be taken seriously. Even for myself, I mean, I think I have a decent natty physique, but when I'm constantly seeing the top tier naturals in my feed, plus all the enhanced guys as well, a lot of the time, I honestly look at my own photos and videos and just think, eh, I mean, it's okay, but it's really nothing special. Whereas, I know that if the pre-lifting version of myself saw where he'd be, even after just a few years of lifting, he would have been very happy with that. And I'm not saying this is good or bad or whatever, but it's just something to think about. You know, rather than sitting where you are now and only looking ahead at where you want to be in the future, try transporting yourself back to when you first started and view your current physique through the eyes of that person. I just think that gives a better balance between continuing to push for more progress, but also appreciating what you've already achieved.
