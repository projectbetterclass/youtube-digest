## 3. [06:14] Definition of free cash flow concept text.

FREE CASH FLOW, Free cash flow is the amount of money left over for the owners of the business, after factoring in cash outflows that support its operations and maintain its capital assets.

## 5. [07:12] Six-step framework for calculating discounted cash flow.

DISCOUNTED CASH FLOW, STEP 1: Find the current free cash flow., STEP 2: Grow the current free cash flow out 10 years into the future., STEP 3: Add a terminal value., STEP 4: Discount all future cash flows to present value., STEP 5: Add together all future cash flows to find intrinsic value., STEP 6: Add a margin of safety.
