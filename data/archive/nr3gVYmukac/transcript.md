# How do squat wedges work?

<https://www.youtube.com/shorts/nr3gVYmukac>

Why does using a heel wedge make it so easy to stay upright when you're squatting? Adding a wedge underneath of the heel increases the distance between the knee and the floor. So, watch what happens when we increase that distance. When that distance increases, if nothing changes in the upper body, that line of force would end up in front of the foot. So, what we have to do is we have to move the line of force back over the foot, and to do that, we move the torso more upright. So, now we can see the line of force is back centered over the midfoot, and in order to do that, we had to shift our torso to a more upright position as a consequence of the distance between the knee and the floor increasing. Neither one of these squats is necessarily better than the other, but if you want to stay more upright and target more of the quads as opposed to the hips, choose a heel wedge.
