if you're using hanging leg raises for your ab work i would swap this movement out most people tend to turn the exercise into more of a hip flexor movement due to the lack of spinal flexion or they end up swinging around like this due to not being able to stabilize themselves instead i would recommend trying out gar hammers which offer up much more stability and will allow you to really focus on flexing that spine which is needed to contract the abs if you find that a steeper incline is too difficult then you can simply lower it till you find a position that works for your desired rep range
