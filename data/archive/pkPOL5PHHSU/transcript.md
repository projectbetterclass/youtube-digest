# The Problem With Cable Curls

<https://www.youtube.com/shorts/pkPOL5PHHSU>

Face away curls are a great way to load the stretch position of the biceps. But, a big problem with angling the cable backward and loading the bottom significantly is the fact that it also pulls your shoulder at twice the distance. So, simple way to solve for that is just to put something behind the upper arm, like one side of a functional trainer, so that you can load the biceps directly without having to worry about any of the shoulder forces.
