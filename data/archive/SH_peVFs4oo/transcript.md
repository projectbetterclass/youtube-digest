# It Takes 20 Hours — But People Wait a Decade to Start

<https://www.youtube.com/shorts/SH_peVFs4oo>

You know, it takes 20 hours of of concentrated work to become proficient at most skills. Playing the guitar, playing the piano. You can read 100 books on sales, but the first thousand cold calls you do, you'll learn more than all the books that you read up to that point. But people will wait a decade to do those first 20 hours cuz they're afraid. They don't want the judgement that they think is coming along from people who aren't actually paying attention to them to begin with.
