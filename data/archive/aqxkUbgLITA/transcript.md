# Only feeling feet during calf training? Try this.

<https://www.youtube.com/shorts/aqxkUbgLITA>

why is it that you feel your calf raises in your feet and Toes rather than the belly of your calf all of the calves attached to the back of the heel which means they don't actually move the toes what this means is that when we place our foot to do a calf raise we don't want the platform to be above the level of these digits we want it to be somewhere below the level of the balls of the toes if your feet are too low on whatever platform you're pushing into you're bound to basically just be clawing the platform rather than actually moving through your heel so in terms of queuing imagine that you have a string that's attached to the back of the heel and that string is pulling the heel up as you raise up and almost moving directly downward vertically toward the ground as you lower if we think about moving through the heel rather than the foot we're actually much more likely to recruit a lot of calf stuff as opposed to a lot of the stuff that manages toe flexion even though you may have some extra range up toward the top the top portion of the motion involves a lot less calf and a lot more of the toe flexors so I would start with just doing the bottom end of the range until you have a really good feel for being able to move your heel and you can move up toward higher positions with a greater control of that heel rather than just pushing through the toes
