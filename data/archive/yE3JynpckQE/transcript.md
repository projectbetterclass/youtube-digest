# Don’t make THIS curl mistake!

<https://www.youtube.com/shorts/yE3JynpckQE>

can you see what the mistake is when I'm doing concentration curls like this if you can't and simply the fact that I didn't have my leg pinned up against the bench pinning your leg up against the bench is a much more convenient and a much more stable way to do concentration curls and it basically requires no extra work in the setup often times when people have their leg floating out like this in space there's a lack of stability that leads to a lack of output and as a consequence a potential lack of stimulus to the biceps so instead of floating your leg 90° From the Bench place it up against the bench and curl directly into the leg and the bench neither of which is going to move
