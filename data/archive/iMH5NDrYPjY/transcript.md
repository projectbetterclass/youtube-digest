# The best calf raise you’ve never done

<https://www.youtube.com/shorts/iMH5NDrYPjY>

Standing calf raises can be a fantastic way to grow your calves, especially the gastrocnemius, the outer calf, but there's one fatal flaw missing and that's the added stability that you get by adding something like this, a strap around the machine so that you can actually shove into it throughout the entirety of the range and not have to actually manage the front-to-back forces that would otherwise make the motion a little bit less stable. To set this up appropriately, make sure that you have some amount of strap that's basically right above the level of the knee, so you're not at the knee but slightly above it. And in addition that the strap is pulling right around 90° from the bottom of your femur toward wherever you can loop it around. I recommend buying a daisy chain or something like a seat belt to do this.
