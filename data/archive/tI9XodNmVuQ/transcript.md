# Is this better than preacher curls?

<https://www.youtube.com/shorts/tI9XodNmVuQ>

So, this might sound a bit gimmicky, but it's actually true. You will feel this variation in your biceps more than a preacher curl. But why is that? And does it mean you should switch? Well, an important thing to keep in mind is that the hardest part of a curl is the point in the range where the line of force in your forearms form a 90° angle. So, unlike in a preacher curl, this movement will be easiest at the bottom where the biceps are more lengthened and then harder at the top where they're more shortened. And when you train a muscle in its shortened position, you create more internal pressure where blood can't flow out as easily, meaning greater metabolite buildup or burn. And you'll get more of a cramping sensation as well from the muscle basically being more bunched up. However, metabolic stress is not a driver of hypertrophy on its own. And the muscle actually can't generate force as effectively at that shorter length, even though it produces more feeling. Most people don't know this, but even though mechanical tension is what stimulates hypertrophy, it isn't something you can consciously feel since there are no sensory nerves inside the muscle fibers to detect it. So, there's nothing wrong with this variation, but it's not better than a preacher curl just because you can feel it more.
