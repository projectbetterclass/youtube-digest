# Fix Your Split Squats With This Tip

<https://www.youtube.com/shorts/P2UsNwOT60A>

instead of doing Bulgarian split squats with dumbbells and trying to figure out how to balance I personally recommend doing them in a machine allows you to directly load your spine there a lot of hack squat type machines that allow you to do this you could also do it in something like a Smith machine where you don't have to worry about the coordination and balance demand in addition if you only have access to dumbbells what I would personally recommend is holding a dumbbell in one hand and holding some kind of support with the opposite hand this again is just so that you can limit the balancing component of the motion so that you can just focus on output through the quads and the glutes of the working leg you can also do this with a simple cable setup which would probably be even more stable than the dumbbell setup even with support
