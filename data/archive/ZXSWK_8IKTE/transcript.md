# No one knows this about wrist straps!

<https://www.youtube.com/shorts/ZXSWK_8IKTE>

Most people are taking way too long to strap things. Here's a simpler way to do it. Instead of starting at the middle or the far end where you have to wrap something and then twist it about a thousand times to actually get it to stick, put the implement as deep as possible into the strap. And then once you're there, you can just fold the strap over a single time and then crank it backward to secure it. If you do this correctly, you should have all this extra room in the strap when you're done. And you won't have to wrap the strap 10 times over just to try to secure
