# BETTER SPLIT SQUAT ALTERNATIVE

<https://www.youtube.com/shorts/Vrzpy65_0Qg>

I finally had the opportunity to try out the single leg hack squat variation that I saw the other day. And let me just say, I think this is going to stay in my program. I would consider this a better alternative for split squats in comparison to a single leg leg press. Not only are we able to load the back leg rectus femoris in its lengthened position, similar to how a split squat would, but on top of that, we also have the pelvis stabilized, which doesn't happen in a free weight split squat. Due to the extra stability, you should be able to load even more weight and bias whatever muscles you're trying to work even better. I went with a higher foot placement to focus on working the glutes, and I'm definitely feeling it today. However, you still work the quads pretty nicely in the mid-range due to the frictional forces in pushing yourself back to get up. If you try it, let me know how it goes.
