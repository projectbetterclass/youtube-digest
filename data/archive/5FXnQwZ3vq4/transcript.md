# Mike Mentzer was right about the pump

<https://www.youtube.com/shorts/5FXnQwZ3vq4>

Don't make the mistake of gauging, or evaluating the success of any one of these workouts by the standard of feeling, whether or not you get sore or achieve a pump. The pump, of course, is only temporary and does not indicate that growth was stimulated. And if getting sore was necessary, I never would have won a physique title as I almost literally never got sore, usually only after a layoff. You can only evaluate the success of any one of these workouts [music] by whether or not you're stronger the next time you perform that workout.
