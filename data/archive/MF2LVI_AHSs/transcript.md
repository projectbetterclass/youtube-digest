# These curls aren’t worth it

<https://www.youtube.com/shorts/MF2LVI_AHSs>

Many people are now talking about how this is a quote unquote optimal biceps curl. And even though you might get a little bit of extra stretch on the biceps, to be honest, I don't really think it's worth it. I would much rather do something where my arm is at my side and where I can use an appreciable amount of load with an appreciable amount of stability on the back side of my arm. So don't sacrifice a theoretical better stretch outcome for something like load, stability, and easier progression.
