# Why do sprinters have big glutes?

<https://www.youtube.com/shorts/BDFHxjg6Ink>

Why is it that most sprinters have really well-developed glutes? Your glute max wraps underneath of the pelvis like a cable wraps around a pulley and pulls the leg straight downward and backward and then behind the body. And it does this in a really straight forward to backward direction. So basically, if you mimic these mechanics in the gym with something like a leg press or reverse lunge, you're going to substantially bias the glute max. The takeaway here is that you can essentially mimic sprinting positions and postures to recruit your glute max maximally.
