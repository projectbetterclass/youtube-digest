# STOP Doing DB Rows Like This

<https://www.youtube.com/shorts/eYNp2qP1sAc>

stop holding the dumbbell tilted upward like this on the dumbbell row doing so increases biceps involvement and decreases back engagement instead tilt the dumbbell slightly downward like this to reduce biceps involvement and increase back activation if you imagine that this is a dumbbell tipping it downward or upward will not directly affect recruitment of the arms or the back what does affect recruitment is how the direction of resistance influences these joints so imagine that this line represents the force of the dumbbell if you pull like this so that the line of force of the dumbbell is directly in line with the forearm and the elbow you'll recruit very little arm in relationship to the back but if you pull so that the line of resistance falls in the middle of the arm now all of a sudden the elbow muscles have this distance to act on the load and the shoulder muscles only have this distance to act on the load in other words when you pull like this the arms are much more recruited as compared to this version here and whether or not you tip the dumbbell downward or upward May indirectly affect this but what matters most is how the resistance actually relates to the joints to maximize for back recruitment in a dumbbell row just keep the line of resistance close to parallel to the forearm
