# Try this special lateral raise!

<https://www.youtube.com/shorts/C2gDuY958ww>

Do you get pain doing cable laterals with the cable in front of your body and also with the cable behind your body? If you do, instead try putting the cable all the way to the bottom of the rack and stagger your feet around the cable so that the load is going directly through your hips. When you do that, keep the stagger and basically raise at a diagonal angle, not quite forward and not directly out to the side, but somewhere in between. This will help you load the shoulder in a path that's probably a lot more naturally comfortable for your arm.
