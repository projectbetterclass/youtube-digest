Moments of Grace
¨ I am convinced that each of us is granted moments of 
grace, where, if we are open to learning, we find out 
what we are meant to do with our lives. 
¨ For me, one of those moments occurred in the second 
year of my MBA program at UCLA, when, cash poor, I 
decided to be a teaching assistant and the day that I 
walked in to teach my first session, I knew that I had 
found my calling. I was going to be a teacher, though I 
was not sure what I would be teaching or to whom. 
¨ As fate would have it, I found myself fascinated by 
finance, and with a PhD under my belt, I ended up as a 
professor at NYU's business school. 
¨ I am a teacher, not a professor, researcher or academic.

Valuation of the week
¨ My try: With both versions of the valuation class, I will also be 
posting what I call my valuation of the week, a company that I 
will value, with links to the excel spreadsheet and the story 
behind the value. 
¨ Your try: I encourage you, if you are taking the class (officially 
or unofficially) to take my valuation and make it their own, 
changing the story and the inputs, and then recording their 
valuations in a shared Google spreadsheet. 
¨ Crowd valuations: In a world where crowds decide what 
movies are successes (Rotten Tomatoes) and which 
restaurants we eat at (Yelp reviews), this is my version of 
crowd valuations. It is an optional exercise, but the more 
people who participate, the more fun we can have.

Final Thoughts
¨ I know that there are some who say that those who 
cannot do, teach, and I have been told that (or variants 
of it) multiple times. I don't mind the insult, since I have 
a thick skin, and I know that there is nothing else in the 
world I would rather do than teach. 
¨ I answer to no one (other than my wife), pick when I 
work (for the most part), get to chance to change how 
people think and make a decent living, doing so. 
¨ If you desire to manage other people's money, be an 
equity research analyst or investment banker or start 
and run your own company, I wish you the very best, but 
I know what I love doing, and I would be foolish to trade 
it in for more money or prestige. Life is too short!
