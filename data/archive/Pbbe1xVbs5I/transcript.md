# Speak for your work

<https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=Pbbe1xVbs5I>

when I first started performing magic the one area that I focused on the most was sleight of hand I spent all my time learning slaughterhand the technical skills the technical knowledge behind magic I fundamentally believed I had brilliant sleight of hand yet I had no successful magic career but then there was this other magician I remember his name was Magic Mike he didn't have the best light of hand but he has something else that magicians call Showmanship and Showmanship is just a fancy word for communication skills so I spent all of my time believing that technical skills is where it's at because the better I get technically the more successful I become the education system taught me this whereas Magic Mike he focused on his Showmanship his sleight of hand was but the thing is he was more successful than me and I was so frustrated because I'm like this is not fair I know more than you and it wasn't until I read a book Showmanship for magicians that completely changed my life because it taught me that you are only as good as you can communicate because people often say I'll let my work speak for itself why not do both let your work speak for itself and speak for your work too
