## 1. [13:29] Slide explaining DILUTION and ROIC/ROCE metrics for evaluating capital management

DILUTION
Always check what the management team was able to do with that new capital.
Are they investing it effectively? Check the ROIC and the ROCE.
ROIC = After-tax operating income / book value of invested capital
= How well the company utilises money from investors.

## 2. [16:02] Slide discussing Amazon's 2000 convertible bond offering and its strategic importance during the dot-com crash

DILUTION
Early in 2000, Amazon sold $672 million in convertible bonds to overseas investors.
The deal was completed just a month before the crash of the stock market, after which it became exceedingly difficult for any company to raise money.
Without that cushion, Amazon would almost certainly have faced the prospect of insolvency over the next year.
The Everything Store: Jeff Bezos and the Age of Amazon (Brad Stone, 2013)

## 4. [33:09] Slide on Share Dilution providing analysis framework with three key metrics

SHARE DILUTION
1. Watch how the shares outstanding changes over time.
- Are they selling a lot of stock?
- Are they keeping the shares outstanding steady?
2. Look at the cash flow statement.
- Proceeds from issuance of common stock
- Repurchases of common stock
3. Examine the average ROIC and ROCE over time.
