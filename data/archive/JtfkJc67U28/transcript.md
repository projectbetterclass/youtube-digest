# Fix Your Overhead Triceps Extension By Doing THIS!

<https://www.youtube.com/shorts/JtfkJc67U28>

Most people set up their overhead extensions in a way that is unnecessarily difficult and often times ends up being quite unstable cuz the rope shimies around. Things get weird, out of whack. So, here's a simpler way to do it. What I like to do is raise the height of the pulley around to the height of my neck. This isn't, you know, a hard rule, but it's what I like to start with. And then, basically what you're going to do is you're going to stick your butt back into the cable column. You're going to throw it back, so to speak, and then you're just going to dip over. And what this will feel like is it will just feel much more locked in. It's a lot easier to get in and out of. It feels a lot more stable. And as you can see, you can push to absolute failure and quote unquote beyond failure with little to no issues whatsoever. So, give this alternative setup a try. Make sure that when you end up in that bottom position that the cable is kind of scraping up against the back side of your shirt. And make sure that as you go through the exercise, your upper arms are overhead and are not moving from that position. Hope this helps.
