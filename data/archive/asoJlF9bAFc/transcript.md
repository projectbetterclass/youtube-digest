Now, a lot of people were giving this guy a hard time for throwing his weights, which I totally understand because it's very dangerous to do so, especially when you have people working out in close proximity to you. The weights could bounce, they could roll, hit somebody in the foot, whatever. It's not safe to do. I personally know a few people who have had weights dropped on their foot by other people in the gym resulting in a broken toe or toenails missing. So, yeah, do not do this. I will teach you guys the proper way to dismount a dumbbell bench press so that this doesn't have to happen. To set up, you're going to keep the weights on your thighs as you fall back. That way the legs are supporting the weights and keeping them stable as you descend, and then you're going to start your press from there. To dismount, you're going to reverse that by bringing your legs up, placing the weights on your legs, and then tipping forward. Then next step from there, very important, make sure you bring the weights back to the weight rack where you found them, keeping them in the proper order. Very, very important.
