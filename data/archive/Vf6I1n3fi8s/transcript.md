# Stop Doing THIS On Curls!

[Music] I see a lot of people doing curls like this without any sort of support underneath of their upper arm this is basically the equivalent of doing a leg curl without putting the thigh pad down onto your thighs and for reference if you can leg curl the entire weight stack it's going to be very difficult for you to even do one or two reps with literally a tenth of the load just because you can hold the weights out in front of your body and still actually do the curls doesn't necessarily mean it's a good idea to train the biceps and in the case of holding your arms out in front of the body you're almost going to double the amount of resistance on the shoulder as compared to the Elbow so instead of holding your arms out in front of your body just put something underneath of your arm like a preacher pad or a bench and your curls will be much more effective for training your biceps
