# My biggest fear (stand up comedy)

<https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=nuUBS7Rzbf8>

one of the things that scared me more than anything in this world and even though I'm a keynote speaker I do public speaking for a living the thing that scares me most is stand-up comedy nothing scares me more than a room filled with less than 10 people who are sitting there with the expectation that you're going to make them laugh gives me the heebie-jeebies so a couple of years ago I made the decision to face this fear and I jumped on stage for a five minute set and I tell you what this was one of the scariest things I did in my life say hello you know in traditional way that these people would say hello in our culture and it's hello man but the moment I did it and the moment I faced that fear I started to add to the bank of evidence that I am a courageous person and just by doing this I now fear stand-up comedy even no I'm kidding I'm no longer scared of it it doesn't frighten me as much before I was 10 out of 10 fearful now I'm about a six out of ten it's not as scary anymore and the more you start to do things like this the more you start to build that bank of evidence that you are someone that's courageous and as you start to get those badges and put them on yourself you'll start to feel more confident
