# DON'T do this on hamstrings curls

Point your toes inward, you'll target the semmitendinosis and semimebrosinosis. If you point your toes outward, you'll target the biceps feorous long head and short head. If you point your toes up, you'll work your hamstring muscles evenly. If you turn your toes inward on a seated leg curl, you'll target all your hamstrings. But if you turn your toes outward on a seated leg curl, you'll also target all your hamstrings. This notion that turning the foot inward and outward significantly alters hamstrings recruitment is based off a very lowresolution indirect evidence that really just records muscle activity. But here's the reality. All of your hamstrings bend your knee and your inner hamstrings attach to this side of the shin and your outer hamstrings attach to this side of the shin. So anytime that the hamstrings pull on one side of the shin, hamstrings on the other side of the shin need to make sure that the shin doesn't just rotate all over the place as you bend the knee. So instead of forcing your shin one way or another on the leg curl, just put your foot wherever it's most comfortable and wherever you feel strongest.
