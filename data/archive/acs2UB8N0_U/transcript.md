# Which bench angle is best for curls?

<https://www.youtube.com/shorts/acs2UB8N0_U>

Which angle is best when you're doing dumbbell preacher curls? Well, no single angle is best, but here's a simple way to think about the differences between angles. The higher the angle you use, the higher the resistance toward the top of the motion. The lower the angle you use, the higher the resistance toward the bottom of the motion. So, when you're doing preacher curls like this, it will be proportionally much harder toward the squeezed position rather than the stretched position. But if you use an angle like this, it will be much harder toward the bottom or stretched position as compared to the squeeze position. Both of these exercises are fantastic, and you should choose the one that makes the most sense for your goal. Personally speaking, I like to do one of each of these variations at different points in the week to emphasize different portions of the range of motion.
