# Why does knee cave happen?

why is it that people's knees cave inward whenever they push out of the bottom of a squat a lot of people seem to think that the knees caving inward is due to some weakness in the adductors of the glutes but this is probably not the case in reality your brain is just pulling your femur inward toward your hip socket so that you can dve directly down through the floor in other words if your stance is way outside of shoulder width to begin with your femur is just going to be dragged to a point where you can actually push downward with your glutes in your adductors as compared to keeping your leg out to the side where you would actually have to actively hold your leg outward so if there is a solution to KNE cave it's simply just to move your stance more underneath of you so you can press more straight upward and downward through the floor this is very similar to how people's elbows often flare out in a bench press so that they can get their arms directly underneath of the bar where they're going to be more efficient pressing the bar upward and flaring the elbow is much like the knees caving in is not necessarily a bad thing in many cases it's just going to make you stronger if your goal is to lift more weight
