## 4. [01:58] Excerpt from Warren Buffett's letter containing text about Berkshire's intrinsic value calculation and business philosophy

Berkshire will forever remain a financial fortress. In managing, I will make expensive mistakes of commission and will also miss many opportunities, some of which should have been obvious to me. At times, our stock will tumble as investors flee from equities. But I will never risk getting caught short of cash. In the years ahead, we hope to move much of our excess liquidity into businesses that Berkshire will permanently own. The immediate prospects for that, however, are not good: Prices are sky-high for businesses possessing decent long-term prospects. That disappointing reality means that 2019 will likely see us again expanding our holdings of marketable equities. We continue, nevertheless, to hope for an elephant-sized acquisition. I believe Berkshire's intrinsic value can be approximated by summing the values of our four asset-laden proves and then subtracting an appropriate amount for taxes eventually payable on the sale of marketable securities.
