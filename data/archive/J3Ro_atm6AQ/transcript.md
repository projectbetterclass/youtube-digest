What's the point in doing a hack squat like this as opposed to having your feet down on the platform? Putting your toes on the platform and allowing the knees to drive way forward is basically a way to turn a regular hack squat into a leg extension. It's different from a leg extension in one key way, which is that if you picture a normal leg extension, your hips are here and your leg is here. So this muscle, the rectus forous, is actually relatively shorter. But when you're on the hack squat and you allow the leg to come this way as you go through the knee extension, what happens is the rectus forous fully stretches at the hip instead of being more short as in a leg extension. So in other words, this variation is a great way to target the fully lengthened position of all of the quads, which the normal hack squat doesn't actually do. This isn't necessarily better. It's just more specific to the goal of targeting the most stretch position of the quads. And it's complimentary to the leg extension, which means that it may provide additional benefit that you wouldn't have if you only do the leg extension. If you're new to this motion, load it slowly so that you can get comfortable getting up on your toes and driving your knees forward.
