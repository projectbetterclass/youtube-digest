# How to deal with hate on social media

<https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=G_OXas1URps>

I've been creating content now for easily nine years if you scroll back on my Instagram or my Facebook you'll see that I've created content for a while and I've learned how to deal with hateful comments and there's a mindset that I adopt and I learned this from Joe Rogan love his podcast and Joe Rogan has a philosophy where he says when people leave a hateful comment on your video on your post that's them broadcasting to the world that they're going through a tough time in their lives because if you think about it if you're just scrolling through social media and you see a video and a 30 second video triggers you to type something extremely hateful and mean that just means that you're going through a terrible time in your life for you to have that much hate for a video and what you have to realize is the hateful comment that they left for you the negativity it doesn't have anything to do with you it has more so to do with them and their lives and their situation that they're currently in and that simple mindset shift has helped me look at these hateful comments with more empathy it doesn't trigger me anymore it doesn't make me think oh I'm gonna hate that person back I've become less like that and I've learned to look at it and come at it from a more empathetic point of view so whether whether you're a content creator or you're just living your life allow this simple little reframe to help you move through the online world and some of those hateful comments
