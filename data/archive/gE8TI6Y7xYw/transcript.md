# Why do you only feel your biceps during pulldowns?

<https://www.youtube.com/shorts/gE8TI6Y7xYw>

Why do you feel so much biceps recruitment whenever you're trying to train your back? If I'm doing a pulling motion and this yellow line represents the line of force the resistance, as I pull down you can see that this line of force stays pretty parallel to the forearm. As a consequence of this there's very low forces on the elbow and very high forces on the shoulder. But if I start to do the pull down in a way where my hand is moving more toward the middle of my arm, what you can see is that line of force changes so that it's farther from the elbow and closer to the shoulder. An exercise with this line of force will have a lot more force in the elbow and as a consequence the biceps as compared to if you had a line of force that was going right through the elbow. So whether you're doing a rowing type of motion or a pull down type of motion, keep the resistance parallel to the forearm throughout the whole time and you won't have a ton of biceps recruitment. Many people falsely attribute this to a lack of mind muscle connection in their back, when in reality the physics of the exercises just dictate that the forces travel more toward the elbow.
