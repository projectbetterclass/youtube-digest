# Should Your Feet Be Up or Down on Leg Extension?

<https://www.youtube.com/shorts/9ufHfMiR5cI>

When you're doing a leg extension, is it best to point your toes up toward your face or down away from your face? Well, let's look at what's actually changing when we do this. When you do this, what's mainly happening is that your ankle is bending this way. The only muscle that crosses the ankle joint that also acts on the knee is this thing right here. It's called the gastrocnemius, otherwise known as the outer calf. So, when you pull this upward like at the bottom of a seated calf press, you are stretching this outer calf more, which may actually make it harder for you to get to the top. On the other hand, if you actively really push your ankle down, the opposite happens where the outer calf shortens more, which may lead to a lower capacity for the outer calf to stabilize the back of the knee. So, in either one of these extremes where you're really cranking your ankle in either direction, you might just be adding more noise to the total picture than is necessary. I personally think that you should use one of these strategies if it's the only one that makes your knee feel good, but outside of that context, I would just relax your foot somewhere in the middle and let your foot kind of do whatever it wants to do so that you can really just focus on the knee motion and controlling the exercise with your quad.
