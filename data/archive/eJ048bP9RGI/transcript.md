my goodies my goodies goodies goodies why do people like to use a thumbless grip whenever they do LP pull Downs when you wrap your thumb around the bar you're naturally going to have to squeeze it most people also put the bar deeper into their Palm when they wrap the thumb and this does two different things number one it makes it more difficult to actually hold on to the bar number two it actually restricts the amount of pronation and supination that you have available in the forarm so when you use a thumbless grip it actually is easier to hold on to the bar and because your grip is slightly looser actually have a little bit more freedom of motion available at the forearm so that you can more easily direct your upper arm wherever is most comfortable and feels best for you I personally recommend using straps with a thumbless grip for most pulling motions to make your pulling motions more individualized for you
