# Improve Your Shoulder Function (NO STRETCHING)

<https://www.youtube.com/shorts/xx2ZVBLgrlc>

you're tired of trying to stretch your way into good shoulder Health here are three exercises that I find help a lot of people that I work with the first is a single arm cable y-rays to set this exercise up set the cable at around hip height grab the cable with one of your hands and raise your arm up and out to the side so that your whole arm is parallel to the cable this exercise is a great stimulus for the lower trap which is often neglected in a lot of rowing motions second exercise is a cable press around to set this exercise up set the cable at around hip height grab the cable with a single arm and keep it pinned to your forearm then grab something else for stability and press across the body toward the sternum make sure that you allow the shoulder blade to move around the body into full Pro traction and that you keep your palm toward your face the entire time this will also train the fully shortened position of the serratus anterior which is rarely done with any other exercise third exercise is a cable row where my arm is coming across the body in The Eccentric portion of the movement for this exercise you're going to want to make sure that your arm path is super tight to your body and that you allow your shoulder to come forward and around the body much like you did in the cable press around this exercise will force you to lengthen all of the different divisions of your lats as well as your rhomboids and some of your traps
