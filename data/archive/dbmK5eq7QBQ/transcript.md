# Shoulders hurt with dips? Try THIS!

<https://www.youtube.com/shorts/dbmK5eq7QBQ>

If you're like me and millions of other people, you've probably had shoulder pain with regular dips. And so here I'm going to provide two solutions for you. Number one, and this is my personal favorite, bring a bench behind you so that you can tip your torso forward. This will do two things. It'll set up the angle a little bit more specifically to your low pec. And guess what? You can load and load and load as much as you need. Now, an alternative to this, if you don't have the room or you can't drag a bench over, is just to pull your feet up in front of you. This will allow you to tip your torso just a little bit farther forward. There's a little bit of coordination involved, but for some of you it may feel much better.
