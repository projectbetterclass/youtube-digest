# Single-Arm Lat Pull-Down Tutorial

<https://www.youtube.com/shorts/3iTWghPtZSo>

Here's a simple guide on how to set up and execute one of my favorite lat exercises, the single-arm lat pulldown. Step one, slide your full body underneath the pad. Make sure that you're not all the way out here so that your knee is under the pad because that's going to be a super unstable. Step two, make sure that your opposite hand is firmly planted and slightly pulls up to the ceiling so that you're making sure that your torso is locked in. Step number three, raise your arm up and toward the cable so that you can approximate the correct angle here. In order to most target the lats, we want to do two things. We want to start with an arm path that kind of looks like this and then also make sure that stays nice and tight to the body the whole time. In terms of pulling, make sure that you're pulling initially from your armpit, following through with your elbow and your hand. Make sure that you're not making these two mistakes. One, which is pulling your hand toward your face using your biceps and your other elbow flexors. Two, which is arching the crap out of your back and also trying to side bend the weight as you pull it down.
