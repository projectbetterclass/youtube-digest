# Stop practicing SHY behaviors

<https://www.youtube.com/shorts/iLm8i4vZR0A>

You're not a shy person. You're a person practicing shy behaviors. Shyness is just a series of behaviors. After teaching communication for over a decade, I've learned that my shy students, they all practice the same shy behaviors. A quiet volume, small body language, poor eye contact, and they take up as little space as possible. Look, it's okay if you're a shy person because at some point in your life, those behaviors have served you. For me, when I was younger, I unconsciously developed shy behaviors which helped me avoid the bullies in school because English was my third language. But at some point, I realized that I can't be shy forever because it's robbing me of growth in my career and connection in my relationships. And this is the one thing I want you to understand. If being shy is just a set of behaviors, then so is being confident. What do you practice when you want to practice confident behaviors? Speak with more volume. Use larger gestures. And don't be afraid to take up more space because when you look more confident and you sound more confident, you'll gradually start to feel more confident.
