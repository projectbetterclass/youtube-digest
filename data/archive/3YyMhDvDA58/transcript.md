# Choose your training team based on what you can stay consistent with!

<https://www.youtube.com/shorts/3YyMhDvDA58>

Will a three-day program produce the same gains as a four and five-day program? Let me get this out of the way first and say that I am running a three-day program right now and that you can absolutely make phenomenal gains on a three-day program. However, if we're assuming that the three, the four, and five-day program were all programmed well by somebody like me, then if you're consistent with doing the four and five-day program, you'd likely make a bit better gains on those programs considering that you can train with a higher frequency, maybe even tolerate slightly higher volumes compared to a three-day program. Now, the question is how much more muscle are you going to gain on a four or five-day program in comparison to a three-day program? And the reality of it is it's likely not going to be that much more. There are diminishing returns when it comes to your volume. When you're adding more sets, you're not going to see double the growth from double the sets. When it comes to picking which training team that you want to join, I would base that more so on what you can stay consistent with rather than thinking that you're going to make so much more progress on the four or five-day training team. Only pick those teams if you can actually adhere to it each week.
