# How to isolate the lower traps

<https://www.youtube.com/shorts/5yvts6YTP9A>

If you have a really tough time targeting this part of your back, your lower traps, here's an exercise I absolutely love. I don't see a lot of people doing. Set up a cable around the height of your hip and stand in front of the cable with the cable pulling your arm across your body. A big point of emphasis with this is going to be for you to allow your arm and your shoulder to go forward in the stretch position when your biceps hit your chest, and then shoving your shoulder backward as you raise. This will target a blend of not only the middle and the rear delt, but a huge amount of the motion is going to be controlled in the bottom by the lower traps because that's what's going to control the shoulder blade going forward and tipping anteriorly and then going backward and tipping posteriorly. You can think about your shoulder blade and your arm kind of functioning as a seesaw in the bottom position where the lower traps are going to be the thing to really tip and control the shoulder blade forward and backward as you go to raise outward.
