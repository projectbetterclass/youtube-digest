# Why does hand position change biceps recruitment?

<https://www.youtube.com/shorts/MFQ5UfFCu6M>

why do you feel your biceps doing this kind of curl and your forearms doing this kind of curl because of where the biceps attaches the biceps do something called supination which in the context of a dumbbell curl is like turning your palm toward the ceiling we put the Palm in a neutral position so that the thumb is facing the ceiling what happens is that the biceps actually lose leverage to bend the elbow and other muscles like the brachialis and the bracho radialis gain leverage this happens because these other muscles are in a better position to now move the elbow as compared to the biceps which are in a better position when the Palm is facing the ceiling
