# Single Arm Lat Pulldown

<https://www.youtube.com/shorts/4-LMlgG3EXQ>

The one- arm lap pull down is by far the best way to train the lat under a single arm position. Most people do it wrong. This is how you do it right. Step number one, stabilize yourself. Step number two, lift your chest up. Step number three, pull your shoulders back. Step number four, pull with your elbow all the way in. Come back up. Once you get to this position, stretch the lat. The reason why you want to train movements in a single arm position is unbeknownst to you, one arm is working harder than the other, and you don't know which one. By splitting it up, you very quickly find out which one it is. Then you can actually target the stronger side and push that one. By doing so, your weaker side will catch up and you'll fix your imbalance. The common mistake most people make is they train the weaker side first, but it actually limits their potential strength over the whole move. and your strong side first.
