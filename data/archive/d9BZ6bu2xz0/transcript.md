# Why Did Arnold Row Like This?

<https://www.youtube.com/shorts/d9BZ6bu2xz0>

why did Arnold do his bent over rows like this with a rounded back when you do a bent over row like this the weight ends up being really far from the bottom of the spine in the hips what this means is that you end up having to do a lot of extra work with the hips and the spine as opposed to the pulling muscles but if you round over what happens is that load moves closer to the spine and the hips which may end up being a little bit more appropriate for training the shoulders some of you may think that rounding your back like this would be bad for your back but as long as you're prepared to handle the positions there's nothing inherently wrong with doing it this way and rounding your back over rather than keeping your spine super rigid will increase the likelihood that you actually make your rowing tissues the rate limiter as opposed to your hips in your spine
