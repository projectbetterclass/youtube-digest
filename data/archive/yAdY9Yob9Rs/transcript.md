# How many sets should you do?

<https://www.youtube.com/shorts/yAdY9Yob9Rs>

How many sets should I do is the wrong question. It's the wrong question for a couple of reasons. The first reason it's the wrong question is because it assumes that there could be one answer like five per week for a muscle and then that's the end of it. But it also assumes that if you came to some reasonable answer like okay, five per week per muscle group is what's appropriate for me right now. It assumes that that's not going to change. When in reality, there's only one guarantee when it comes to the lifting journey, which is that the things that you do and how much of it you do will change. The exercise, the number of sets that feels perfect today will not be the same way in a month, a year, or 2 years, or 5 years once you've become an essentially different organism. So the question of how many sets is a lot less helpful than how many sets seems appropriate for this given muscle group for me and my goals and my constraints right now. And guess what? Even if you get to a position where you've made a ton of progress doing a certain number of sets, that doesn't mean that that number of sets is going to be appropriate or even the exercises you're doing are going to be appropriate moving forward. The reason this is the case is because as you progress, new problems appear. And when new problems appear, different strategies may need to be used. And so even if a strategy works, number one, you can't know if that strategy will continue to work or will continue to be good for you. And number two, you also don't have the counterfactual, which means that if five sets per week per muscle group worked really well for you, you don't know whether three would have worked better for whatever reason or if 10 would have worked better or 15 would have worked better. So there's just no certainty that you can actually come to with answering this question. But what I find as a general trend is that beginner lifters and people who are closer to beginners want to know the answer to this question a lot more than advanced lifters. So, that's something to consider.
