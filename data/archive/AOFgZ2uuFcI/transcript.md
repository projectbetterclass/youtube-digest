My vocal coach used to say this, Melissa Trinkman from Southern California, an opera singer. She used to say, "Your voice is your personality." That's how people perceive your personality. When the people give you compliments on your personality, they're giving you compliments on how you're using your voice. So, that's why when you shift the way you communicate, it indicates a shift in personality. That's why it scares the people that we love cuz they they start to think, "Oh no, what's happened?" What's happened to this beautiful person that I've known that's stayed the same for this entire period and now they're changing. That change scares people. And if you want your loved ones to be supportive of you trying out new behaviors, you have to prime them. So, what you say is you say say, "Hey honey, look, I I just watched this great workshop by a gentleman named Vin Jang uh link in the descriptions. I've been lazy with my voice for the longest time in my life. And I want to give you more of me. I want to give you more energy. So, if you see me starting to try to use my voice differently, it's because I'm trying to be better for you. I'm trying to be better for the kids. I hope you encourage me as I as I try to be better." So, when you prime it with love, kindness, and that kind of compassion, then all of a sudden it changes the way people view change.
