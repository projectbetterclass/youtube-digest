# How to fix your rope crunch:

<https://www.youtube.com/shorts/G28cKMpTSU8>

this is the number one mistake that I see people making on cable crunches with cable crunches the main goal is to train the ABS which means that we actually need to allow for bending and extending of the spine what we don't want to do is basically just hinge at the hips which would mean that mainly the hip flexors are pulling us down as the ABS just stabilize the trunk so instead sit back fix your hip angle and make sure that you're moving primarily through the trunk so that your spine is bending and straightening throughout the range of motion it will be much more specific to the ABS as opposed to a combination of the ABS and the hip flexors
