# A little-known rotator cuff fact

<https://www.youtube.com/shorts/SsJK9nzmj-c>

So, not many people actually know this, but the the rotator cuff is a special snowflake muscle group, uh, specifically the infraspanatus actually, where if you just choose exercises that you can't really load, you can't really progress, and if you don't put any effort into them whatsoever, um, that the muscle group will just magically adapt. So if you want to uh grow muscles of your rotator cuff specifically in action or rotation, treat it like any other muscle group. Uh choose an exercise that is loadable that you can progress over long periods of time and take those exercises at or close to failure if you want those groups to grow stronger and get bigger.
