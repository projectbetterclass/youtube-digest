# Incline Dumbell Press

<https://www.youtube.com/shorts/4ZpdkkLJhFY>

The incline dumbbell bench press is by far the best way to develop your pecs. Most people do it wrong. I'm going to show you how to do it right. So, the most important step is to warm up. Grab a weight that's light. When we're warming up, take the light weight to its furthest stretch position. Hang out here until you no longer feel that stretch in your chest. Once you no longer feel the stretch in the muscle, perform as many reps as it takes to get physical heat in the muscle. Aim for around 15 to 20 reps. The next thing we do is feeder sets. Unlike the warm-up set, the goal is to do one or two reps so we can acclimate to our working set. So, let's say my working weight is 40 kilos. Warm up on 10, do two or 20, two or 30, and I'm right and ready to go for 40. Now that you're on your working weight, let's focus on the setup. I like to have one foot back and one foot forward. This allows me to stabilize, and this allows me to drive. Start the movement from the deepest stretch you can possibly go. And instead of pushing the weight up, squeeze chest together and upper back into the bench. That's about it.
