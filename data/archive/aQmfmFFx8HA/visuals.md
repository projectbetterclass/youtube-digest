## 4. [03:52] Financial Times article text with quote from Eswar Prasad on China's economic risk and deflation concerns.

The Chinese economy is now at serious risk of sliding into a deflationary episode that could spark a self-reinforcing downward spiral in growth and private sector confidence, said Eswar Prasad, a China finance expert at Cornell University. The government needs to act quickly and decisively to put a floor on growth and limit deflation before matters get out of hand. China has bucked the global inflation trend of other large economies, many of which unleashed sweeping stimulus efforts during the pandemic. Beijing, by contrast, sought to control the virus through a three-year zero-Covid policy.
