Pre-report Narrative
Amazon is a "Field of Dreams" company, 
growing revenues by selling products & 
services at cost or below, with the intent of 
using that market power to extract proﬁts 
in the future.
Amazon's Earnings Report on January 28, 2016
1. Revenue growth for most recent twelve months was 20.25%, an 
astonishing number, given that the revenues last year were $88 billion.
2. Amazon posted its third proﬁtable quarter in a row, a departure from 
its past history of reporting losses.
3. The company's guidance suggests that growth will continue to be in 
the low single digits next year.
4. The Web services business (Cloud) is growing fastest, with 
revenues increasing 69% over the prior year to reach $2.38 billion with 
an operating margin of 28.5%.
Post-report Narrative
Amazon continues on its path of revenue 
growth, but it is starting on its pathway to 
proﬁtability, at least in its media and cloud 
businesses, perhaps setting up trade offs 
between revenue growth and proﬁts.
