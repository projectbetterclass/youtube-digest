# I’m an OG goblet squat hater 💀

<https://www.youtube.com/shorts/RulbfGfvNOw>

I was fully on board with your leg days until I read that you're doing elevated goblet squats. I think that goblet squats are one of the most overrated leg exercises that anybody could possibly do. Now, could there be a good reason for somebody doing them if they're at home? Maybe, but probably not. If you have two dumbbells, you're much better off doing a suitcase carry with the dumbbells on both sides. This is because your legs are going to be much, much, much, much, much, much stronger than your arms are going to be. So, if you're doing a goblet squat, you have to hold the weight that you're using in front of you like this. So, you're much more likely to fatigue first because of your arms, your traps that are having to hold and keep that weight in place rather than your legs. What I most often see though is that people are doing a goblet squat and they're just not able to get anywhere near their full potential in terms of loading or the reps that they're able to do because of that. You have a leg press at your gym, I would suggest using the leg press instead or even just doing a barbell squat, a Smith machine squat, kind of better options. You're doing split squats on another day, you could just make that quad focus instead. So, if you have goblet squats anywhere within your program and you want to make much better leg progress, I would suggest switching that out. Hope this helps and as always make sure to like and follow for more.
