# Don't make THIS lat training mistake!

<https://www.youtube.com/shorts/41zHRIcRo0M>

Many people seem to think that this is a lower lat movement, but that is not true. This is predominantly an upper lat movement. Yes, the lower lats will be involved, but the reason that the upper lats are the prime movers is because the resistance of the exercise is pulling the arms in a forward direction. You need a simple way to think about this, just think about the direction of resistance acting parallel and opposite to the fibers of the upper lats. If you wanted to target the lower lats more specifically, you would need more of a vertical direction of resistance so as to align the lower lat fibers with that direction as well. So, horizontal pulling motions equals horizontal or upper lats. Vertical pulling motions equals vertical or lower lats.
