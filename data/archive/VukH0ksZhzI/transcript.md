# How to fix over-active traps

how do you fix overactive traps whenever you're doing lateral raises instead of using dumbbells just use the cable where you can actually load the bottom end of the range a lot more and if you don't have access to cables instead of doing regular dumbbell laterals use a single dumbbell lean against something and do them single arms so you can actually load the bottom end of the range so here's how this works if the resistance that you're using is always pulling your shoulder downward your upper traps and your middle traps are always going to have to hold your shoulder girdle upward so the key is really just to use a resistance that's pulling your arm toward your body rather than pulling your whole shoulder gdle downward so whether it's a cable or a dumbbell angle the resistance this way so you can just move with the middle delt as opposed to a resistance that's this way where you have to use a combination of the middle D and all this trap stuff so fixing your overactive traps is much less about actually doing anything from a muscular perspective and just changing the resistance so that you don't have to actually activate them in the first place
