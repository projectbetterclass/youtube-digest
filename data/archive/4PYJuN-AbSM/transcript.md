# The #1 Pressing Mistake (& how to fix)

<https://www.youtube.com/shorts/4PYJuN-AbSM>

This is one subtle mistake that can completely ruin your ability to train your chest in any press. And the mistake is putting the bar or handle too high up in your hand. Just think about it this way. If you have a bar or handle and it is really deep in your palm and it's stacked directly over your wrist, it doesn't want to bend your wrist in any direction. But if the handle or bar is actually positioned higher up here, do you all see how now the handle actually wants to pull my wrist backward like it's trying to crank my wrist this way? Now, all of a sudden, there's a ton of strain of all these muscles on the front side of the forearm. And in addition, you can't push directly into the bar because you're constantly having to manage the position of your hand. So, here's a simple trick that I like to use whenever I'm pressing. First, dig the handle or bar as deep into your palm as possible without actually wrapping your fingers around the bar. When you do this, and you dig your hand in, the bar will be forced to already center itself over the top of your wrist. And once the bar is stacked appropriately over the wrist, all you need to do is just gently wrap your fingers to secure it. During my warm-up sets, I'll actually not even wrap my fingers fully around the handles. I wouldn't recommend doing this with any amount of weight that you would find to be dangerous. It's a simple, reliable way to make sure that the handle or bar is placed in the appropriate position stacked over the wrist.
