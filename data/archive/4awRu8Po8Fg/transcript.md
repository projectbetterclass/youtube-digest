what's the difference between an incline curl and a preacher curl at the same bench angle if you use a bench angle for the incline curl that allows your arms to be roughly 90° from the floor you'll be able to stretch both of the biceps significantly more than in the preacher curl at the bottom portion of the range of motion there's very little resistance on the elbow and at the top portion of the range of motion there's a lot more resistance on the elbow in other words in the standard incline girl the weight is much lighter at the bottom and heavier at the Top If you do a preacher curl at the same bench angle your arm will be in front of your body so both of the biceps will stretch significantly less the way that this preacher curl is set up the resistance is much more challenging at the bottom and much lighter at the top so overall the incline curl does stretch the biceps more from a muscle length perspective but from a strength perspective it's actually heavier where we're weaker okay of the preacher curl both of the biceps are less stretched from a muscle length perspective but from a resistance profile perspective the motion is much heavier when the elbow is straighter meaning that it's heavier where we're a little bit stronger and in addition it's lighter where we're weaker ultimately which exercise you choose should depend on what your goals are and what other exercises
