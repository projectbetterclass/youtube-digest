# Differences between the overhead triceps extension and a push down

what's the difference between a triceps extension with your arm over your head versus at your side the only part of the triceps that actually is influenced by shoulder range of motion is the long head of the triceps which attaches to the shoulder blade because of where it attaches on the shoulder blade when we Bend our elbow and actually stretch our arm up and overhead that long head gets stretched a lot more than the other two heads and when we do regular extensions at the side it's not as stretched because the long head of the triceps is stretched more in the overhead position it'll actually contribute a little bit more to the motion this is because muscles are generally stronger when they're more stretched in addition when we do a normal triceps extension the force of the cable wants to pull our shoulder girdle up which basically means that the lats and the pecs have to resist that motion more whereas when we do the overhead extension the force wants to really pull us downward instead of upward so muscles like the upper and middle traps as well as the pecs on the front side of the body play a bigger role in stabilizing this motion whether you're doing an extension with your arm at your side or with your arm overhead all of the triceps will get a very meaningful degree of stimulus but because the overhead position stretches the long head just a little bit more it's likely that the long head will receive a little bit more stimulus than the other two heads in the overhead position
