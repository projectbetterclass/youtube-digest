# Breaking down Jalen Brunson's workout

<https://www.youtube.com/shorts/476cY1KirCA>

Let's break down Jalen Brunson the champs workout. First he's going to start off with two ankle and foot related drills. The first is a mobilization where he basically just has his foot suspended over something where he's working through what's called supination and pronation as well as ankle inversion and eversion where this backbone is kind of pivoted. Then he's basically going to stand on a surface where he's actually forced to train the muscles associated with that range. As you can imagine the foot's ability to react to changing forces is super super important for any sport. Then it looks like he's going to mobilize two things. One is the spine and then two is a wrist extension isometric where the front side of the forearm is being stretched. From there he's going to move into shoulder external rotation with a cable where he's training the muscles in the backside of the shoulder. Mainly the infraspinatus, the teres minor and some of the rear delt. Then there he's going to go into a single arm pullover and he's going to be working the lats, the teres major and even some of the lower portions of the chest. This setup is specifically really heavy toward the top so it's a great way to challenge end range shoulder motion. Then he's going to move on to a pure scapular retraction exercise where the shoulder blade is basically being pulled toward the spine. This is going to train all those muscles between the shoulder blade and the spine like the rhomboids and the middle traps. And this is a really really good drill for making your shoulder blades move a little bit more fluidly. Then he moves on to the good old pushup and the good old pullup where with the pushup he's going to be training the chest, the front delts and the triceps. And then with the pulldown he's basically going to be hitting the entire back minus some of the traps as well as the muscles like the biceps and the brachialis and the brachioradialis which bend the forearm. Then he's going to finish off with some rows to train the entire back including the traps, some overhead hollow holds to train the traps as well as the abdominals and then a neck extension isometric where he's pushing his neck back into something to train the muscles on the backside of the neck.
