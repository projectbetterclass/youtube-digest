# Pec deck fly set up

<https://www.youtube.com/shorts/7R_yw1x9A88>

Pec deck flies don't suck. You're just doing them wrong. Let's go over some of the most common mistakes that I see on the pec deck fly that you're probably doing. First things first, if the seat height is not set properly when you're doing your pec deck flies, then it's not going to feel good. Adjust the seat height so that your elbow and hand are slightly below shoulder height. Whoa, whoa, whoa, don't just muscle it out of the start with your heavy working weight. Instead, grab one handle at a time then rotate into position. And we're done, right? Nope. Instead of having your arms extended like this, allow for a bend in the elbow as you're coming back. This is going to help with taking some of that tension off of the biceps as you're coming back through the eccentric. This is better, but we're still not quite there. Instead of thinking about the hands coming together, think about driving your bicep into the side of your pecs. This will train your pecs through a larger range of motion, so make sure at the end of the fly that your arms are fully extended. Honorable mention, a common mistake that I see from people who like to keep their arms extended when they're doing their pec deck flies is that they have the arms rotated incorrectly. Instead, you want to make sure that your elbows are pointed back in the same direction that the machine is moving. This should feel much better on your elbows and shoulders, especially with all that big boy weight that you're using. Hope this helps, and as always, make sure to like and follow for more.
