The pec deck is by far the best way to grow your chest. Most people do it wrong. I'll show you how to do it right. Step number one is you want to take this pin as far back as your chest will allow it. Step number two, lift your chest up, feel the tension in your chest, and aim [music] to squeeze the chest to move the weight. Step number three is you want to perform the movement and stay in the same shape. So, as you're performing the movement, it'll start to get hard. What you don't want to do is move your chest to get the weight. You want to stay in the shape and keep pushing even if it's hard. Last step is [music] ensuring that you take this to partial failure. Meaning the the movement doesn't end when you can complete a rep. The movement ends when you can no longer initiate the movement. And that is how you grow your chest with a pecly.
