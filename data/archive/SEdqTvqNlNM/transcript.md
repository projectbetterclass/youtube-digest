# Why are trap bar deadlifts easier than straight bar?

<https://www.youtube.com/shorts/SEdqTvqNlNM>

Why are you so much stronger on hex bar deadlift compared to barbell deadlifts? Reason number one is simply range of motion. The handles on a trap bar are higher up off of the ground as compared to a barbell, so you're starting from a higher hip position and as a consequence, the range of motion is actually shorter, so you have to do less work. Reason number two, the amount of resistance on the hips and low back is actually greater for the same amount of load in the barbell variation because the barbell has to stay in front of your shin and as a consequence, your hips have to stay farther away from the bar. Reason number three is that the quads can contribute significantly more to the trap bar deadlift. This is because with the trap bar deadlift, we can actually move the resistance direction behind the knee, meaning that the quads can actually contribute to vertical motion of the bar. So, decreased range of motion, decreased resistance to the hips and the spine for the same amount of load, and increased contribution of the quads all make you appear much stronger with the trap bar as opposed to the barbell.
