# Why I let my elbows flare on overhead extensions

<https://www.youtube.com/shorts/1diIxRXhFZM>

There actually is a reason that I allow my elbows to flare out whenever I'm doing my overhead extensions. The long head of the triceps, the biggest head of the tricep, originates on the infraglenoid tubercle of the scapula. So, by allowing my elbows to flare out, this in theory is going to provide a better hypertrophy stimulus, a better growth stimulus. This is because the long head will be better aligned in that position with its origin on the scapula. I also find that it tends to feel a bit better on my shoulders compared to trying to tuck them in.
