# How to leg press for GLUTES

<https://www.youtube.com/shorts/2vz8_c5cePM>

Other than the fact that this chick is smoking hot, the most impressive thing about this is her absolutely awesome glute focused leg press technique. So, the major features of a really nice glute focused leg press are as follows. Number one, she's getting real close to what we call full hip flexion, which is basically closing the angle between this bone and this bone. Everyone's hip flexion is a little bit different. Don't be concerned if your hip doesn't go all the way up here, it's not a problem. Just do what's comfortable for you. The second thing is that her legs are not super wide out to the side. It's not that wide is bad, but doing a wide leg press out here will involve more of the inner thigh muscles as compared to the glutes. So, if you really want a glute focus, imagine pulling your knee up to your chest, not outward outside of your shoulder. And then number three, if you think about the force and the force direction of the foot plate as this thing, what you want to imagine is a force direction that is roughly going directly through the shin for a lot of the range of motion. This makes it so that the force direction is closer to the knee, meaning lower resistance on the knee, and farther from the hip, meaning more resistance on the hip. So, force direction through the knee roughly, and then a not bad starting point for this setup is to try to keep your shin roughly 90° from the foot plate as a good starting point.
