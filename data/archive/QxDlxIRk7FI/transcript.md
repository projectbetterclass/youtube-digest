When you step too far forward without any kind of lean, the force of the cable now wants to pull your entire body backward. And this not only makes it much harder for your shoulder to stabilize the motion so that you can just perform a triceps extension, but you're also continuously being pulled off of balance from your feet every time you try to initiate a rep. So instead, simply stand as close as possible to the pulley and imagine that the cable is scrubbing up against the fibers of your shirt. Now, you might have noticed one thing about the way that I perform the overhead extension, which is that I don't really fully lock my elbows. And this is for two reasons.
