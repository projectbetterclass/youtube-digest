# Getting jacked is easy and lazy af

<https://www.youtube.com/shorts/sYDkrvnhXrs>

Did you know that most of getting jacked in the gym is sitting on your ass? Yeah, I'm not joking. You are supposed to be waiting two to three full minutes in between your sets when you're weightlifting. Yeah, and actually if you don't wait that time period, you're going to be leaving up to almost half of your potential gains on the table. If I'm in the gym for an hour and a half, usually about an hour and seven minutes of that is sitting around. She's right, but a lot of people hear this and completely miss the point. Just because you take longer rest periods doesn't mean you're going to make better gains. The entire point of resting at least two to three minutes is so you can properly recover and perform the next set at your full potential, but that only matters if the previous set was hard enough to begin with. If you didn't actually get close to failure, it doesn't matter whether you rest one minute or five, you're just sitting around longer between easy, less effective sets. So, rather than worrying about exact rest times, focus on training harder first and foremost. Then just rest as long as you need to feel fully recovered, and if you really did push with high effort, it'll usually fall within that range automatically on most exercises. And if you try to jump back in faster, you'll usually feel it right away. Your heart rate and breathing will still be elevated, you'll have lingering muscle burn, maybe even a bit of nausea if it's a leg day, and all of that will contribute to larger performance drop-offs from set to set. So, yeah, most of your workout should be spent resting, but only because you're pushing your sets intensely enough that you have no other choice.
