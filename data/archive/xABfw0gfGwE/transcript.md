# Don't Make THIS Back Training Mistake

This can be a fantastic exercise to train the lats, but it runs into one major issue, and that issue is stability. If you're trying to pull a lot of weight in this position, it's very, very easy just to get toppled over as you pull. So, here are some quick solutions. The first one is to try to place your foot up against the cable stack if you can. This is going to be a step up from the first version, but it's still not going to be that stable. A step up from this would be if you were able to go on a horizontal row station where you could put one of your legs up against the foot platform so that you weren't toppling over as you pull. But a better option than either of those would be a situation where you could use both your hands and your legs. Here's one example where I'm grabbing the cable on one side of a cable column, moving to the other so that I can use my hand up against the cable stack and my same-side leg to brace up against the floor. You could also do this variation on a chest-supported row machine or with the cable and a bench setup because both of those are highly stable and effective options. So, in summary, if you're going to do a single-arm row, make sure you can use your hand, your foot, or both.
