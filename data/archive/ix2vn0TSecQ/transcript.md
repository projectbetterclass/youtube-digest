# Eating Carbs At Night = Fat Gain?

<https://www.youtube.com/shorts/ix2vn0TSecQ>

You can eat as many carbs as you want at night and still lose fat as long as you're doing this one thing. And no, it doesn't involve apple cider vinegar shots, a blood glucose monitor, sinking your meals with the lunar cycle, or whatever other biohacking the latest podcast bro and blue light blocking glasses told you. This recent study took 42 trained men and split them into three groups. One group ate all their carbs earlier in the day before training and none after. The other two groups spread their carbs out with one eating slow digesting carbs at night and the other fast digesting. So, which strategy came out on top? Well, the group that ate no carbs in the evening lost fat, gained muscle, and improved performance, and the group that spread their carbs out achieved the same results. This is just one study, but it does line up with most of the research showing that as long as total calories are matched, carbohydrate timing has no meaningful impact on body composition. It's not about when you eat your carbs, it's about how much you're eating overall. No, having some rice with your dinner won't magically sabotage your results, regardless of what ketogenic Karen or low carb Larry 69 tried to tell you. If you're still in a calorie deficit for the day as a whole, then you'll still lose fat.
