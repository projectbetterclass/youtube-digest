# STOP doing this for Glutes

<https://www.youtube.com/shorts/vVz1LFmJN4I>

Seen a lot of people use the cable pull through specifically for training glutes and I think it's a terrible exercise for loading the glutes specifically because of the direction of the loading and what it actually requires from a muscular effort standpoint. All that the pull through actually loads is basically your ability not to fall over as well as some of your spinal extensors. So instead of using the pull through to train glutes, what I would do is use things like single leg leg presses, reverse lunges and and even RDLs instead. If you want to actually train a short position movement, you could do something like a cable kickback or even something like a hip thrust, but get rid of the cable pull through.
