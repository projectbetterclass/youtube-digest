# Am I happy with my physique? No.

<https://www.youtube.com/shorts/MuLUFSONlK4>

Am I happy with my physique? No. And the sad truth is I was probably happier with it whenever I looked like this. I was always a small kid growing up and about a year into lifting I had added some muscle mass, but my standards for what was big were the same as they were in high school. Now that I actually work in the fitness industry, I'm hyper aware of the way that I look, honestly to an unhealthy level. So if you've ever felt similar to this, then just know that you're not alone and it's actually very common when training for aesthetics. You start lifting, your physique improves, but as you progress further and further, the bar for what you consider to be an impressive physique just keeps moving along with it. Now ultimately it's just human nature to never be fully satisfied and to keep striving for improvement. There's nothing wrong with that, but just make sure to balance it out by keeping in mind where you started and actively giving yourself credit for the progress you're making along the way. It's really easy to get lost in the process and take it all for granted. I'm as guilty as anyone when it comes to this and it's something you really need to make a conscious effort to remember. And not only that, but also recognize that the simple fact that you train hard and consistently, your calories and food choices are under control, you're at a healthy body fat level and you carry enough muscle to where it's obvious that you work out, that already puts you ahead of 95% plus of the population as far as fitness is concerned. By all means push to be the very best you can be if that's your goal, but don't forget to zoom out and keep the overall picture in mind, too.
