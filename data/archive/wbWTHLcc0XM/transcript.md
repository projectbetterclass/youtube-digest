The lats don't even benefit from stretch mediated hypertrophy though, right? The current evidence that we have on that topic suggests the lats do not benefit from that. However, I don't think that's a good reason not to train the lats through their full active range of motion. The mid to low lats have their best leverage overhead, so working through that range of motion is still going to be very beneficial.
