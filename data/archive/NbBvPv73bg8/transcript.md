# Barbell Rows aren’t bad but…

<https://www.youtube.com/shorts/NbBvPv73bg8>

I would think, or at least, I would hope that nobody's under the impression that you cannot grow your back with bent-over barbell rows. But, just because you can do something, it doesn't always make it the best choice. In comparison to chest-supported rowing, the bent-over barbell row is just much less efficient for growing the upper back or lats. Chest-supported rowing is much more stable, so you'll be able to recruit more muscle fibers for whatever muscles you're trying to work. You're also much less likely to fail due to other muscles that you are not trying to work, like your erectors or core as a whole. It's also going to be much easier to maintain your form leading up to failure with these highly stable movements and keep tension on the target tissue. And if you don't believe me, then just load up a chest-supported row using the same amount of shoulder flexion that you would have with a bent-over barbell row and same arm path, and see just how much more you can do on the chest-supported row. For me, I can easily load up three plates on a chest-supported row, but if I were to try and maintain the same torso position using that same weight on a bent-over barbell row, it would be much more difficult to do without form aggression. Hope that helps, and as always, make sure to like and follow for more efficient training tips.
