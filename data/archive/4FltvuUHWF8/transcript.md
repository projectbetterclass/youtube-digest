# Why did Ronnie Coleman press like this?

<https://www.youtube.com/shorts/4FltvuUHWF8>

why did bronnie Coleman do his dumbbell presses like this the bottom of the dumbbell press the weight is far from your joint so it's heavily loaded but at the top of the press the weight Stacks over the joints meaning that they're a lot less loaded so staying in this bottom half of the range keeps the PCS in the front delts heavily loaded the entire time whereas when you lock out at the top you gain an ability to rest in between reps so by staying just in the bottom half you're keeping tension on the pecs throughout the entirety of the range of motion this would not however be the case if you were doing something like a cable fly where the cables were angled outward because in the locked position the cable still pulling the arm in an outward direction making the arm still loaded so if you're going to do half reps on any exercise just make sure that it makes sense relative to the context
