# Don't Do THIS On Lat Pull-Downs?

<https://www.youtube.com/shorts/e6wx7w5Ps80>

don't do this think elbows to floor slow and controlled ining forward and backward as you do a pull down is a completely fine thing to do and in fact it may be more beneficial relative to your goals for example if you wanted to Target the lats then yes I would advise staying more upright and pulling straight down to your side but if you wanted to Target a combination of the lats and the upper back then leaning backward as you pull will actually allow you to make at a little bit more of a high row as compared to a strict pull down so if your goals are to Target more of the lats I would recommend staying a little bit more upright with the arms in front of the body but if you want to distribute the stimulus a little bit more evenly between the lats and the upper back then leaning forward and backward as you pull is a great strategy
