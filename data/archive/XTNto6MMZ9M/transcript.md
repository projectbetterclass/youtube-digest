[Music] dumbbell rotations can be a great way to train the forearms but here's a simple way to make it even more effective instead of putting your hand in the middle of the dumbbell offset the dumbbell in your hand toward the pinky side if you put the dumbbell in the middle of your hand one side of the weight will just offset the other but if you shift it toward the pinky side the weight will actually try to twist you toward one side so that when your palm is facing the ceiling your wrist will actually be pulled this way and so that when you turn your wrist over the dumbbell will actually be pulling it in the opposite direction if you keep the dumbbell in the middle of the hand and you just rapidly twist like this you're not going to have substantial direct loading that would actually be pulling your wrist in either direction and if you want to be even more specific to the forearms you can grab a regular cable handle and offset the cable to one side of the wrist this way you'll be directly loading rotation of the forearm the entire time so that you can Target each of these motions specifically as opposed to just doing something that's kind of in the middle
