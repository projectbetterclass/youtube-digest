# Building muscle isn't that hard

<https://www.youtube.com/shorts/whyD8cSX8uE>

Part of me dies inside every time I see some bodybuilder or influencer acting like building muscle is this brutally difficult thing where you have to suffer and struggle and revolve your whole life around your training and diet. I know I'll get some pushback for saying this, but building muscle isn't actually that hard. It really isn't. It's only hard for people who make it unnecessarily hard. They fall for the whole more is better mentality and now they're beating themselves into the ground six days a week with marathon workouts, high reps, drop sets, super sets, force traps, all this extra work that feels harder and takes more time, but ironically produces worse results because they push themselves past their ability to recover and adapt optimally. The truth is that three workouts per week for about an hour, training to around one rep short of failure on a handful of sets per muscle in low to moderate rep ranges, you can literally build a top-tier physique if you just do that consistently for a few years on a properly structured program. That's how I've trained for basically my entire lifting career. It's the same method we use inside TrainWithUs and I get messages from members every single day telling me they're seeing better gains in less time and without the extra fatigue, soreness, and joint stress. Now, if you want to do a bit more four or even five days a week is still solid, and no matter what, you still need to show up, train hard, and be consistent. I'm not saying this is effortless, but it doesn't have to be this constant daily war with yourself, either. Hypertrophy training really isn't that deep.
