# Fix your leg extension instantly!

<https://www.youtube.com/shorts/72OZzVRTms0>

if you have a tough time holding yourself down in a leg extension here are two very simple solutions for you the first solution is just to use regular lifting straps like you would on any heavy pulling motion simply just Loop the straps around the handles and secure them in so you don't have to expend a bunch of effort actively pulling yourself down to the seat the whole time solution number two is to get a seat belt you can buy one for cheap on Amazon or a lifting belt that you can wrap underneath of the seat to hold you down both of these methods work great just pick whichever one is more convenient and feels more stable and less effortful overall so that you can focus Less on having to hold yourself down to the seat and more just on training your quads
