# How not to use this glute machine

<https://www.youtube.com/shorts/ons20l15FsM>

I see a lot of people wrapping bands around their backs on the seated hip abduction machine to pull them forward, and I would not recommend doing this. If your goal is to train your glutes, let's talk about why. So, a lot of people think the glutes will increase in their activation with this because in a lot of cases when the trunk is pulled forward like this, like in an RDL or a deadlift, the glutes are loaded. But, the major difference here is that when the torso is pulled this way, the hips aren't actually loaded more. This is because the person is sitting down. There is no way for a person to load their hips in this position. So, instead, the muscles they use to resist this are just their spinal erectors. Now, even if you don't believe that, which you should, think about what the goal of this exercise actually is. When people are pushing their femur outward against something, this is going to train the outer glutes and the upper glutes. And even if this was loading the glutes in any substantial way, it would be loading the lower glute max proportionately the most. The lower glute max is not going to be able to pull the leg out anyway because it actually sits underneath of the femur in this case. So, from this position, it would pull the leg downward, not outward. So, instead, just ditch the band and do it the normal way.
