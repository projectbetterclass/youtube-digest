# HOW TO Bulgarian Split Squat

<https://www.youtube.com/shorts/yIJmMjnmdxs>

The Bulgarian split squat with a stick is by far the best way to train a single leg under a stable position. Most people do this wrong. This is how you do it right. So, the way most people do this wrong is they utilize the stick to change their center of mass. We fundamentally want to be performing the Bulgarian split squat with an addition of the stability of the stick, not to change its biomechanics. Step number one, place the ankle on the bench and the stick slightly outside the foot position to give you stability. Step two, simulate the squat with one leg and put your hamstrings deeply in those calves. You should feel a deep stretch through the back quadriceps.
