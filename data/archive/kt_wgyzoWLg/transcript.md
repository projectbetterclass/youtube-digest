# Summer Body Ready

<https://www.youtube.com/shorts/kt_wgyzoWLg>

Summer's 8 weeks away, and this is what you need to do to get your body summer ready in five simple steps. Step number one, understand energy balance. Step number two, get into the gym and start training a minimum of five days a week. Step number three, get your step count between 15 to 20,000 steps. I don't know that sounds aggressive, but it just depends on how far away you are from your goal. And then step four, drink enough water. And step five, eat your fruits and vegetables.
