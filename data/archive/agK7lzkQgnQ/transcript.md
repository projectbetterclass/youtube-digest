# The mentor trap

<https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=agK7lzkQgnQ>

Don't seek a mentor when you're not sure what you want to do with your life. When I was in my mid-teens, I had no idea what I wanted to do when I was going to grow up. And as a result, that's when I fell for the mentor trap. Instead of looking for a specific mentor to help me achieve a specific outcome that I was after, I didn't know what I wanted to do. So, what did I do? I went and I sought out the wealthiest person I knew. I believe this is a common mistake most people make. When they don't know what they want to do, and as a result, they try to find a mentor to help them have clarity, that mentor actually doesn't help them gain clarity. That mentor just really persuades them to do what they're doing. The first thing you must do is you must seek clarity. Seeking clarity, though, is really hard. This question requires a ton of trial and error. This question requires you to go out and try a ton of different jobs. But because seeking clarity is so difficult, it requires so much effort, this is why most people avoid it and then just try to find a mentor. There's a wonderful quote from Bruce Lee where he says, "When the student is ready, the master will appear." And what that's alluding to is that you have to know what you want first before you can find a meaningful teacher. Stop trying to find a mentor when you don't know what you want to do. Right now is the most important time when you have to spend the time on you looking within, not looking on the outside, but looking within. So, avoid the mentor trap. Don't just find the most successful person you know in your network. The first person that helps you gain clarity is you.
