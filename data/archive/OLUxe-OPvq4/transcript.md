# Why should you do single arm triceps extensions?

<https://www.youtube.com/shorts/OLUxe-OPvq4>

why would you do a single arm cable triceps extension when you could just do it with two hands if you allow your elbows to bend and straighten you'll notice that they come slightly outward to inward what this means is that if you're holding a rope it might be uncomfortable for you to keep your arms more narrow where you have to kind of try to move them more vertically up to down when you use a single cable and you angle it across your body like this you can really nicely match the path of your elbow and where the elbow wants to move with the direction of resistance in addition it's much easier to focus on one arm at a time especially if you feel like one of your arms is lagging behind the other single arm and double arm triceps motions are both great options to train the triceps so pick the one that you like more
