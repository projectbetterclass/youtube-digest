are a great exercise but this is a very common four mistake that i see with people doing their rdls of any variant when you're doing your rdls there's no need to finish the movement with more knee bend instead just focus on pushing your hips back the entire way down and once you reach that end range where you can no longer push the hips back you're done from there all you have to do is extend the hips and come right back up
