# Financial Advisor uses options

<https://www.youtube.com/shorts/aDSWRCDzEOA>

I had a conversation with a financial advisor that was managing just shy of $2 billion. This was back in college and he was a speaker and he was also an alumni telling a very select group of finance students how he successfully manages money as a financial advisor. Now, he said that he has been in business for 20 years and he manages some celebrity money. Talking to him was early in my option journey and I was super excited to ask him how he uses options to make his clients a lot of money. Well, when I finally asked him his secret, the answer surprised me. He said that he has consistently beat the market and got his clients steady returns for nearly two decades selling covered calls. And I was like, "That's it?" And he said, "Yep, that's what he focused on." And since that day, I made it my mission to learn how to sell covered calls properly and I've researched the strategy for a number of years now.
