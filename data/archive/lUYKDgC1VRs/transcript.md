# Mid exercise for the triceps and pecs

<https://www.youtube.com/shorts/lUYKDgC1VRs>

Watch this. Very strong center who believes in what he's firing. Is that exercise going to give you a sick pump? Yeah, given enough reps you are going to have a great tricep and pec pump. However, if you want an exercise that's going to be much more effective for your triceps, which is what I believe they were trying to train there, then instead just do a skull crusher. Or if you want you can keep that pullover type motion in because that is going to involve some triceps, and then you can do a JM press instead of a regular chest press. That would be much more specific to the triceps.
