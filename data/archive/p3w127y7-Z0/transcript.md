# Do this if you struggle with cables!

<https://www.youtube.com/shorts/p3w127y7-Z0>

If you or a client can't lift the lightest weight on a cable stack, here's a quick solution for you. Rather than grabbing the handle with your hand, simply loop your hand through the loop so that the strap comes on the forearm or even above the level of the elbow. What this will do is it will decrease the resistance on your shoulder so that you or your client who can't lift the lightest weight on the stack can actually do reps with the cable.
