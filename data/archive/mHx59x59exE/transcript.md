# Why would you do this?

<https://www.youtube.com/shorts/mHx59x59exE>

why would you ever put a dumbbell underneath of a hyperextension like this when the hyperextension is flat on the ground there's pretty much no resistance at the bottom where you're strong and all of the resistance is at the top where you're weak but if we put a dumbbell under here now there's much more resistance at the bottom and relatively less resistance at the top doing them with a slight angle like this upward can make a huge difference for people who don't have a ton of comfort with this motion and the higher that you tip the front of this the more that that will skew to where the weight is heavy at the bottom and lighter at the top
