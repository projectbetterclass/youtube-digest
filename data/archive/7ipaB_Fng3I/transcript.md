# JPGCOACHING x LARRY GAO PUSH DAY

<https://www.youtube.com/shorts/7ipaB_Fng3I>

Y'all, Larry is much bigger and taller than he looks in his videos. Let me just say that right now. When I pulled up to Barbell Brigade, I was very caught off guard. He should do bodybuilding or something. Very aesthetic. He got me to barbell bench for the first time in forever. I ended up doing some heavy triples with him. Then we hit some incline dumbbell bench in the 6 to 8 rep range, using some controlled eccentrics, of course. We finished up chest with some costal cable presses. We basically worked the chest from top to bottom and we were both feeling it the next day. Took Larry through a lot of new movements and he crushed it. Hit those presses in the 8 to 10 rep range, then we did some cable lateral raises. These were done in the 8 to 12 rep range, and that was all we did for shoulders. Excellent movement for working the middle delts. For triceps, all we did was this superset. We did cross body cable extensions in the 8 to 12 rep range, stopping a rep or two away from failure. Then we finished off the session with these overhead triceps extensions, and that was it. And that's Larry in the background trying to get a better pump than me for post-workout posing.
