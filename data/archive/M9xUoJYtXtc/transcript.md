# Exercise Tutorial: Single-Arm Lat Pull-Down

<https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=M9xUoJYtXtc>

a simple guide on how to perform a single arm lat pull down to train the lower portions of the lats step number one find a lat pull down and also find a single D handle to attach to the cable step number two align yourself on one side of the pad if you plan to use your right arm you want your right leg underneath of the left pad step number three find your active range of motion up into shoulder flexion and lean back a little bit to aim your arm directly at the origin of the cable step number four with your working leg anchor yourself in by doing a calf ra up into the pad in addition make sure that you use your opposite side hand to secure your upper body so that you don't rotate away from the working arm step number five to execute pull down narrow to the body and aim your elbow toward your hips make sure that the forearm stays parallel to the line of pull of the cable here are four common mistakes that people do with this exercise number one is restricting scapular motion and not letting the shoulder complex move the lats play a humongous role in moving the shoulder girdle don't restrict its motion because you're just going to decrease the stimulus to your lats mistake number two not actively shoving up into the thigh pad anchor remember that your lower body is just as much of a part of this motion as your upper body is if your lower body can't secure you then your upper body can't pull anything downward effectively mistake number three side crunching side crunching often gives assistance to actually completing the motion which means less stimulus to the muscles that you're trying to work finally mistake number four losing the lean and leaning forward as you go through the set make sure that you maintain your lean and to do this make sure you maintain your anchor specifically at the hand and not only just the foot
