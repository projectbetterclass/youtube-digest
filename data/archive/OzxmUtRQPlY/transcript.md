# Pre-Workout Ingredients Ranked (Add Vs. Skip)

<https://www.youtube.com/shorts/OzxmUtRQPlY>

Creatine, skip. Creatine has no immediate effects on performance. It works by building up in your muscles over time from daily use. Definitely a legit supplement that you can include in your pre-workout for convenience, but the timing doesn't actually matter. Beta-alanine, skip. Same thing as creatine in terms of saturation, plus it's mainly beneficial for longer efforts lasting around 1 to 4 minutes. For typical muscle building sets, it's not doing much other than making your skin crawl and butt hole tingle. Caffeine, add. It's one of the most research-backed supplements for improving strength and focus, especially on days where you're more fatigued. Just don't go overboard since overstimulation can actually make you perform worse and mess with your sleep later. Electrolytes, skip. You don't need a special electrolyte blend to do lat pull-downs and treadmill walking in an air-conditioned gym. Unless you're sweating heavily for long periods, your normal diet usually covers the small amounts you do lose, and most people already consume more sodium than they really need. L-tyrosine, add. This is an underrated ingredient that can help you maintain concentration and perform better under stress. I've been using tyrosine before workouts for over 10 years. It's probably my favorite besides caffeine. L-arginine, skip. It's marketed for bigger pumps, but the majority gets broken down before it even reaches your bloodstream. L-citrulline raises arginine levels more effectively than taking arginine itself. B vitamins, skip. They help your body convert food into energy, but taking extra before your workout won't give you a boost if you're already getting enough. The only noticeable change you might see is the color of your piss. L-theanine, add. It's technically a relaxation ingredient, but it pairs well with caffeine to smooth out the buzz. In pure form, we use a 1:1 ratio of theanine to caffeine so you feel more grounded and locked in rather than just wired and jittery.
