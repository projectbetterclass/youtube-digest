# Why doesn’t overhead press train middle delts?

<https://www.youtube.com/shorts/pMEiee6IkCI>

why is it that an overhead press doesn't train the middle delt but a lateral raise does okay I have two bananas here one represents the front delt one represents the middle delt when I'm doing a lateral raise and my arm is in a neutral degree of rotation and I move out to the side you can see that this second banana the middle delt banana is actually going to be the thing that has leverage to actually be on top of this joint to resist the load direction if my arm is in this externally rotated position you can see that the front out is now on the top side of the joint and the middle Del is actually wanting to pull more backward than it is upward another easy way to visualize this is to have a band that represents the middle delt and as I move my arm up in this more neutral position you can see that it's in a really good position to move the load but the second that I rotate my arm backward you see what happens to the band it actually moves away from being the thing that's on the top side of the joint which again in this case is the front delt which pulls the arm up in an overhead press
