Most people think they need way more volume to build muscle than they actually do because their program isn't properly set up, they don't train hard enough, and they don't have a proper progression scheme in place, but if everything is dialed in, it absolutely does work. It just comes down to proper execution. And two truly highquality workouts a week will beat four to five subpar ones every time. This doesn't mean it's going to maximize your gains 100%, but it won't be too far off. And not everyone is trying to build as much muscle as possible anyway. So depending on your current goal, schedule, and priorities, 2 days a week is definitely a viable option.
