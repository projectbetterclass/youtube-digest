To overcome this challenge, Joby partnered with Dassault Systemes, to develop an immutable filetype similar to the solutions used in the financial technology space. This filetype also adds some advantages for the 3D printing laser technologies that Joby uses by providing extra controls and rules on how the laser moves while making parts. Joby's goal with this collaboration is to allow 3D-printed parts to be more auditable in the present and to have a filetype that supports new requirements for certification in the future.
