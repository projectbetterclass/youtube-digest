# 5 Foods To AVOID For FAT LOSS

<https://www.youtube.com/shorts/8Y3CAt1zb1s>

These are five foods I would avoid if I was trying to lose fat. First thing I avoid is scotch fillet steaks. Super delicious, but the problem is they're pretty much 50% fat. A better fix would be rump steak with all the visible fat trimmed off, or better yet, eye fillet. A little bit more pricey, but no fat in it. The next one is salmon with the skin on. Looks super healthy, and it is. Like, it's high in omega-3 fatty acids and all the rest. The problem is just high in fat. An easy switch is barramundi, tuna steaks. The next thing I would never do is full-fat yogurt. Just because you get almost double the calories with the same protein equivalency. I just pick low-fat yogurt as a switch. It tastes basically the same. You can't tell the difference. The next thing I avoid is white potatoes. Only because there's a lower calorie option in spud lite potatoes. You can have a lot more volume for a lot less calories. Remember the goal is you want to eat a lot of food, just not a lot of calories. Another thing I would avoid when dieting is also nuts. Only cuz they're pretty much all fat and they're super high in calories. The other thing I would avoid is fruit juices. This whole thing is about 750 calories. The easy switch is just something like Coke Zero or soda water.
