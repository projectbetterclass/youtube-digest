# Is this the BEST forearm exercise?

<https://www.youtube.com/shorts/d9VvUM9UuZE>

So, I don't know how anyone comes up with these ranking systems, but all these different forearm exercises are basically the same because all of them have one thing in common, which is that they take the hand and they pull it this way, which requires muscles on this side of the joint, the front side of the forearm, to curl against the load. Dumbbell wrist curls, great. Barbell wrist curls, great. cable wrist curls. Great. Just find the wrist curl that is most comfortable for you. Just rinse and repeat that wrist curl every single week. Personally speaking, I'm not saying this is the best exercise. I prefer to attach two D handles to a cable set low so that my palms can have the freedom to rotate as I do the curls in whatever direction. But since there's no such thing as a 10 out of 10 exercise, you might like one of the other options a little bit more, and that's totally fine.
