# Why is more range of motion not better?

<https://www.youtube.com/shorts/EWvZo1V-HSs>

why is using more range of motion on a leg press not always better than using less range of motion whenever we're doing a leg press our goal is to train the muscles around the knee and around the hip but a lot of times what happens is people get to a certain point of hip and knee motion and then their pelvis tucks underneath of them and their lumbar spine starts to round off of the pad so technically speaking their legs are moving farther back as well as the foot plate but most of that motion actually just comes from the whole pelvis tipping backward instead what we want to do is just move through the knee and the hip even if this means getting less range we'll be training just those muscles as opposed to all the muscles that surround the lower back so rather than just thinking that deeper is better in moving your lower back just go as deep as your knee and your hip allow for
