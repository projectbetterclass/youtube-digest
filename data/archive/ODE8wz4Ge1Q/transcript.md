# Here's how to gain clarity in your life

<https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=ODE8wz4Ge1Q>

The reason we don't have clarity is because we don't think about the thoughts that are running through our head. We don't capture the thoughts that are running through our head. And as a result of going a week, 2 weeks, a month, a year, 2 years, 4 years, 5 years, 10 years, 20 years, the longer you leave those thoughts in your head and you don't capture them, the more confused you'll become because your brain just becomes a series of thoughts and it's like an email inbox. Let's use that as an example. You might have 365 unread emails. And then if you leave it for 2 years, you have 700 unread emails. And it becomes overwhelming, not lost, you're just overwhelmed. So, what I do is I journal and every time I journal, I'm writing down the thoughts. I'm marking the emails as unread. And the more emails you start to read, the more thoughts you write down and then you think about those thoughts and reflect on why you're having those thoughts, clarity comes from that process. Otherwise, you get overwhelmed. You're thinking about, "Oh, my work life, what am I going to do there? Oh, my fitness and health life, what am I going to do there? My relationship life with my partner and my family and my friends, it's all complicated there. And then oh my goodness, I've got all these tasks I have to do for the house and and it all becomes too much. It all becomes so noisy that we start to feel that crisis. So, for me, where do I find my clarity? I find my clarity in analysis, in review, and in reflection.
