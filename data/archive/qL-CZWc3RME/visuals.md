## 6. [10:39] Text slide listing three key conditions of the pre-crisis housing market.

## 7. [11:34] Informational text slide defining Interest-Only ARM mortgage structure and terms.

INTEREST-ONLY ARM; The borrower is only required to pay the interest owed each month, for a certain period of time. During the interest-only period, the borrower is not required to pay down any principal owed. The length of the interest-only period can last anywhere from a few months to many years.

## 9. [13:45] Text slide defining the worst mortgage product type offered during the bubble.

THE WORST OF THE WORST; The interest-only, negatively-amortising adjustable-rate subprime mortgage. (Borrowers of subprime quality could get a loan where they did not have to pay their monthly repayment)

## 10. [14:20] Text slide with quote explaining the dependency of subprime mortgages on continuous home price appreciation.
