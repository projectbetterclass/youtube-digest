# HOW TO Face Pull

<https://www.youtube.com/shorts/4w7eBipjlSc>

The face pull is by far the best way to train the rear delts. Most people do this wrong. This is how you do this right. What most people do is they start pulling the weight before they set their shoulders up correctly. The better way is to initiate by pulling your shoulders back as far as they can go and then from there simply taking the rope and pulling it towards your face. Hot tip, make sure the the pulley is at chin height. Second hot tip, when you grab the handles, I like to put my thumbs above, not below. When you put them below, it feels like you're squashing your thumbs. Simply just take your thumbs and place it above the plastic balls.
