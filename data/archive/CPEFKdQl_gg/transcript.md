# Using straps - for leg extensions?!

<https://www.youtube.com/shorts/CPEFKdQl_gg>

most people use wrist wraps to put on handles to do rows but have you ever used them during leg extensions whenever you're doing a leg extension the force that you exert against the pad wants to essentially pull you up and out of the seat and sometimes when the loads get really heavy it becomes harder and harder to hold yourself down with just your hands and your shoulders so instead what you can do is simply wrap the wrist wrap around the handle so that you tie yourself into the seat and don't really have to put much effort into actively stabilizing the motion as a consequence of this you can just focus on output into the pad
