# Chest supported rows uncomfy?

<https://www.youtube.com/shorts/ovcso9L2hGA>

as a coach who has worked with hundreds of women a common complaint that I get from my clients is that their chest typically doesn't feel very comfortable when they're doing their chest supported rowing here's a simple solution that you can Implement at pretty much any commercial gym place an AB pad right under your chest or even up to two back to back if you need a little bit more clearance if you don't have an app pad to use then you can also try rolling up a yoga mat and using that instead as you can see I have a little bit more clearance now and there's less pressure on my chest this small change has helped out a lot of the women that I trained so give it a go on your next back day and let me know how it goes
