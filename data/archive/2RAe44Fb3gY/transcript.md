# The worst glute exercise?

<https://www.youtube.com/shorts/2RAe44Fb3gY>

Sitting down and doing a hinging motion like this is not going to be great for the glutes. And the reason is because you're sitting down. And if you're sitting down, the load that the glutes would actually need to resist is just being supported by the bench. The solution is simply instead of just a sit, to stand so that your glutes actually have to hold up your entire spine and the weight that you're lifting. Because a vast majority of this motion is just being controlled by the spine and the spinal extensors.
