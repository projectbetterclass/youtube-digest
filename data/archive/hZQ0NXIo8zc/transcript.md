# Exercise Tutorial: Middle Pec Press-Flye

here's a simple guide on how to set up a cable press around motion for the middle portion of the PEC step number one set the cable around elbow height step number two align your same side foot with the bottom of the cable stack and grab the opposite side cable column with your opposite hand step number three ensure that the cable is perfectly parallel to the direction of the forearm the cable should ultimately just brush against the hairs of your forearm gently step number four press forward and around toward the middle of the sternum step number five maintain the cable angle with the forearm and maintain both of your anchors as you go through this pressing motion here are four common mistakes that people make when trying to execute this motion mistake number one inap appropriate anchoring often times people set up with their feet backwards so that their same side foot is forward or they don't even grab the opposite side of the cable stack to begin with this will completely ruin your ability to actually create any degree of output through the pecs mistake number two the cable wrapping around someone's body or moving too far away from the body make sure that the cable is gently brushing along the hairs of your forearm and isn't running into and wrapping around your body and or moving away from your body mistake number three not allowing the scaps to move if you don't allow your shoulder blades to move you're not going to be able to actually get to the fully specific and shortened position of the middle PEC and mistake number four only doing a press or only doing a fly presses and flies are great but they're not specific to this goal so rather than just doing a press or just doing a fly which may each have their own merits make sure that you're doing this motion as a combination of a press really into a fly toward the end of the motion
