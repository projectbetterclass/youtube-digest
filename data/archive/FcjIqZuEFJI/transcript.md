# How i fixed my elbow pain on preacher curls!

<https://www.youtube.com/shorts/FcjIqZuEFJI>

If you have elbow pain on preacher curls, try this. Instead of grabbing the handle fully, open the palm of your hand and place the handle closer to the bottom side of your palm. This will allow you to actually adjust your grip more, not only in its entirety, but throughout the entire range of motion whenever your natural forearm motion needs to take place. This obviously won't work for everyone, but it's worked for me and a tremendous amount of people who have had elbow pain on this exercise. So, give it a try if you struggle with elbow issues on this machine and let me know how it goes.
