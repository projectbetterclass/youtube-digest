# Best Lateral Raise Variations For Bigger Side Delts

<https://www.youtube.com/shorts/6-GJAPiUgHk>

Machine lateral raise, S tier. It's the most stable variation. It's also the easiest to set up. You just sit down, grab the handles, and go. My personal favorite. Lying cable lateral raise, S tier. If you don't mind the extra setup, this is a really good cable option that keeps everything locked in, and you can also use cuffs if you prefer it. Single arm cable lateral raise, S tier. You can easily adjust the cable to modify where it's hardest. Waist height makes it heavier at the bottom. Floor height makes it harder toward the middle. Solid choice. Dumbbell lateral raise, S tier. It can be a bit easier for your body to sway, but that's still fine as long as you're keeping your form mostly strict, and you can lean against an incline bench if you want something to brace against. Behind the back cable lateral raise, S tier. Same deal as the one-arm variation, except this is just more efficient since you can hit both sides at the same time. Side-lying dumbbell lateral raise, S tier. Easy way to take a regular dumbbell lateral raise, which is heaviest at the top, and flip the resistance profile around so it's heavier toward the bottom. Cable Y raise, S tier. I wouldn't do this as your only variation, but if you want to hit the side delts in their shortened position, then this is a great option for that.
