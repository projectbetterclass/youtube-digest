# Stop disrespecting your isolation exercises

Is your lateral raise stronger than it was 2 months ago? [music] If you really have to think about it, that's a problem. See, most lifters track their compound lifts, then throw isolations in at the end just chasing the pump. But, isolation lifts can sometimes give you a clearer read on whether you're actually building muscle. That's because on compound movements, technique, coordination, and body weight can all affect your performance alongside muscle growth, whereas isolation strip a lot of that away. Lateral raises, leg curls, and push-downs are less skill dependent, so if your strength on them keeps climbing over time, that's a very strong sign the targeted muscle is growing. This can be especially useful during a cut because as your body weight and calories come down, some compound lifts can dip even if you haven't lost any actual muscle. I see this with TrainWithUs members pretty often. For example, someone might lose 10 lb of body fat and have their hack squat drop slightly, but still keep improving on their leg extensions, meaning their quads are probably still maintaining their size or even still growing. That's why we log every isolation lift in the app and track it over time the same way we track compounds, so you always know [music] your training is actually moving forward. Now, none of this is meant to downplay compound lifts. Once your technique is dialed in, progressing on them will still be highly correlated with growth, but isolation should get the same attention.
