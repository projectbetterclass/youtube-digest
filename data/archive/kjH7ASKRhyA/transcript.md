# So many bodybuilders get this wrong...

<https://www.youtube.com/shorts/kjH7ASKRhyA>

Anybodybuilders today still seem to be operating on the notion that their purpose is to go into the gym to see how many sets they can do or discover how long they can endure. And this is erroneous because a bodybuilding workout is not [music] an endurance contest. Your purpose is not to go into the gym to see how tough you are, how much work you can perform, or how long [music] you can endure. Your actual literal purpose as a bodybuilder is to go into the gym and intelligently do what nature requires, [music] to trigger the growth mechanism into motion, and get the hell out, go home, rest, and grow.
