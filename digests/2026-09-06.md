**Key points**
- The key lat activation technique is initiating the pull-down with scapular depression (pulling shoulder blades down) before moving the hands/elbows.

**Takeaway:** Initiating pull-down exercises by depressing the shoulder blades first (rather than leading with the arms) ensures the lats do the work instead of other back muscles.
