## [The UNTHINKABLE is About to Happen to Stocks](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=ukjHHCtLq2g)

**Tom Nash** · 🟡 Skim
> Useful data-driven framing on current valuation and market cycle positioning, but the core message (dollar-cost average, buy dips, hold long) is standard and the "unthinkable" premise is overblown marketing.

**Key points**
- S&P 500 forward P/E is 20, matching the 10-year average; current P/E of 25 is lower than last year—market is fairly to cheaply priced, not expensive
- Current bull run is 100% over 4 years, below the 265% average and 5.5-year average length; AI rally is only 150% vs. 600% for the internet era—not yet at euphoria
- Earnings are strong: 87% of companies beat EPS (11% above average), earnings grew 52% vs. 23% estimate, and 10 of 11 sectors grew earnings—not concentrated in mega-caps
- Market corrections are normal: 10% drops in 2023–24 match the 10% average per year; IPO activity at ~140 annualized is 3× lower than 1999's 460—no euphoria signals
- Strategy: invest consistently (DCA), deploy half budget monthly plus reserves, double down on 20%+ dips, trim 10–30% after 50–150% unrealized gains; focus on 10–20 year horizon where historical returns are nearly 100% positive

**Takeaway:** Despite recent market gains and fears of a crash, the speaker argues that fundamental data—valuation, earnings breadth, bull run length, and corrections—all show the market is fairly priced and acting normally, not approaching euphoria; the strategy is to invest consistently over decades, double down on dips, and trim winners.

**🔍 Reality check:** The video opens with alarmist framing ("unthinkable," "title wave") but then argues for a moderate, optimistic view—classic bait-and-switch. While the valuation metrics presented are sourced from real data, the interpretation is selective: favoring metrics that support a bullish case (forward P/E, sector breadth) while geopolitical and recession risks are dismissed without detail. The prediction that "every 20 years is 100% positive" conflates historical trend with future guarantee, and ignores sequence-of-returns risk and inflation. The speaker is actively selling academy memberships and a stock-research tool (stock-mvp.com), creating incentive to maintain a pro-bull narrative. The strategy itself (DCA, trim winners, add dips) is sensible but presented as novel; the 10–20 year horizon assumption doesn't help short-term volatility. This is not financial advice; market timing and sector rotation carry real risk regardless of historical averages."
