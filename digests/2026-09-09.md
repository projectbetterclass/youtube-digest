## [If It Scales, It's Free. If It Doesn't, You Pay.](https://www.youtube.com/shorts/OA_6vQHI9Yc)

**Alex Hormozi** · 🟡 Skim
> Clear, actionable principle captured in the key points; the 60-second format leaves little room for depth or nuance beyond the core idea.

**Key points**
- Core principle: offer for free anything that scales (books, courses, events); charge for non-scalable services (personalized advising, 1-on-1 work)
- Hormozi gives away all his digital products (books, courses) at no cost while monetizing through high-touch advisory services
- Frames this as a 'limits test' to determine what provides enough value to justify a price tag versus what should be freely distributed
- Scalable content builds audience and trust; non-scalable services create premium revenue streams

**Takeaway:** Give away everything that scales easily (digital content, mass events); charge only for expensive-to-deliver, non-scalable services like 1-on-1 advising or personalized support.

**🔍 Reality check:** This strategy works best for creators who already have significant assets, audience, or capital to absorb the cost of free content and can afford to rely on high-ticket services or other monetization. It assumes you *can* create premium, non-scalable services worth a substantial price—not every creator has that option. Also assumes free digital products will reliably lead to paid high-touch opportunities; conversion isn't guaranteed. Hormozi's specific business model (portfolio companies, advisory, events) is not representative of all industries or business types."
