## [DeepSeek’s New AI System Shouldn’t Be Possible](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=L9mMfAFwbl4)

**Two Minute Papers** · ✅ Watch in full
> High-signal overview of a genuinely novel AI infrastructure concept with clear technical explanation of how self-modification is made safe; the idea of adaptive operating systems around AI has significant implications.

**Key points**
- DeepSeek Harness is an open-source framework that allows AI systems to modify and extend their own functionality on the fly, without manual reprogramming
- Key innovation: the system includes automatic cleanup and undo instructions with every change, making modifications reversible and preventing system collapse
- Every major component is customizable and can be rewritten—UI, agents, workflows—including custom modes like code review or research verification that the AI creates on demand
- The framework is remarkably efficient and lean, designed to run locally with no tracking or token-limit restrictions
- Hundreds of community plugins already available days after release, enabling diverse use cases from storyboarding to local AI lab monitoring

**Takeaway:** DeepSeek Harness is a self-modifying AI framework where the system dynamically rewrites its own components and interfaces based on user requests, with built-in safeguards ensuring stability and reversibility.
